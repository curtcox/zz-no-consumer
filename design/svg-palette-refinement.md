# SVG palette material and contrast review

## Follow-up material pass

After the barrier audit expanded the palette, a second review refined 17 defaults.
Permission passes now use clipped paper stock and punched scope windows, separating
them from recessed alloy budget meters. Network walls gain granular surfaces;
privilege frames have layered metal edges; review checkpoints pair fibrous dockets
with brushed hardware; irreversible controls retain their unpressed state beneath a
brushed cover. The threshold is a raised rail above a separate measured bar, and the
objective pennant gains attachment stitching and cloth folds.

Background margins carry more of their purpose: an archival sleeve has folded corners
and a paper custody band, source reservoirs have storage and transcript margins,
calendar lanes have distinct steel and paper headers, and review trays have recessed
edges. Centers remain translucent and available for scene evidence. Texture marks
remain non-quantitative. Colors inherit scene ink alongside the existing paper and
shadow neutrals; no new success, risk, or uncertainty state is introduced.

The pass was compared against prior versions at equal ink and size and checked in
lettered examples of scope, thresholds, evidence, review, timelines and preservation.
All preceding versions remain available in the workshop.

## Initial material pass

The September 2026 review adds material, silhouette and state distinctions to 78

## Visual vocabulary

- Records use warm stock, grain, folded corners, binding gutters and irregular
  paragraph lines. A task is a clipped docket with a target; a folder has a layered
  open sleeve; a transcript has a sequence gutter; a directory entry sits in a dark
  storage frame. These distinctions survive removal of their labels.
- Hardware uses beveled planes, shaded glass, ventilation mesh and separate
  controls. An aperture has mechanical shutter blades. Blank monitors remain blank;
  rack details do not invent activity indicators.
- Access and budget symbols use bright occupied cells against empty cells. Gates
  distinguish closed mesh from open, foreshortened leaves. Task clocks have a dark
  squared housing; external clocks have a light circular dial and minute ticks.
- People use restrained garment planes and gesture contours without adding facial
  identity. Desks, chairs and rooms gain wood grain, upholstery, floor and ceiling
  values. Background shading remains translucent behind actors and evidence.
- Routes keep solid shafts and triangular endpoints because reflective detail
  breaks their continuity at narrow panel sizes. Read/write routes use opposing
  lanes. A broken trace retains a conspicuous gap. Boundaries and reconstruction
  frames retain different continuous, hatched and broken treatments.

Reusable accents inherit the scene's ink through `currentColor`. Material neutrals
add depth without assigning new meanings to moss, claret or amber; the future
register remains desaturated. Empty slots, missing cells, redaction tape and
unknown interiors stay visibly empty, missing or obscured. Texture conveys a
material or drawing convention, never additional evidence.

## Review and maintenance

Review each item against its earlier version at thumbnail size, on paper and dark
surfaces, then in representative composed storyboards. The initial review covered
all revised items and twelve panel comparisons spanning hardware, human rooms,
scope boundaries, source records, clocks, and the future sequence. It led to
brighter state cells, stronger packet markers, aligned room planes and solid routes.

Paint definitions are local to each SVG and contain only the gradients or patterns
that version uses. Workshop thumbnails supply a consistent steel ink so they agree
with the main preview. Ant anatomy readers ignore paint definitions; a checker
fixture prevents a texture path from becoming an extra limb or QR silhouette.

Use the [component workflow](svg-components.md) to save further versions, select
defaults, regenerate affected storyboards and rebuild the published gallery.
