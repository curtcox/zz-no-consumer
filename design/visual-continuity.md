## Canonical environment geometry

### Evaluation facility `ENV-EVAL-A`

- Concrete slab, paired rack aisles, overhead black cable trays.
- Matte dark rack endcaps with small neutral number plates.
- Service-cart silhouette deep camera-right.
- Primary camera axis looks down the cold aisle with the selected workload left of center.
- Pages 001–003, 006–012, and 030–037 reuse this geometry unless the script explicitly changes scale.

### Shared cache `ENV-CACHE-A`

- Physical context: service rack connected to multiple workload lanes.
- Interface context: reverse-sorted directory rows, narrow timestamp column, large blank area for controlled lettering.
- Moss belongs only to rows proven visible or propagated; unrelated cache activity stays steel/gray.
- After the wipe, preserve geometry and remove all moss so the absence reads as state change.

### Security operations center `ENV-SOC-A`

- Two shallow workstation rows; modest wall displays; no world map.
- Signal, correlation, severity, and paging owner occupy distinct UI regions.
- Reuse the same incident card across pages 055–056 so classification—not detection—changes.

### Laboratory room `ENV-LAB-A`

- Long neutral table, acoustic wall panels, partially erased whiteboard, flat display, water glasses.
- No head-of-table hero composition. Authority moves through documents, screens, and actions.

### Investigation room `ENV-INV-A`

- Same table and camera axis across pages 075–083.
- Evidence density increases from screens to printed timelines to AI summaries and conflicting annotations.
- Empty wall space shrinks while confidence does not increase automatically.

### Composite forum `ENV-FORUM-A`

- Plain wood or laminate, microphones, binders, neutral walls, generic screens.
- No flags, seals, judicial bench, legislature-specific architecture, or recognizable real venue.

### Curt's office `ENV-CURT-A`

- Desk against dark wall; two external monitors; laptop centered below.
- Desk lamp camera-left; drink camera-right; printer/paper stack behind chair; doorway deep background.
- Default camera families: over-shoulder from doorway, side profile from lamp, hands above keyboard, monitor reflection.
- Maintain room layout through states A–E; only light level, drink, paper, tabs, and correction density evolve.
