# Story location and background audit

Reviewed 12 September 2026 against the canonical scripts' location declarations,
panel frames, page plan and story contract. The gap was usually a distinct setting
drawn as the same generic room, folder or terminal, rather than a missing story event.
The new components give those settings separate reusable geometry. They are available
in the component workshop under **Locations**.

## Boundaries retained

Physical rooms are reconstructed or invented staging. The system enclosures and
dossier fields are abstract diagrams, not architectural claims. No new setting
adds a person, institutional logo, address, operational route, credential value,
source quotation or incident event. Captions and source qualifications remain owned
by the canonical scripts.

The script's references to public records, regulatory demands and research programs
do not justify inventing offices for their authors. Critics and model responses stay
text on screen. Unidentified future scenes retain their blank identity and lineage.
Black fields, absence-of-evidence panels, unconnected hypotheses and the final cursor
are deliberate backgrounds and remain intact.

Only establishing views and setting-bearing compositions gain these components;
close-ups do not automatically receive a room. Semantic boundaries keep their
existing labels and paths. The wiki and Artifactory retain separate geometry, and
no background connects their populations. Stored artwork versions remain available.

## Review

Scene intents describe the new visual role; source snapshots and canonical narrative
are unchanged. Review included the location palette and lettered examples of physical
rooms, empty paging chairs, evidence scale, cache containment, platform ownership,
worker-local scope, wiki history and the unidentified future interface. Structural
checks cover geometry, lettering, component integrity and generated outputs; visual
clarity remains an editorial judgment.
