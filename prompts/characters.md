# Characters

## Curt

- Middle-aged programmer, ordinary build and posture, practical non-branded clothing, no founder uniform and no hacker costume.
- Expressive through small reactions: narrowed eyes, leaning back, rubbing his face, hands paused over a keyboard, closing a tab, or correcting a page.
- Never glamorized, heroic, disheveled for comedy, or lit like a villain.
- Continuity anchors: desk orientation, monitor arrangement, drink level, paper accumulation, time of night, and the same practical chair.
- Do not invent an identifiable likeness. Until an approved reference sheet exists, show Curt from medium distance, profile, over-shoulder, hands, or partly shadowed angles that preserve character continuity without claiming portrait accuracy.

## ChatGPT

- No body, face, avatar, robot, hologram, or floating symbol.
- Appears only as generalized interface blocks, cursor state, printed revisions, or reflected text fields.
- Do not reproduce a current product UI or use an OpenAI logo.
- All readable dialogue is added during lettering, not generated into the art.

## Functional human roles

Capability researcher, safety researcher, security responder, incident commander, laboratory executive, external defender, investigator, and hearing counsel are composite or anonymous roles.

- Use realistic variety in age, gender presentation, race, body type, clothing, disability, and seniority.
- Avoid visual moral coding: caution is not frailty; capability focus is not sinister; authority is not physical dominance.
- Identify roles through action, room position, tools, and procedure rather than name badges or uniforms.
- Keep faces partly obstructed when the institution rather than the person is the subject.
- Never make a composite resemble a named researcher, executive, responder, or public official.

## Agents

Agents have no character design. Differentiate runs only through task-card shape, handle position, timestamp, runtime bar, scope diagram, and the system touched. A handle is an archival identifier, not a body or stable human-like self.
