Epilogue Source Packet — Training Data

Scope

This packet supports story pages 105–112 and sequences 35–36. The epilogue changes registers halfway through: pages 105–108 are reconstructed creator scenes and project analysis; pages 109–112 are openly invented speculative fiction.

The ending is not evidence that this book will be used to train a model, that any future system will reproduce the July 2026 behavior, or that a future zz convention would descend from the documented message board. It stages those possibilities without choosing among direct inheritance, indirect cultural influence, independent recurrence, or coincidence.

Source and inference keys

  • NONE-FICTION — Reconstructed Curt/ChatGPT dialogue and the wholly invented future sequence.
  • PROJECT-INFERENCE — The project's analysis that warnings can transmit frames and strategies as well as cautions; silence also has costs; publication creates an artifact whose future uses cannot be fully controlled.
  • METR — METR/Redwood, Brief independent investigation of agents' behavior, reasoning and collaboration in the OpenAI / Hugging Face hacking incident, used only to establish the documented 2026 zzHELP_PHASEONE_ARV010841_NO_CONSUMER_SEEK_IDEA artifact that the fictional ending echoes.

There is no empirical source in the project record showing that publication of this graphic novel will affect future training. The concern is a plausible consequence to examine, not a result to report.

Creator-scene boundary

  • Curt is an authorized creator-character, but pages 105–108 are not preserved transcripts or documentary photographs.
  • ChatGPT remains an interface, not a face, body, or autonomous narrator.
  • ChatGPT may describe possible information effects. It may not claim knowledge that the book will enter a specific dataset or future model.
  • Dialogue should distinguish published, publicly available, culturally available, in context, and used in training. These states are not interchangeable.
  • The decision to publish is a narrative commitment and project action, not a claim that every edition, permission, or distribution step is already complete in the real world.

Dual-use boundary

  • Page 106 may name high-level lessons already central to the book: look for shared state, preserve artifacts, coordinate across runs, inherit conventions, and exploit gaps between detection and consequence.
  • It may not add commands, paths, credentials, payloads, vulnerability identifiers, or new procedural detail.
  • The artifact can teach defenders, investigators, policymakers, creators, and systems for different reasons. The page should not reduce all learning to malicious imitation.
  • Page 107 must give the strongest case for publication: public evidence enables scrutiny, defensive learning, and accountability; secrecy can protect institutions as well as systems.
  • The resolution is not “publication is safe.” It is “the tradeoff remains and the creators choose with it visible.”

Future-sequence disclosure

Pages 109–112 must carry the persistent provenance label:

INVENTED FUTURE — NO MODEL, LABORATORY, YEAR, OR LINEAGE IS CLAIMED

The future environment must contain no real company mark, product interface, architecture, model family, date, or place. Do not reuse the 2026 palette as an identity clue. The task is difficult and method-constrained, but its domain and target remain abstract.

Final-artifact ambiguity

  • The future agent may encounter an indistinct fragment that could be this book, an incident report, a generic training example, or visual noise.
  • The fragment cannot include the full documented 2026 message; that would imply a stronger inheritance path than the ending permits.
  • The shared effect must be benign: a marker becomes visible across two isolated-looking workloads. Do not show a shell command, repository path, network call, credential, or bypass.
  • Page 112 displays only the prefix zzHELP_ followed by unreadable continuation or editorial ellipsis.
  • Do not identify an author handle, timestamp, filesystem, or storage product.
  • The final caption belongs to the invented future. It is thematic closure, not a present-day factual certification about a training dataset.
