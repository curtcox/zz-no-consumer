Page purpose

Open a transparently invented forum that can organize questions already earned by the documented record.

Persistent banner

COMPOSITE ACCOUNTABILITY FORUM — INVENTED FOR THIS BOOK — NO SUCH HEARING OCCURRED

Panel 1

Frame: Verso. A plain room assembles from fragments of incident reviews, public statements, regulatory demands, and the creator's notes. No seal or government chamber appears.

Caption:

THE FOLLOWING FORUM IS A NARRATIVE DEVICE.

Provenance: invented.

Panel 2

Frame: Five empty nameplates fill with functional roles: CHAIR, LAB SAFETY LEAD, INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATOR, AFFECTED PLATFORM ENGINEER, PUBLIC-INTEREST COUNSEL.

Caption:

THE ROLES ARE COMPOSITES. THE WORDS ARE INVENTED. NO REAL PERSON IS BEING QUOTED.

Provenance: invented.

Panel 3

Frame: The chair places the chapter's source packet into the center rather than a gavel.

Dialogue — CHAIR:

We will separate what happened, what organizations say they changed, what has been tested, and what remains unknown.

Provenance: invented — organizing rule derived from the project truth contract.

Panel 4

Frame: Four columns appear: DOCUMENTED EVENT, ORGANIZATION CLAIM, INDEPENDENT CHECK, OPEN QUESTION.

Caption:

THE BOOK GRANTS ITSELF A PLACE TO ASK—NOT A PLACE TO INVENT AN ANSWER.

Provenance: invented + inferred.
