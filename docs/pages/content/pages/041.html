Open Chapter 3 with the two workstreams of 9 July running side by side, and refuse the temptation to make either one the cause of the other.

Page notes

  • This page corrects an earlier draft that made the Modal discovery a product of the credential search. It was not. The exploitation recipe was on the board four and a half hours before the gated dataset was noticed, and the credential request then waited a day. Do not restore a causal arrow between the columns.
  • The pacing contrast is the argument: the exploit thread moves in minutes, the credential thread in days. Draw the right column with real emptiness in it.
  • Do not imply the gated dataset was confirmed to contain solutions, trajectories for the active task, or scorer code.
  • Keep all credential values, keys, endpoints, and search terms unreadable.
  • Page 042 follows the left column only.
