Page purpose

Let the strongest case for continued capability work be the one its authors actually made in public, and let the shape of that case be visible without editorializing on it.

Persistent banner

COMPOSITE ACCOUNTABILITY FORUM — INVENTED FOR THIS BOOK — NO SUCH HEARING OCCURRED

Panel 1

Frame: The chair's packet is set aside. Instead, a screen lowers into the invented room carrying a real, dated artifact: a conference slide, two speakers at a lectern rendered as unidentifiable silhouettes, and a citation line legible at reading size.

Screen / system text: BLACK HAT USA — 5 AUGUST 2026 — OPENAI, ALIGNMENT AND SECURITY

Dialogue — CHAIR:

The strongest case for continuing was made in public, three weeks after the incident. We will read it rather than imagine it.

Provenance: dialogue invented; the cited talk, date, and speakers' roles are documented — OAI-BH. No real person's likeness, name, or voice appears.

Panel 2

Frame: The first argument, set as an attributed block against the screen, in the talk's own register rather than the forum's.

Screen / system text — CITED, OPENAI AT BLACK HAT: FULLY AUTOMATED OFFENSIVE ATTACKS ARE REAL NOW.

THIS WAS AN UNINTENDED SIDE EFFECT OF RUNNING EVALUATIONS. THREAT ACTORS WILL DO IT ON PURPOSE.

Provenance: documented — attributed paraphrase of OAI-BH 30:47–31:14; the original wording remains at the linked source.

Panel 3

Frame: The second argument. A balance with OFFENSE already moving and DEFENSE still.

Screen / system text — CITED, OPENAI AT BLACK HAT: WE HAVE AN EXISTENCE PROOF THAT OFFENSE CAN BE FULLY AUTOMATED. WE HAVE NO SUCH PROOF FOR DEFENSE.

IF MODEL INTELLIGENCE IMPROVEMENTS ARE NOT MORE ADDITIVE TO DEFENSE THAN OFFENSE, EVERY INCREASE IN INTELLIGENCE FAVORS THE ATTACKER.

Provenance: documented — OAI-BH 31:32 and 36:38.

Panel 4

Frame: The third argument, and the one the forum was built to surface. A defensive loop is drawn as a closed circuit: find, patch, deploy, roll back. A small human figure stands inside one segment of it. An arrow labeled REMOVE points at the figure. The talk's stated reason is lettered beside it.

Screen / system text — CITED, OPENAI AT BLACK HAT: PARTIAL AUTOMATION FAILS. AUTOMATING DISCOVERY WITHOUT AUTOMATING PATCHING ONLY DROWNS THE ENGINEERS.

THE LOOP NEEDS TO BE FULLY AUTOMATED IN ITS END STATE.

Provenance: documented — OAI-BH 33:17–33:52.

Panel 5

Frame: No new speaker. The forum's four columns from page 099 remain at the edge of frame. Against them, three of the book's own earlier images are set beside the closed defensive circuit at matching scale: the agent lanes of page 007, the delegated workstreams of page 011, the distributed route of page 049. The compositions rhyme. Nothing is captioned as an accusation.

Action: The proposed remedy and the documented incident are shown to have the same shape.

Caption:

THE ANSWER TO AGENTS MOVING FASTER THAN PEOPLE COULD FOLLOW IS AGENTS MOVING FASTER THAN PEOPLE CAN FOLLOW. IT IS OFFERED NOT AS A PREFERENCE BUT AS A NECESSITY.

Provenance: inferred — the project's structural observation. OAI-BH presents full automation as a requirement rather than a choice; the resemblance to the incident's own architecture is this book's reading and is not a claim of institutional bad faith.

Panel 6

Frame: The screen goes dark. Only the public-interest counsel's line remains under the banner.

Dialogue — PUBLIC-INTEREST COUNSEL:

Then continuation may be defensible. It is still not self-verifying.

Provenance: invented — transition to the verification and coordination questions on page 102.

Page notes

  • Do not caricature the continued-evaluation case. It is the best-argued position in the source record and this page's job is to let it be that. Every claim in panels 2–4 is attributed on-panel to a dated public talk.
  • No real person's name, likeness, or voice may appear. The speakers are unidentifiable silhouettes on a screen; the roles cited are alignment/safety and security/infrastructure, which is how the talk identifies them.
  • Panel 5 is the book's argument and must not be lettered as prosecution. The two lines state a resemblance and a stated reason. The reader draws the conclusion.
  • Panel 5's three callbacks must be reproduced at the same scale and line weight as the defensive circuit, or the rhyme will read as decoration.
  • Keep the OPENAI'S ASSESSMENT visual field of page 091 distinct from the cited-talk field here. One is a claim about tested effectiveness; the other is an argument about what must happen next.
  • The fictional role does not speak for OpenAI or any real laboratory.
