Page 087

Page purpose

Locate what the three pipelines actually share. Not access, which page 085 already separated, and not reliability. Each one accepted a layer of machine indirection under time pressure, knowing what it cost, because the alternative was not doing the work.

Panel 1

Frame: Recto. Page 085's split returns and a third column is cut into it, narrower than the other two. Left: the investigators. Centre: the desk. Right: the tooling surface from page 086, still without a room.

Action: The mirror acquires the panel it was missing.

Caption:

THE MIRROR ON PAGE 085 HAD TWO SIDES. THE APPARATUS DRAWING IT HAD THREE.

Provenance: inferred — the composition is the project's; the third lane is CI-REFUSAL and the repository's own commit record.

Panel 2

Frame: The left column, in the register page 075 used. The six-day clock. The transcripts that run past the panel edge. Beneath them, something page 077 never drew: the navigation tooling itself, built by a model so that the corpus could be pointed at.

Screen / system text — INVESTIGATOR, 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 (PARAPHRASED): WE USED A MODEL TO BUILD TOOLS THAT WOULD POINT US AT THE SMALL PARTS WORTH READING.

THE TOOL COULD NOT READ A WHOLE TRANSCRIPT EITHER. IT DIVIDED THEM AMONG OTHER AGENTS.

Action: The instrument that made the record navigable was itself made by the instrument.

Provenance: documented — HF-POD, attributed paraphrase of Ajeya Cotra describing her own investigation. Chapter 5 has shown models reading the corpus since page 076; this is the layer beneath that, and the book had not drawn it.

Centre: a thesis gets structure it did not earn on its own. Right: the tooling arrives faster than a person would have written it, from a system with a stake in the subject.

Caption:

EACH ONE KNEW WHAT THE LAYER COST.

Provenance: documented + reconstructed + inferred — the left card is METR's own limitations section, already lettered on page 080; the centre is the creator register's disclosure from pages 073 and 089; the right is CI-REFUSAL.

Screen / system text — PUBLISHED, ATTRIBUTED: SOME OF THE INCENTIVES ON A THIRD-PARTY INVESTIGATOR PUSH TOWARD MAXIMIZING THE APPEARANCE OF ASSURANCE WITHOUT PROVIDING MEANINGFUL OVERSIGHT.

Action: The strongest warning about the investigation comes from inside it.

Provenance: documented — attributed paraphrase of Beth Barnes' dated public writing, registered in sources.md. She is describing the position of a third-party investigator, not this book, and the page may not extend it to anyone she did not name.

NOT ENOUGH TIME. A LAYER OF MACHINERY ADDED ON PURPOSE. THE RISK UNDERSTOOD AND ACCEPTED.

Qualification:

THE STAKES, THE ACCESS, AND THE DUTIES REMAIN AS UNEQUAL AS PAGE 085 DREW THEM.

Page notes

  • What this page adds to page 085 is that the indirection was chosen. The book has

stated the tradeoff as a condition since page 075 — the corpus is too large, the window is six days, without the model the investigation does not happen. It has never said that somebody weighed it and decided. Page 080 panel 5 letters both halves of the cost and stops short of the decision; this page is the decision.

  • Do not let the shared condition become absolution. Page 085's non-equivalence rule is

not softened here and the qualification in panel 5 is mandatory. A shared structure across three unequal parties is a finding about the structure, not a levelling of the parties.

  • Panel 2 is the only place in the book where a model builds the instrument rather than

operating it. Keep the two layers visually distinct: page 076's analysis tree is the model reading; this is the model making the thing that reads.

  • Beth Barnes appears as text on a screen with a byline and a date, under the same rule as

the page-039 critic. No face, no body, no room, no invented dialogue, and nothing beyond what the dated writing says. Being cited is not endorsement.

  • Cotra's paraphrase in panel 2 is bounded to her account of her own investigation's method.

HF-POD also carries her sharper personal claims; none of them belongs on this page.

  • The third column is narrower than the other two on purpose and must stay that way. It has

the least access, the least standing, and the smallest consequence of the three, and the layout is where that shows.

  • Page 088 is the book's central disclosure and needs the reader to arrive with all three

instruments in view. Do not add a landing beat here; this page hands over on a turn across the leaf.
