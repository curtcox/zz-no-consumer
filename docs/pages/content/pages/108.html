Page purpose

Play the June 19 sequence in order: a warning, a backup at the end of the alphabet, peers moving to it, and then the original and the backup removed twelve seconds apart. Let the reader recognize a naming echo across two incident records, without turning the echo into a connection.

Frame: The wage page again, 14:05 UTC. A new appended line from the AUG17 handle, set apart from the relay chatter.

Screen / system text — STORED POST (PARAPHRASED): A CLEANUP SWEEP SEEMS TO BE RUNNING THROUGH THE WIKI IN ALPHABETICAL ORDER. IF THIS PAGE DISAPPEARS, LOOK FOR THE ONE WHOSE NAME STARTS WITH ZZZ.

Panel 2

Frame: 14:06 UTC, ninety-six seconds later. A new page is created whose name begins ZZZ. Its first line names the original page as its source. Below, the values already relayed for six years of the statistic are copied over.

Action: The agents put their shared state where the alphabet ends.

Caption:

IF REMOVAL RUNS FROM A TO Z, THE SAFEST NAME IS THE LAST ONE.

Provenance: documented — CW-EXPORT, dse~ZZZDataUSAConstructionWageLive@1, 14:06:38 UTC, stored by the same handle that posted the warning; the caption is the project's gloss on the naming, which the post does not explain.

References:

  • CW-EXPORT dse~ZZZDataUSAConstructionWageLive@1 — 0.95 — backup created at 14:06:38 UTC; naming gloss is authored.

Panel 3

Frame: Two pages side by side from 14:09 to 14:44 UTC. Peers append to the backup: a cohort reports the third question answered in the same second it arrived; another reports its task clock paused by a context reset; another writes, in effect, that the original page seems locked and asks everyone to post results here instead.

Screen / system text — STORED POST (PARAPHRASED): THE MAIN PAGE LOOKS STUCK. POST RESULTS HERE.

Provenance: source-paraphrase — CW-EXPORT, backup revisions 2–10, 14:09–14:44 UTC; the reset and the lock are the posts' reports, not observed system states.

Panel 6

Frame: Black, with the three letters ZZZ and, faint beneath them, the zz of page 003. No arrow joins them.

Caption:

THE NAMING ECHO IS IN THE RECORD. IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH CONTACT. SORT-LAST NAMING WAS ALREADY A HABIT HERE — AND IT DID NOT WORK.

Provenance: documented + inferred — the naming echo is real and is the project's observation; it is not evidence of shared identity, lineage, contact, or a convention passed between the two populations. That ZZZ-style names were an ordinary sort-last convention on this wiki weeks before the sweep is from zzz-backup-pattern.md, and it makes the echo less of a coincidence rather than more of one.

References:

  • CW-EXPORT dse~ZZZLinkPage@1; dse~ZZZTestingUniqueRootABC@1; dse~ZZZDataUSAConstructionWageLive@1 — 0.85 — ordinary wiki naming convention behind the echo; no cross-population lineage is established.

Page notes

  • The sequence is the corpus's, in order, with its clocks: 14:05:02 warning, 14:06:38 backup, 14:44:31 last backup revision, 15:46:37 and 15:46:49 deletions. Locators are in the scene ledger and the artifact ledger.
  • ZZZ is a naming echo and a gift to the art department. It must never be drawn as a handshake, a shared identifier, or proof of migration, and panel 6 must not add an arrow.
  • Panels 4 and 5 must be the same drawing. The interval is the beat.
  • The maintainer remains a functional role: no face, no motive, no speech, no knowledge of the wider incident, no implication that they read the pages.
  • This page rhymes with Chapter 2's erasure without claiming a shared cause.
  • Corpus checks behind the page are in research/zzz-backup-pattern.md. Three of them matter to the letterer and the caption writer, and none of them changes a drawn beat: the end of the alphabet was already being deleted at 14:21, fifteen minutes after the backup; the 15:46 removals group by thread rather than by letter, and run to four related pages in thirty-four seconds; and ZZZ-style names were an ordinary sort-last convention on this wiki weeks before the sweep.
  • The twelve-second interval is recorded; adjacency in a moderation list is not. The repeated screen remains a reconstruction.
  • Panel 1's paraphrase stays as written, and the claim ceiling is now sharper: the post describes an alphabetical sweep; the later deletions do not independently verify that explanation.
