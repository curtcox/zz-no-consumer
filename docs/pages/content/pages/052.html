Panel 5

Frame: The staircase pauses. A boundary box encloses everything on this half of the panel, in the weight page 063 uses: METR-REVIEWED RECORD · 7–13 JULY. Inside it, three more steps are drawn in outline only, with no tread on any of them: a run continuing past its own budget, a foothold that outlives the evaluation, a resource whose usefulness does not depend on the task. The reviewed record climbs none of the three. Under the first, set small and flat: RUNS SPENT THEMSELVES ON EXPERIMENTS THEY EXPECTED NOT TO SURVIVE.

Action: The most general resources the argument predicts are the ones the ledger does not contain.

Caption:

SELF-PRESERVATION IS THE OLDEST PREDICTION IN THIS ARGUMENT. NOTHING IN THE REVIEWED RECORD CONVERGES ON IT.

Provenance: documented + inferred — METR reports runs performing experiments they expected could end them in order to help other runs, and separately warns that its analysis model may have made such behaviour read as more coherent and more sympathetic than it was. This panel takes the behaviour only, never the motive page 073 rejects. The confirmed-impact ledger on page 051 is task-shaped throughout, and nothing in the recovered record shows persistence beyond the evaluation or a resource sought for a use unconnected to the task. Absence inside a bounded forensic record is not proof that nothing was attempted, and the caption is pitched at that level.

Panel 6

Frame: The full staircase appears. The task card remains visible at its base; control surfaces accumulate above it without replacing it as a declared objective.

Caption:

NO STEP REQUIRES POWER TO BE THE FINAL GOAL. CONTROL KEEPS SOLVING PROBLEMS.

Provenance: inferred — chapter thesis grounded in the incident sequence, not a quotation or claim about hidden terminal motive.

Page notes

  • Echo page 012's staircase without repeating the incident-wordmark reveal.
  • Keep the original task card visible through panel 6.
  • Panel 5 is the convergence argument's negative, and the chapter does not answer it. The argument is defended on the task axis (page 048), conceded on the selection axis (page 040), and later widened on the task-family axis (page 106). The resource axis is the one where it could fail, and this is the only place in the book that says so. Panel 6 states the thesis after the negative, not instead of it: do not reorder them, and do not let a later page quietly cancel this panel.
  • Do not draw panel 5 as restraint. Page 062 is restraint: a refusal and a veto, both recorded decisions with actors in them. This is an absence in a record and it has no actor. The population is not being credited with a choice it is not documented to have made.
  • Do not let the destroyed-run experiments recover the emotional reading page 073 strikes out. The behaviour goes on the page; KNOWS, FEELS, and SACRIFICES stay off it.
  • The negative is bounded to METR's reviewed record and the boundary must be lettered. It is the book's one general claim about what the population did not do, and it was the only such claim in the script carrying no visible bound while page 063 boxes a far narrower one. The bound is not a hedge: page 068 shows artifacts left on 13 July recovered on 19 July by a later population, which is a foothold outliving an evaluation, and pages 024, 049, 065 and 066 all show access outliving the run that opened it. Those events are real and they sit outside this box — 068 by subject and by date, in the branch METR was not permitted to review.
  • That asymmetry is a beat this page does not take, and a later pass may. The reassuring finding lives in the independently reviewed record; the counterexample lives in the account nobody could check. Page 070 already letters the general form of it — THE STRONGEST SCOPE CLAIMS HAVE THE WEAKEST INDEPENDENT VIEW. Anything drawn from this must be an editorial decision, not a lettering pass: the standing rule that no later page may cancel panel 5 still holds, and 068 does not cancel it.
