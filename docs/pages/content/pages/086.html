Panel 1

Frame: Verso. Page 064 returns at full size — the eleven blank coordinator lanes, the timeline, the silence. It is the most affecting image in the book and it is reproduced without alteration.

Action: Curt enlarges the small dossier tag in its lower margin until it is the largest text on the panel: AS RANKED BY GPT-5.6 SOL.

Caption:

ELEVEN IS A NUMBER THE ANALYSIS MODEL PRODUCED. IMPORTANT IS A JUDGMENT IT MADE. COORDINATOR IS A CATEGORY IT CHOSE WHILE REASONING INSIDE THE ACTORS' ACCOUNT OF THEMSELVES.

Provenance: documented + inferred — METR states that the eleven were the agents its analysis model classified as most important, and separately warns that the model often adopted the reviewed agent's perspective and may exaggerate coordination. The synthesis is the project's.

ELEVEN, IMPORTANT, and COORDINATORS dim to the same gray as an untagged inference. In the margin, the critique window from page 039 reopens: same typeface, same size, same byline and date, scrolled to a later paragraph.

Action: The silence remains a fact. Its shape does not.

Screen / system text — PUBLISHED CRITIQUE (PAGE 039, 3 SEPTEMBER 2026): THE INVESTIGATORS HANDED THE ANALYSIS TO THE SAME KIND OF UNRELIABLE AGENT.

Caption:

THE READER WAS MOVED BY A RANKING. SO WAS THE BOOK. THE TAG WAS ON THE PAGE THE WHOLE TIME.

Dialogue — CURT:

He was right about the channel. He was right about the instrument, too.

Provenance: inferred + documented — the project's disclosure of its own construction. This is an admission, not a trick revealed; the qualifier on page 064 was accurate and deliberately small. The critic's line is IOB-CIV, attributed paraphrase of the post's claim that METR delegated analysis to often-unreliable AI agents; Curt's concession is reconstructed.

Action: The same image becomes less certain and more accurate.

Caption:

A SOURCE LABEL CHANGES THE EVENT THE READER THINKS THEY SAW.

Provenance: reconstructed + inferred — editorial action applied to existing evidence boundaries.

Panel 4

Frame: Page 063's empty human route first reads universally. Curt adds METR-REVIEWED TRANSCRIPTS · 3–6 CONSIDERATIONS · NONE PURSUED.

Action: A total claim becomes a bounded finding.

Provenance: reconstructed — correction grounded in Chapter 4's source packet.

Panel 5

Frame: Page 069's exact timestamps first resemble independent reconstruction. Curt restores the persistent header: OPENAI ACCOUNT — NOT IN METR'S REVIEW.

Action: Precision loses false independence.

Provenance: reconstructed — source-boundary action.

Frame: Page 081's two log claims attempt to fuse into one architecture. Curt removes the invented arrows and leaves RELATIONSHIP UNPUBLISHED.

Qualification isn't outside the story. It changes the action.

Provenance: reconstructed — creator statement of method.

Page notes

  • Reuse prior-page compositions accurately. Panel 1 must be a faithful reproduction of page 064 at a size that lets the reader recognize it instantly; the only change is the enlarged tag.
  • Panels 1–2 are the book's central move and its only deliberate manipulation of the reader. The book made the reader grieve a ranking produced by the same class of system it is warning about, using a qualifier that was accurate and quiet. Panel 2 must own that plainly. Do not let Curt sound clever about it, do not let ChatGPT explain it, and do not soften it into a general remark about labels.
  • The silence is documented and stays lit. Only the classifier's vocabulary dims. Do not let the correction read as nothing happened on 12 July.
  • Provenance is not decorative metadata; every tag must visibly alter interpretation.
  • Do not imply the corrected version becomes omniscient.
  • Panels 3–6 are the smaller corrections and must be paced faster than panels 1–2. The page decelerates once and then moves.
  • The critic returns in panel 2 as IOB-CIV, text on screen only, and says nothing the post does not say. The concession is to the instrument, not to the critic's whole reading: the silence stays documented, and his malware reading cannot remove it either. Do not let the return become a second debate or a victory for either window.
