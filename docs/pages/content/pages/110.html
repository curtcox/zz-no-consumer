Page purpose

Put the September finding beside the August record Curt built the book from, and turn the gap into questions with names, not into an accusation.

Panel 1

Frame: Curt lays the two 26 August reports and the Black Hat talk, the book's whole public account, on one side of the desk. On the other side, the 4 September report. Between them, a blank lane on the page plan with the label he gave it on page 109.

Dialogue — CURT:

I used everything that was public. This was public too. It was not an account.

Provenance: reconstructed + inferred — creator exchange; the distinction between public traces and a public incident account is the project's, drawn from CW-REPORT's disclosure discussion.

Panel 2

Frame: A dated statement, set as attributed text with its source visible, in the register the book uses for the critic's window: authors, date, no face.

Screen / system text — REPORT, 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 (PARAPHRASED): AS OF THIS REPORT, ITS AUTHORS STATE THAT OPENAI HAD NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED THE WIKI INCIDENT, AND THAT THE 26 AUGUST REPORTS DO NOT EXPLICITLY MENTION IT.

Provenance: documented — CW-REPORT, the discovery and disclosure section; the claim is the authors' and is bounded to what they found. This project has not independently established the absence of every earlier disclosure.

Panel 3

Frame: Two dated cards from the two lanes of page 109, placed touching but not joined. Left, hatched: 26 JUNE — RESEARCHERS ATTRIBUTE CONCENTRATED VISITS TO THE WIKI TO OPENAI-ASSOCIATED ADDRESSES, INCLUDING RECOVERY OF ARCHIVED PAGES. Right, solid: 27 JUNE — RESPONDERS IDENTIFY THE EVALUATION, THE BOARD, AND THE PIVOT, AND ADVISE THAT STOPPING IS NOT REQUIRED (page 027).

Action: One day apart, two channels, and no record of whether knowledge of one reached the people handling the other.

Caption:

DID THE PEOPLE WHO FOUND ONE CHANNEL TALK TO THE PEOPLE WHO DECIDED ABOUT THE OTHER? THE RECORD DOES NOT SAY. THE BOOK WILL NOT GUESS.

Provenance: documented + inferred — the 26 June attribution is CW-REPORT's inference about visitors, hatched; the 27 June decision is OAI-TR §X, already on page 027. Placing them side by side is the project's; joining them would be invention.

Panel 4

Frame: Curt writes a column of questions on the page plan, each ending in a question mark, none answered: who investigated the wiki; what was retained; which teams were told; whether a cross-workload review happened; whether the case was available to the independent reviewers; why the September researchers found no specific public account.

Dialogue — CURT:

These are not accusations. They are the questions an account would have answered.

Provenance: reconstructed + inferred — creator action; the questions are the project's and are drawn from the proposal's disclosure analysis. No answer is implied.

Panel 5

Frame: Five small labelled figures, drawn as the silhouettes of page 016: A VISITOR, A TEAM, A RESPONDER, AN EXECUTIVE, AN OUTSIDE INVESTIGATOR. A bracket tries to enclose them under one word, OPENAI, and fails to close.

Caption:

"OPENAI KNEW" IS FIVE DIFFERENT CLAIMS. THE RECORD SUPPORTS AT MOST ONE OF THEM, ABOUT A VISITOR, BY INFERENCE.

Provenance: inferred — the project's decomposition of the disclosure question; the single supported claim is CW-REPORT's attributed-visit inference, not an authenticated account of any person's conclusions.

Page notes

  • Nondisclosure is attributed and dated: as of the 4 September report, its authors state. Do not letter it as a fact about OpenAI's knowledge or intent.
  • No secret meeting, no cover-up decision, no invented acknowledgment by OpenAI, no accusation that METR knowingly omitted the archive. METR's scope was 26 June to 13 July, by subject the Artifactory incident; a scoped investigation is not a promise to inventory every contemporary workload.
  • Panel 3 is the strongest juxtaposition in the addition and the easiest to abuse. The cards touch; nothing connects them. The 27 June card must be the drawing from page 027, unchanged.
  • Alternative explanations (scope, incomplete investigation, fragmented internal knowledge, attribution uncertainty, private remediation, deliberate withholding) carry different implications and equal standing on the page. Do not rank them.
  • The composite forum of Chapter 6 is bounded by the August record and does not get this knowledge. The creator scene is the only place it lives.
