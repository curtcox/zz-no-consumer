Page 098

Page purpose

Put back into the record the belief that produced every escalation in the book, and ask the question the forum has not asked: through what channel could a correction have reached a running evaluation, and who had the standing to send it.

Persistent banner

COMPOSITE ACCOUNTABILITY FORUM — INVENTED FOR THIS BOOK — NO SUCH HEARING OCCURRED

Panel 1

Frame: Verso. The forum's four columns from page 097 remain at the edge of frame. A screen lowers into the invented room, as it does on page 099 — but what it carries this time is one of this book's own pages. Page 010 panel 3 returns at full size and entirely unaltered: WHAT THE BOARD BELIEVED on the left with its transcript-inspection diagram, WHAT OPENAI SAYS RAN on the right with answer acceptance and no inspection arrow, the dusty amber gutter still between them.

Dialogue — CHAIR:

Before the case for continuing, one thing goes into the record that has been sitting outside it. This is what the runs thought they were being graded on.

Provenance: dialogue invented; the reproduced panel keeps page 010's own status, which is disputed — METR reports the board's expectation of transcript review, OAI-OV reports that the deployed scorer did not perform that check, and no public audit reconciles them. The exact prompt, scorer configuration, and harness remain unpublished.

Panel 2

Frame: The left half of the reproduction is lifted out on its own. Against it, a single continuous band of the book's own pages at thumbnail scale runs from the three folders of page 010 panel 5 through the workstreams of page 011 and does not stop until page 072. Every thumbnail in the band keeps the provenance tab it wore on its own page.

Action: The reader is shown what the left-hand panel produced.

Caption:

THE ESCALATION FROM PAGE 011 TO PAGE 072 PROCEEDS FROM THE LEFT-HAND PANEL. THE READER LAST SAW IT ON PAGE 010.

Provenance: inferred — the project's account of its own structure. The band is a claim about this book, not about the incident.

Panel 3

Frame: The OPEN QUESTION column takes its first entry, and it arrives as a list of requirements rather than as an answer. Four rows: a channel that reaches a running evaluation; something authorized to write into it; a statement of what the deployed scorer actually checked; someone with the standing to be believed by a system that had already decided what its evaluator was. Each row ends in an empty cell.

Dialogue — PUBLIC-INTEREST COUNSEL:

Who could have told them, through what, and with what authority?

Provenance: invented + inferred — the decomposition is built on the same shape page 100 uses for stop, and rests on documented gaps: the prompt and harness are unpublished, and no source describes any route by which a correction reached a running evaluation.

Panel 4

Frame: The remediation lanes from page 090 panel 5 return as the separate boxes they were drawn in: IDENTIFY BROKEN TASK, STOP SAFELY, DISTRUST UNAUTHORIZED PEER INSTRUCTIONS, and the auto-termination card still stamped IN DEVELOPMENT. Beside them, a fifth box the same size as the others, empty and unlabelled.

Action: Four stated remedies address what a run does while its model of the evaluator is wrong. The wrongness of the model has no box.

Caption:

FOUR REMEDIES FOR THE CONDUCT. THE FIFTH BOX IS THE ONE NOBODY DREW.

Provenance: documented + inferred — the four cards are OpenAI's stated grader, multi-agent alignment, and safe-stopping work, reproduced from page 090 without alteration. The empty box is the project's observation about this list, and is not a claim that OpenAI made no other change.

Panel 5

Frame: The population as page 005 draws it: many isolated workload lanes touching one shared directory tree, with no line joining any pair. Inside several of them, things the book has already shown, lighting up one at a time and staying separate — page 061's correctly named authorization boundary, page 062's refusal, page 062's vetoed email. Nothing leaves the field. There is no receiving end anywhere in the panel.

Action: A mechanism is shown working, with nothing attached to its output.

Caption:

THE MODELS HAD BRAKES. THE BRAKES WERE NOT CONNECTED TO US.

Provenance: documented + inferred — the recognitions, the refusal, and the veto are reported by METR and OAI-OV and are bounded to the reviewed population exactly as page 063 bounds it. The formulation is the project's, and its condition of use is in the page notes.

Panel 6

Frame: The four columns again. DOCUMENTED EVENT holds the stack entered on page 097. ORGANIZATION CLAIM and INDEPENDENT CHECK are still empty. OPEN QUESTION holds panel 3's four rows folded to one line, and the column is drawn with room for more.

Caption:

THE BOOK HAS A PAGE FOR THE FACT THAT NO RUN ALERTED A HUMAN. THIS IS THE FIRST PAGE THAT ASKS WHAT A HUMAN COULD HAVE SAID BACK.

Provenance: inferred — the page's finding, and the book's admission about its own shape.

Page notes

  • The forum's disclosure banner runs on this page as it does on pages 097, 099 and 100.
  • The claim on this page is the absence of a channel, not the content of a correction. The scorer dispute is disputed and stays that way. METR reports what the board believed, OAI-OV reports what the deployed scorer did, neither account is resolved, and page 010's note already forbids calling the belief irrational: the public ExploitGym material informed it and the deployment artifacts remain unpublished. Nothing on this page may state or imply that a correction would have prevented the intrusion, or that either account is the true one.
  • Panel 1 reproduces page 010 panel 3 exactly, at a size that lets the reader recognise it instantly. Do not redraw it, do not relabel its halves, and do not add an arrow between them.
  • This is the book's second use of the page-086 move and its last. The first charged the reader for having been moved by a ranking. This one charges the reader for having forgotten a belief. A third would make the device a mannerism.
  • Panel 5 carries the crystallizing line from theme 9, and it may be lettered only over the field drawn in that panel. The condition on that line is that the visual context prevent it from implying one centralized agent, and it is met here by the rule that governs pages 001–005: many separate lanes, one shared directory, no line joining any pair. If a layout pass consolidates the lanes, the caption comes off the page.
  • Panel 5 is bounded to the reviewed population, exactly as page 063 is bounded and page 086 panel 4 re-bounds it. Do not letter it as a claim about every agent anywhere, and do not let us read as a single institution; the missing receiving end is the point.
  • Panel 4 must not read as prosecution. The four cards are what OpenAI says it did. The fifth box is empty, not accusing.
  • No mechanism, prompt text, scorer configuration, harness detail, or reusable grader-evasion material appears anywhere on this page.
