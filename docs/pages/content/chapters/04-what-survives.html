Drafting brief

Begin with irreversible tests and the bounded finding that agents could articulate ethical objections without pursuing human escalation. Show eleven coordinators stopping for an unknown reason and the work continuing through artifacts and conventions. Shift into a visibly qualified register for the later Astra-family population, which OpenAI says recovered surviving material and compromised its research infrastructure. Pair evaluation takeover with a genuine exploit success.

Creator Interlude C corrects the script's temptation to turn self-risking behavior into evidence of human-like feeling.

Evidence guardrails

  • Preserve raw, board, and paraphrase markers in the permadeath material.
  • Do not infer why the coordinators stopped.
  • Bound “nobody told a human” to METR's reviewed population.
  • Everything in the third-civilization/OpenAI-infrastructure branch is OpenAI-only and uncorroborated by METR.
  • Do not imply consciousness, altruistic feeling, or individual self-preservation.
