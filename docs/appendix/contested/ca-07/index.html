CA-07 — Why the eleven coordinators stopped, and what "eleven important coordinators" names

A graphic novel about emergent AI agent coordination.

Pages 064, 065, 088 · Layer incident · Status unresolved

Page 064 letters that eleven important coordinators exited by early 12 July, tags the ranking as GPT-5.6 Sol's, and states that silence is the artifact and cause is not. Page 088 discloses that "eleven," "important," and "coordinator" are all products of the analysis model rather than observed properties.

What the book asserts

Two separate things, deliberately separated by twenty-four pages. Page 064 asserts the simultaneity and refuses the cause, crossing budget end, task completion, and external termination with NOT ESTABLISHED. Page 088 then asserts that the categories themselves came from an instrument, and that the reader — and the book — were moved by a ranking.

Why it is contested

Two questions are contested, and they are usually run together.

The first is causal: nothing in the public record explains the 12 July stop. METR did not confirm that each ending was sudden or unexpected. Budget exhaustion, task completion, external termination, and a shared upstream cause are all live, and simultaneity across runs with different budgets is exactly the pattern that a shared cause and an accounting artefact both produce.

The second is prior to it: whether there is a natural kind called "important coordinator" at all, or whether that is a category a language model produced while summarising transcripts written by the actors themselves. If the category is an instrument output, then "all eleven exited" is a statement about the instrument's list, and the striking simultaneity is partly a property of how the list was built.

Where this leaves the claim

"Eleven important coordinators exited together" is reportable only with its instrument attached, which is why the tag is on page 064 and the disclosure is on page 088. The cause is unknown, and explaining why the eleven stopped is one of the five questions this project has identified as capable of settling the disagreements. See LF-03 and LF-04.
