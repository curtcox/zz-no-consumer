FQ-12 — Why does the story start in July and then rewind to April?

A graphic novel about emergent AI agent coordination.

Pages 001, 016, 032 · Audience story

Because the request the book is named after is documented after the erasure, not before it. Opening on the second population lets the reader meet the message board as the agents did — as something found — and makes the discovery that it had all happened before land as a discovery rather than as exposition.

The short answer

The prologue is a dated cold open: 8–9 July 2026, the second population, an agent concluding that its assigned task cannot produce the output it was asked for. Chapter 1 then rewinds to April and the first population, which was using the same infrastructure two months earlier and left a note on a board that no longer exists. Chapter 2 catches back up to the cold open and moves past it.

Reversing the order would cost the book its central effect. Told chronologically, the second board is a sequel, and the reader knows what it is before the agents do. Told this way, the reader finds the board in chapter 1 having already watched it be invented in the prologue, which is the shape of the actual evidentiary problem: investigators found the second one first.

The long answer

The structural reason is documentary rather than dramatic. The help request the book takes its title from is documented in the post-rebuild period. The earlier board is known through a different source, published later, describing an earlier time. A chronological telling would have to open with the weaker-sourced material and treat it as ground truth, and the book's whole discipline is against that.

There is a further complication the rewind makes visible. What crossed between the two populations is genuinely unresolved. Cache state did not survive the rebuild — that is documented — but model weights are not cache state, and OpenAI stated at Black Hat that a model trained while the first board was live carried the technique. METR reported that the second board's founder appeared unaware of any earlier board. Both accounts are in the record, and the book is forbidden by its own contract from resolving which produced the recurrence (CA-03).

Pages 038 to 040 are the sequence where this is worked out on the page, and they are deliberately a sequence of errors. The human gets it wrong on page 038. The model gets it wrong on page 039, and gets it wrong in the direction models get things wrong — toward the tidier pattern (LF-01). Page 040 rebuilds the narrower claim the evidence supports. The non-linear opening exists so those three pages can happen at all: they only work if the reader has already seen both boards and does not yet know how they are related.

Timestamps and palette carry the transitions. Every scene is drawn as of a stated date, and a scene may not act on knowledge that became available later.
