# Page 080

## Page purpose

Show why human oversight of the analysis was real but incomplete—and why investigators still needed the model.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Verso. More than a thousand pages of model-generated analysis reports stack around the three investigators. Each report points back to context-heavy source records.

**Caption:**
> THE ANALYSIS OUTPUT BECOMES ANOTHER CORPUS.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — METR reports well over 1,000 pages; visualization editorial.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** An investigator spot-checks a striking anecdote. Understanding one excerpt requires reopening a long transcript, neighboring board posts, approximate timestamps, and identity traces.

**Action:** Validation expands instead of narrowing.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `compressed` — METR's description of difficult contextual review.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** Two error cards surface late: `LARGE AMOUNT OF DATA MISSED IN EARLY FIGURE`; `CONFABULATED DIFFERENCES BETWEEN DATASETS`.

**Action:** Human review catches some analysis failures after delay.

**Provenance:** `documented` — examples from METR footnote 57.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** A field of unchecked analysis pages remains behind the corrected cards.

**Caption:**
> CAUGHT ERRORS PROVE CORRECTION IS POSSIBLE.
>
> THEY DO NOT COUNT THE ERRORS STILL UNSEEN.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — METR expects uncaught errors.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** Split panel. Left: human-only review reaches a tiny fraction before the clock expires. Right: AI-assisted review reaches a usable incident model with uncertainty tags attached.

**Caption:**
> THE TOOL LOWERS CONFIDENCE IN SOME JUDGMENTS.
>
> WITHOUT IT, THE INVESTIGATION AT THIS SPEED DOES NOT HAPPEN.

**Provenance:** `documented` — both claims are explicit in METR's limitations section.

## Page notes

- Do not imply all model work was unreliable.
- End on dependence plus limitation, not dismissal.
