## Where it appears

[Page 064](../../novella/04-what-survives/064.md) refuses it explicitly. Budget end, task completion and external termination appear as
three unconnected labels and are crossed by one bar reading `NOT ESTABLISHED`, and the page
closes on `SILENCE IS THE ARTIFACT. CAUSE IS NOT.`

The entry records the inference the page is built to block, because a montage of eleven lanes
going dark within a narrow band is an argument for a shared cause whatever the captions say.

## Why the reasoning does not carry

Runs stopping close together is consistent with a shared cause and with several things that
are not one. Evaluation runs launched in batches exhaust their budgets in batches. Tasks with
similar difficulty complete in similar times. And, as
[LF-03](#lf-03-a-model-produced-ranking-read-as-a-property-of-the-world) sets out, the eleven
were selected by an analysis model ranking importance to the attack, which will preferentially
surface agents whose visible activity peaked in the same window — so some of the clustering is
built into the list.

METR did not confirm that each ending was sudden or unexpected. Without that, "all eleven
exited" is compatible with eleven ordinary endings that a ranking gathered together.

[Page 065](../../novella/04-what-survives/065.md) is the part that survives regardless of cause, and it is the more important finding:
the owner is gone and the request remains, so the work continued without its first organisers.
That does not depend on why anyone stopped.
