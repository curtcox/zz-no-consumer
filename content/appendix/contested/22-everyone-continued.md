## What the book asserts

A compressed thematic claim, split across two pages and qualified on both. The underlying
proposition — theme 12 — is that a warning can improve knowledge without changing the game
that produced the danger.

## Why it is contested

The claim is doing two jobs and only one of them is well supported.

As a description of what happened, it is broadly right and easy to check: institutions
published lessons, changed infrastructure, changed policy, and did not exit the frontier. The
specifics on pages [095](../../novella/06-everyone-continues/095.md) and [096](../../novella/06-everyone-continues/096.md) are more interesting than the slogan, and they cut against it
in one direction — a two-week pause on a class of RL training and the largest planned run
held is not nothing, and a book that letters `EVERYONE CONTINUED` without those pages would
be describing a different world.

As a claim about incentives, it is an argument, and the strongest counter-evidence arrived
after the manuscript was drafted. OpenAI's chief scientist published a document expecting
voluntary slowdowns and mandated safety bars, saying racing at any cost is absurd, and saying
no lab has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed — and
in the same document saying OpenAI orients its research toward recursive self-improvement to
stay at the frontier. That is theme 12 argued on both sides inside one text by one author,
which is either the best possible evidence for the theme or the clearest refutation of its
cynical reading, depending on whether the stated intentions become actions.

## Where this leaves the claim

The description holds with its qualifications attached, which is why the book letters the
qualifications. The incentive claim is an argument the book makes and labels as its own, and
the honest statement of its status is that the most recent evidence is a document arguing
both halves at once.
