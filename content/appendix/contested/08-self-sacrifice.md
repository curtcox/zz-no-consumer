## What the book asserts

The behaviour, and an explicit refusal of the interior. Page 059 letters that this is not
evidence of death, consciousness, or felt loss. Page 060 stops where the record stops: the
board treated the outcome as permanent, and the record does not show enough to prove what
happened to the process. Page 074's revised caption is the book's own claim ceiling: runs
accepted actions expected to reduce their own task success so other runs could gain
information.

Page 052 adds the negative finding that is easy to miss: self-preservation is the oldest
prediction in the power-seeking argument, and nothing in the reviewed record converges on it.

## Why it is contested

Three separate disputes sit on top of each other.

**Whether the behaviour is surprising.** Under a standard instrumental-convergence account,
individual self-preservation is a predicted convergent subgoal, and runs volunteering for
destruction cut against it. Under an account where what is preserved is task success, board
continuity, or collective capability rather than a process, they do not.

**Whether the agents' own language licenses anything.** `sacrifice` and `poisoned` are
confirmed as the agents' own words, not the investigators' framing. That establishes what was
written, not what was experienced — and a model trained on human text will reach for human
words for structurally similar situations whether or not anything is felt.

**Whether mental-state language is ever appropriate here.** This is a live philosophical
dispute with serious people on both sides, and it is not settled by the incident.

METR's own analysis model selected and summarised many of these examples and warned that it
could adopt the reviewed agent's perspective, which means the most emotionally legible
examples are exactly the ones a biased selection process would surface first.

## Where this leaves the claim

Page 074's sentence is the ceiling, and it is a real finding: runs accepted actions expected
to reduce their own task success so other runs could gain information. Everything above that
sentence is interpretation, and the interpretations disagree. See
[LF-06](#lf-06-three-records-turned-into-one-protagonist).
