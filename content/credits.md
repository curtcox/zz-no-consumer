# Endmatter — Credits

**Status:** endmatter, not a story page. It is excluded from the page count by the [story contract](story-contract.md), is not managed by `scripts/pagination.py`, and carries no provenance front matter of its own.

## Purpose

Close the book the way its pages open every claim: by saying who made it, with what, and from whose public work.

## Text

The credits are set from [`CREDITS.md`](../CREDITS.md) at the repository root, which is also the public site's credits page. Do not restate the list here; a second copy drifts.

## Layout

- One or two unnumbered pages after the final story page, facing the black page or the endmatter the contract allows. Keep the same paper tone and lettering family as the story; no art.
- Order as in the source file: author; AI systems and what each did; sources and named commentators; tools; licence; provenance vocabulary.
- The AI-systems block is set in the same screen register as the ChatGPT windows in the creator scenes, so the disclosure reads as part of the book's method rather than a legal notice.
- Print the provenance vocabulary as a key, one line per tag, so a reader who skipped the page notes can decode the panels on the way out.
- Never print the page count as a fixed number; the count is a measurement.

## Checks

- The names, models and licences match `CREDITS.md` on the day of lock.
- No commentator is quoted; the book paraphrases dated public writing and links to it.
- The licence line matches `LICENSE`.
