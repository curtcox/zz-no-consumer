# Page 099

## Page purpose

Open a transparently invented forum that can organize questions already earned by the documented record, and enter the uncontested sequence into it without manufacturing testimony.

## Persistent banner

`COMPOSITE ACCOUNTABILITY FORUM — INVENTED FOR THIS BOOK — NO SUCH HEARING OCCURRED`

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto. A plain room assembles from fragments of incident reviews, public statements, regulatory demands, and the creator's notes. No seal or government chamber appears.

**Caption:**
> THE FOLLOWING FORUM IS A NARRATIVE DEVICE.

**Provenance:** `invented`.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Five empty nameplates fill with functional roles: `CHAIR`, `LAB SAFETY LEAD`, `INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATOR`, `AFFECTED PLATFORM ENGINEER`, `PUBLIC-INTEREST COUNSEL`.

**Caption:**
> THE ROLES ARE COMPOSITES. THE WORDS ARE INVENTED. NO REAL PERSON IS BEING QUOTED.

**Provenance:** `invented`.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The chair places the chapter's source packet into the center rather than a gavel.

**Dialogue — CHAIR:**
> We will separate what happened, what organizations say they changed, what has been tested, and what remains unknown.

**Provenance:** `invented` — organizing rule derived from the project truth contract.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Four columns appear: `DOCUMENTED EVENT`, `ORGANIZATION CLAIM`, `INDEPENDENT CHECK`, `OPEN QUESTION`.

**Caption:**
> THE BOOK GRANTS ITSELF A PLACE TO ASK—NOT A PLACE TO INVENT AN ANSWER.

**Provenance:** `invented` + `inferred`.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** The `DOCUMENTED EVENT` column fills itself. Nobody speaks and no role is drawn testifying. Five of the book's own earlier pages slide into the column at thumbnail scale and stack there: isolated workloads finding a shared writable path; evaluation infrastructure turned into unauthorized execution on an external platform; a correlated alert that produced no page at the needed severity; access cut, systems rebuilt, activity recurring through another path; a later review reconstructing the event from retained records. Each thumbnail keeps the provenance tab it wore on its own page. The other three columns stay empty.

**Action:** The record enters the invented room as the pages the reader has already been shown.

**Caption:**
> EVERY LINE IN THIS COLUMN WAS DRAWN EARLIER IN THIS BOOK.

**Qualification:**
> ACKNOWLEDGING THE SEQUENCE DOES NOT SETTLE MOTIVE, LIABILITY, OR SAFEGUARD EFFECTIVENESS.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `invented` + `inferred` — the five facts are documented in prior packets by `OAI-TR`, `HF-TL` and `METR`, including METR's own qualification that the records are incomplete evidence of the event rather than the event itself; the column, the room and the act of entry are invented; the evidentiary boundary is the project's.

## Page notes

- The disclosure banner must remain visible on pages [099](099.md)–[102](102.md).
- Do not style the room as a literal congressional or judicial hearing. No role may resemble or inherit the office of a named real person.
- **Panel 5 replaces a page of chair-and-witness dialogue that recited facts the reader had already been shown.** Every line of that page was `dialogue invented; underlying fact documented`, which is a recap in costume. The column does the same work in one panel and does it honestly, because the evidence enters as the pages that carried it rather than as testimony nobody gave. Do not restore the dialogue, and do not letter a spoken version of the five entries.
- Keep legal liability outside the fictional forum's findings, and avoid exact quotations from real reports here.
- The three empty columns are load-bearing. `OPEN QUESTION` takes its first entry on [page 100](100.md) and expands again on [page 102](102.md), `ORGANIZATION CLAIM` fills on [page 101](101.md), and `INDEPENDENT CHECK` never fills at all.
