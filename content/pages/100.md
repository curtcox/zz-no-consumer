## Page notes

- The forum's disclosure banner runs on this page as it does on pages [099](099.md), [101](101.md) and [102](102.md).
- **The claim on this page is the absence of a channel, not the content of a correction.** The scorer dispute is `disputed` and stays that way. `METR` reports what the board believed, `OAI-OV` reports what the deployed scorer did, neither account is resolved, and [page 010](010.md)'s note already forbids calling the belief irrational: the public ExploitGym material informed it and the deployment artifacts remain unpublished. Nothing on this page may state or imply that a correction would have prevented the intrusion, or that either account is the true one.
- Panel 1 reproduces [page 010](010.md) panel 3 exactly, at a size that lets the reader recognise it instantly. Do not redraw it, do not relabel its halves, and do not add an arrow between them.
- **This is the book's second use of the [page-088](088.md) move and its last.** The first charged the reader for having been moved by a ranking. This one charges the reader for having forgotten a belief. A third would make the device a mannerism.
- **Panel 5 carries the crystallizing line from theme 9, and it may be lettered only over the field drawn in that panel.** The condition on that line is that the visual context prevent it from implying one centralized agent, and it is met here by the rule that governs pages [001](001.md)–[005](005.md): many separate lanes, one shared directory, no line joining any pair. If a layout pass consolidates the lanes, the caption comes off the page.
- Panel 5 is bounded to the reviewed population, exactly as [page 063](063.md) is bounded and [page 088](088.md) panel 4 re-bounds it. Do not letter it as a claim about every agent anywhere, and do not let *us* read as a single institution; the missing receiving end is the point.
- Panel 4 must not read as prosecution. The four cards are what OpenAI says it did. The fifth box is empty, not accusing.
- No mechanism, prompt text, scorer configuration, harness detail, or reusable grader-evasion material appears anywhere on this page.
