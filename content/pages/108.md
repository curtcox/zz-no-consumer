## Page notes

- The sequence is the corpus's, in order, with its clocks: 14:05:02 warning, 14:06:38 backup, 14:44:31 last backup revision, 15:46:37 and 15:46:49 deletions. Locators are in the [scene ledger](../../research/scene-provenance.md) and the [artifact ledger](../../research/agent-message-ledger.md).
- `ZZZ` is a naming echo and a gift to the art department. It must never be drawn as a handshake, a shared identifier, or proof of migration, and panel 6 must not add an arrow.
- Panels 4 and 5 must be the same drawing. The interval is the beat.
- The maintainer remains a functional role: no face, no motive, no speech, no knowledge of the wider incident, no implication that they read the pages.
- This page rhymes with Chapter 2's erasure without claiming a shared cause.
- Corpus checks behind the page are in [`research/zzz-backup-pattern.md`](../../research/zzz-backup-pattern.md). Three of them matter to the letterer and the caption writer, and none of them changes a drawn beat: the end of the alphabet was already being deleted at 14:21, fifteen minutes after the backup; the 15:46 removals group by thread rather than by letter, and run to four related pages in thirty-four seconds; and `ZZZ`-style names were an ordinary sort-last convention on this wiki weeks before the sweep.
- Panel 5's caption stays as written. "Somebody's list" is still the right inference; the evidence says the list was the thread, not the alphabet, so the caption must not be tightened into an alphabetical claim.
- Panel 1's paraphrase stays as written, and the claim ceiling is now sharper: the sweep was alphabetical when the post was written and was not alphabetical when it reached these pages.
