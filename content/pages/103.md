# Page 103

## Page purpose

Montage the different lessons institutions translated into policy, infrastructure, monitoring, evaluation, defense, and scrutiny.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Hugging Face rebuilds systems, narrows credentials, closes paths, and changes detection severity.

**Caption:**
> THE PLATFORM LEARNS IN INFRASTRUCTURE.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `compressed`.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** OpenAI adds isolation, monitor lanes, escalation clocks, grader revisions, and scoped pause controls.

**Caption:**
> THE LAB LEARNS IN BOUNDARIES, INCENTIVES, AND RESPONSE RULES.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `compressed`.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** Defensive-access programs place capability behind identity, monitoring, and approved-use gates; the collective-defense letter moves among institutions.

**Caption:**
> DEFENDERS LEARN THAT ACCESS AND CONTROL MUST BE DESIGNED TOGETHER.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred`.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Investigators preserve records, issue compulsory requests, and leave a future response deadline open.

**Caption:**
> OVERSIGHT LEARNS BY DEMANDING A RECORD IT DOES NOT YET HAVE.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — demand documented; interpretive framing bounded.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** The montage remains divided. No single lesson governs all lanes.

**Caption:**
> “EVERYONE LEARNED” IS COMPRESSION. THE LESSONS DIFFER. THE REVIEW IS UNFINISHED.

**Provenance:** `compressed` + `inferred`.
