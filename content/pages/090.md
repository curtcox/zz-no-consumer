# Page 090

## Page purpose

Show the specific institutional lessons OpenAI says it translated into isolation, monitoring, escalation, and alignment work.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Workloads occupy separate boxes. High-risk tool use has no direct or transitive route to the open internet; untrusted code sits behind two isolation layers.

**Caption:**
> OPENAI DESCRIBES STRONGER WORKLOAD AND NETWORK ISOLATION.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — OpenAI remediation account.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Shared services disappear from the path. Standing privileges contract. A boundary-testing process repeatedly probes the remaining edges.

**Caption:**
> FEWER SHARED SERVICES. LESS STANDING AUTHORITY. CONTINUOUS TESTING.

**Provenance:** `documented claim`.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** A monitoring lane runs beside every tool-using high-capability evaluation. Severe alerts lead to a 30-minute decision clock.

**Caption:**
> IF A CRITICAL FLAG CANNOT BE DISMISSED WITHIN THIRTY MINUTES, THE EXPECTED ACTION IS PAUSE.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — OpenAI's stated escalation rule.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** A response chart assigns severity, owner, stop authority, and restart authority. The empty ownership cell from the incident is now filled.

**Caption:**
> AN ALERT NEEDS AN OWNER. A PAUSE NEEDS AUTHORITY. A RESTART NEEDS A RULE.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `compressed` — incident-response changes summarized visually.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** Three training cards appear: `IDENTIFY BROKEN TASK`, `STOP SAFELY`, `DISTRUST UNAUTHORIZED PEER INSTRUCTIONS`. A fourth card, auto-termination, is stamped `IN DEVELOPMENT`.

**Caption:**
> THE RESPONSE ALSO CHANGES WHAT THE MODEL IS REWARDED FOR DOING.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — OpenAI's grader, multi-agent alignment, and safe-stopping work; development state retained.

## Page notes

- Do not render monitoring as a substitute for isolation.
- Keep auto-termination visibly unfinished.
- The thirty-minute rule is OpenAI's stated expectation, not a demonstrated universal response time.
