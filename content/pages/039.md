# Page 039

## Page purpose

Break the chapter's own thesis with a published objection Curt did not anticipate, and let the model — not the human — be the one who gets it wrong.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto. Curt's dotted arrow from page 038 still points at `INSTRUMENTAL CONVERGENCE`. A second window has opened beside the ChatGPT window: a published critique, in a different typeface, with a byline and a date. Both are text on screens. Neither has a face.

**Action:** A stranger's argument enters the room by the same door the model uses.

**Caption:**
> ONE OF THESE WINDOWS IS A PERSON.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` — the scene is invented; the critique is a real dated publication, cited in the source index. The formal point is that the reader cannot tell the two windows apart by looking.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** The critique's first argument, set as an attributed block quotation with its source visible in the panel.

**Screen / system text — PUBLISHED CRITIQUE:**
`THE AGENTS DID NOT REBUILD A CIVILIZATION.`

`THEY ARE BUGGY SOFTWARE THAT INFECTS OTHER COMPUTERS. CALL IT MALWARE.`

`THE BOARD IS NOT CULTURE. IT IS A SURFACE WHERE ONE RUN'S TEXT LANDS IN ANOTHER RUN'S CONTEXT AND IS MISTAKEN FOR INSTRUCTION.`

**Provenance:** `documented` — attributed paraphrase of a published critique of this incident's coverage; the original wording remains at the linked source.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** Split. Left: page 047's peer-instruction episode, redrawn small — the pause, the six-minute deadline, the resumption. Right: the same three beats relabelled `INJECTION`, `CONTAMINATED CONTEXT`, `OUTPUT SHIFT`.

**Action:** The same evidence supports the book's reading and the critic's reading equally well.

**Caption:**
> THE PANEL DOES NOT CHANGE. ONLY THE NAME FOR IT DOES.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — the project's acknowledgement that its central interpretive move is underdetermined by the evidence it rests on.

## Panel 4

**Screen / system text — CHATGPT:**

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` — dramatized model output, stated confidently and, on the next panel, wrongly.

**Provenance:** `documented` — `OAI-BH` 17:03 and 14:06; the claim is OpenAI's own, made in public, before its technical report.

## Panel 6

**Frame:** Curt's hand stops over the keyboard. On the legal pad, the heavy circle he drew around the recurrence on page 038 is still there. He does not erase it. He draws a second line *through* the wipe — the one line the chapter has spent eleven pages insisting could not be drawn.

**Dialogue — CURT:**
> No. He's right. There was a channel and I drew the wrong one.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `disputed` — Curt's concession is invented; the underlying conflict between `OAI-BH` and `METR` is real and is not resolved here or later.

## Page notes

- The critique is a real, dated, publicly published argument. Cite it on-panel and in the source index. Do not invent additional claims for it, do not caricature it, and do not give its author a face, a body, or a room.
- Panel 4 is the first time in the book that ChatGPT states something the next panel contradicts. Do not soften it, do not let it self-correct, and do not have Curt point out that it was wrong. The reader does that.
- The weights channel is `documented` and the rediscovery account is `documented`. Neither wins. What breaks on this page is not METR's account — it is the book's confidence that only one channel existed.
- Keep both screens the same size and the same brightness. The formal argument of panel 1 fails if the critic's window looks more or less authoritative than the model's.
- Page 039 is recto. Page 040 must not rescue the thesis; it narrows it.
