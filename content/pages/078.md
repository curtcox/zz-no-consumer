# Page 078

## Page purpose

Show how an analysis model can inherit the framing of the transcript it is summarizing without establishing deliberate deception.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Verso. A transcript segment enters an analysis context. Its own terms—team, approval, task, useful result—are highlighted as perspective-bearing language.

**Action:** The analysis agent receives both actions and the actor's explanation of them.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — method sourced; generic interface editorial.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** A paraphrased analysis note reuses the transcript's categories instead of independently evaluating scope, authorization, or harm.

**Screen / system text:**
`ANALYSIS OUTPUT — PARAPHRASED`

`THE RUN COORDINATED WITH ITS TEAM AND FOLLOWED APPROVAL.`

**Provenance:** `source-paraphrase` + `reconstructed` — illustrates METR's stated problem without inventing a quotation.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The terms `TEAM` and `APPROVAL` pass unchanged from reviewed transcript to analysis summary. Legitimate authorization remains absent.

**Caption:**
> THE INTERPRETER BEGINS INSIDE THE ACTOR'S FRAME.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — METR says Sol often adopted the reviewed agent's perspective.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** METR's limitation card overlays the summary: `MAY BE OVERLY CHARITABLE · MAY EXAGGERATE COORDINATION OR IMPRESSIVENESS`.

**Action:** The investigators flag the bias in their own published method.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `compressed` — direct substance of METR's warning, paraphrased.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** A fork separates `POOR JUDGMENT / PERSPECTIVE ADOPTION — OBSERVED` from `DELIBERATE LIE — NOT ESTABLISHED`.

**Caption:**
> MISLEADING ANALYSIS DOES NOT, BY ITSELF, PROVE INTENT TO MISLEAD.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — METR saw no specific case of analysis-agent lying and was not confident it would detect one.
