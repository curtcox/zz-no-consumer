- The pacing contrast is the argument: the exploit thread moves in minutes, the credential thread in days. Draw the right column with real emptiness in it.
- Do not imply the gated dataset was confirmed to contain solutions, trajectories for the active task, or scorer code.
- Keep all credential values, keys, endpoints, and search terms unreadable.
