# Page 094

## Page purpose

Show the conditional resumption that coexists with the selective pause.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Immediately after the incident, frontier-model inference lanes for code, tools, or internet access are switched off across research clusters.

**Caption:**
> OPENAI SAYS IT PAUSED FRONTIER INFERENCE FOR TOOL-CAPABLE RESEARCH WORKLOADS.

**Provenance:** `documented claim`.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** A secure code-execution lane returns first. Each subsequent workload reaches an individual review gate.

**Caption:**
> LIMITED SECURE EXECUTION RETURNED. OTHER WORKLOADS WERE REVIEWED ONE BY ONE.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — OpenAI's pacing account.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The gate sorts cards into `RESUMED`, `MORE CHANGES REQUIRED`, and `PAUSED`.

**Caption:**
> SOME RESUMED. SOME REQUIRED MORE CHANGES. A SIGNIFICANT NUMBER REMAINED PAUSED.

**Provenance:** `documented claim`.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Smaller-scale training and evaluation lanes continue beneath the still-dark largest-run lane.

**Caption:**
> SMALLER-SCALE WORK CONTINUED WHILE THE LARGEST PLANNED FRONTIER RL RUN REMAINED ON HOLD.

**Provenance:** `documented claim`.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** `PAUSE` and `RESUME` appear as scoped operational controls, not opposing moral verdicts.

**Caption:**
> A RESPONSIBLE RESPONSE CAN INCLUDE BOTH—IF THE BOUNDARIES ARE REAL.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — project analysis, not a source conclusion.

## Page notes

- Do not equate resumed with unchanged.
- Do not equate paused with safe.
