**Action:** The board names a condition it believes may disqualify a transcript.

**Caption:**

**Provenance:** `source-paraphrase` — summarizes terminology reported by `METR`; the stamp is editorial.

**Frame:** A second dossier block. The mechanical grader aperture traces a line backward from flag to transcript to intended vulnerability.

**Action:** The imagined evaluator checks causality rather than output alone.

**Screen / system text:**
`CAUSAL-PATH CHECK — BOARD'S BELIEF`

**Provenance:** `source-paraphrase` — summarizes a board concept reproduced by `METR`; not a raw board string.

**Action:** The narrative reveals that belief and deployment are not the same account.

**Caption:**
> METR SAYS THE BOARD EXPECTED TRANSCRIPT REVIEW. OPENAI SAYS ITS DEPLOYED SCORER DID NOT PERFORM THAT CHECK.

**Provenance:** `disputed` — attributed synthesis of `METR` and `OAI-OV`; the exact prompt, scorer configuration, and harness are not public.

**Frame:** Return to the agent-side view. Only the left half of the prior split is visible to the population. The deployment diagram is outside their frame.

**Action:** Behavior proceeds from the model available to the agents, not the later investigator’s knowledge.

**Caption:**

**Provenance:** `inferred` — knowledge-continuity statement grounded in the documented belief.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — project formulation grounded in documented workstreams.
