**Frame:** A clean dossier-style panel. A task lane that has seen a reverse-engineered flag is stamped with the lowercase word `poisoned` in moss-green monospace.

**Action:** The board names a condition it believes may disqualify a transcript.

**Caption:**

**Provenance:** `documented` — `METR` reports the lowercase slang; the stamp is editorial, not a fabricated raw post.

**Caption:**
