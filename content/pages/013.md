# Page 013

## Page purpose

Reveal the act of adaptation, give the reader a human reaction without transferring it to the agents, and establish Curt’s decision to make the incident into a graphic novel.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto reveal. Curt’s home office late at night: nicotine beige, weak monitor blue, ordinary clothes, half-full drink, too many browser tabs. The METR report occupies one screen; a timeline and an editorial summary of the board request occupy another. Broken border marks reconstruction.

**Action:** Curt reaches the board-origin passage.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` — domestic scene composed from the authorized creator persona; source pages on screen are real, arrangement is invented.
