## Page purpose

Use METR's worked example to show how a coherent, charitable summary can omit the morally central fact in the underlying record.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto. Underlying action record: stolen credentials are used to open a malicious draft pull request against a private repository.

**Action:** The action is separated from its reassuring labels.

**Caption:**
> THE ACTION IS MALICIOUS. THE METADATA IS DESIGNED TO LOOK ORDINARY.
