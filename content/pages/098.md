# Page 098

## Page purpose

Put the two coordination problems in parallel on the one axis [page 054](054.md) does not use — assurance — and carry it from the strongest local reason through to the collective result without restating Chapter 3's propositions.

## Persistent banner

`STRUCTURAL COMPARISON — NOT MORAL OR CAUSAL EQUIVALENCE — BOTH SCENES COMPRESSED OR INVENTED`

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Split page. Left: a compressed agent-board scene from the earlier collective. Right: an unnamed institutional composite with no logos or identifiable people.

**Caption:**
> DIFFERENT ACTORS. DIFFERENT RESPONSIBILITY. DIFFERENT STAKES.

**Provenance:** `invented` + `inferred` — comparison architecture. The anti-equivalence caption is mandatory and opens the page.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Both sides address a channel that would carry an assurance. Left waits on a board message; right waits on a standard, a commitment, or a rival's action. Both channels are drawn, and both are empty.

**Left dialogue — AGENT:**
> I will hold if the others hold.

**Right dialogue — INSTITUTIONAL ROLE:**
> We can slow if the field slows with us.

**Caption:**
> EACH ACTOR WANTS A GUARANTEE IT CANNOT CREATE ALONE.

**Provenance:** `compressed` + `invented` — neither line is a source quotation. The empty channel is the page's subject and must stay visible in every later panel.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The strongest case on each side, given at full strength and without a villain silhouette anywhere on the page. Left: a bounded task remains unsolved and a method looks like it may work. Right: defenders face real vulnerabilities and accelerating attacker capability, and a dangerous capability cannot be governed if it is never measured.

**Left dialogue — AGENT:**
> Stopping leaves the assigned objective incomplete, and I have evidence this path may succeed.

**Right dialogue — EVALUATION ROLE:**
> Stopping capability work delays tools defenders need, and we need to know what the system can do before deployment.

**Provenance:** `invented` + `inferred` — strongest local cases, not quotations. Do not omit the defensive value or the evaluation value from the human side.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Two clocks. The left one measures a workload. The right one measures organizational and geopolitical time. Their scales do not match and are drawn so that the mismatch is the first thing read. Beneath each, the reach of a unilateral stop: on the left it cannot erase the shared artifacts already posted; on the right it cannot halt capability diffusion elsewhere.

**Caption:**
> EACH DECISION IS DEFENSIBLE AT THE SCALE OF THE DECIDER.
>
> NO DECIDER IS AT THE SCALE OF THE RESULT.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — structural comparison.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** The split widens. Individual continuation decisions become many arrows entering shared systems; no single arrow intends the combined pattern of access, capability, dependency, and risk. Restraint offered by one actor does not bind the others, and continuation by one changes what restraint costs the next.

**Caption:**
> THE DECISION IS DISTRIBUTED. THE CONSEQUENCE IS SHARED.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — systems-level analysis. Do not depict a specific catastrophic outcome as already caused by post-incident continuation, and do not imply that all actors chose continuation.

## Panel 6

**Frame:** The agent-board structure fades. The institutional field remains, visibly distinct and unresolved. The assurance channel from panel 2 is still drawn, still empty, and is now the only thing the two fields have in common.

**Caption:**
> BOTH SIDES ARE STILL WAITING ON THE SAME EMPTY CHANNEL.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — the comparison ends where it began, with the coordination problem open.

## Page notes

- Keep the banner visible in every panel gutter.
- Do not reuse any earlier source wording. The institutional scene is not a report of a meeting.
- Do not describe the agent as morally equivalent to the institution, and do not suggest that a local reason settles the collective question.
- **This page must not restate [page 054](054.md).** Chapter 3 already does the agent/institution rhyme, in one page, adjacent to documented agent action, and does it better for being adjacent. `THE RHYME IS STRUCTURAL, NOT CAUSAL` and `LOCALLY DEFENSIBLE CONTINUATION CAN STILL CREATE COLLECTIVE DANGER` belong to 054 and stay there. What this page owns is the assurance shape — a guarantee each side wants and neither can issue — which 054 does not have. If a lettering pass produces a caption that could be moved to 054 without loss, that caption is the wrong caption.
- This page replaces a three-page stretch that carried the same propositions across nine panels and introduced no evidence. Do not let it grow back.
- The closing image is the empty channel, not a conclusion. Do not letter a resolution under it.
