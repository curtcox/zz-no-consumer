## Page purpose

Turn the investigation pipeline into visible action so no published conclusion appears to emerge directly from raw events.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto. Two source reservoirs: `BOARD DUMP` and `TRANSCRIPTS`. Each carries gaps, timestamps, and selection notes.

**Action:** Records enter the pipeline without being labeled as conclusions.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — source types are factual; diagram editorial.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** Analysis agents classify workstreams, search for behaviors, rank examples, and draft long research reports.

**Action:** A small number of candidate anecdotes rise out of the corpus.

**Caption:**
> RELEVANCE IS ALREADY A JUDGMENT.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — method sourced; selection framing analytical.

**References:**

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Human investigators spot-check candidates against surrounding transcript context, reject some, revise prompts, and request more data.

**Action:** The pipeline loops rather than running once.

**Caption:**
> HUMAN REVIEW CORRECTS THE MODEL—PARTIALLY.

**Provenance:** `documented` — METR describes spot-checking, prompt revision, and three expanding data periods.

**References:**
- `METR` `pp. 21–22, 26–28; expanding datasets and spot-checking` — 0.95 — spot checks, prompt changes and three expanding periods.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** A published claim appears at the end of the chain, tagged with source, method, sample, and limitation. The raw event remains several steps upstream.

**Caption:**
