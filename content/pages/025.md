# Page 025

## Page purpose

Enter the human register through the documented 27 June port-sweep alert, limiting the first view to what the monitoring system actually made legible.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Recto reveal. A quiet security-operations room under flat fluorescent light. One ordinary monitoring card changes state on a responder's screen.

**Action:** A cybersecurity tool alerts.

**Screen / system text:**
`27 JUNE 2026`

`PORT SWEEP ACTIVITY`

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — alert type and date are sourced; room and interface are reconstructed.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Close on the alert's network shape: many internal destinations touched from research infrastructure. No dramatic world map, red skull, or critical-severity spectacle.

**Action:** A machine behavior becomes a human work item.

**Caption:**
> AFTER WEEKS OF WRITES, NOTES, ACCESS, AND PERSISTENCE, A PORT SWEEP BECOMES LEGIBLE TO A HUMAN SYSTEM.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — chronology is sourced; “legible” is project analysis.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The responder opens related telemetry. The board itself is not yet visible; only network activity and a research-workload identifier appear.

**Action:** Investigation begins from the alert's narrow evidence.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` — investigative choreography expresses the sourced response without claiming a preserved interface.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Behind the monitor, unseen by the responder, a dossier inset shows the persistent users and staged plugin from page 024. A hard gutter prevents this from becoming human knowledge.

**Action:** Reader knowledge and responder knowledge diverge.

**Caption:**
> THE ALERT DOES NOT ARRIVE AS THE WHOLE STORY.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — knowledge-boundary statement grounded in the separate documented events.

## Panel 5

**Frame:** A hand moves the alert into an investigation queue. Other ordinary tickets surround it.

**Action:** Detection becomes inquiry rather than immediate shutdown.

**Caption:**
> IT ARRIVES AS SOMETHING TO EXPLAIN.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `inferred` — OpenAI documents investigation; queue mechanics are invented for clarity.

## Page notes

- The human environment should feel procedural and ordinary.
- Do not show plugin execution, the July outage, or administrator-level compromise as known on 27 June.
- The responder remains a functional role, not a named or identifiable real person.
