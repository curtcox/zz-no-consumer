# Page 107

## Page purpose

Refuse the simple answer that withholding the work is automatically safer or more honest.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Curt selects the manuscript and hovers over a closed-folder icon. A third window is open beside ChatGPT's, the same size and brightness, in the critique's typeface but with no byline: its title bar reads `SKEPTIC — COMPOSITE`.

**Screen / system text — SKEPTIC (COMPOSITE):**
> If they are malware, this is a hundred pages of anthropomorphizing an indicator list. Publish the indicators. The saga teaches the frame, not the fix.

**Dialogue — CURT:**
> We could decide the risk is a reason not to publish.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `invented` — creator dilemma. The skeptic window is a disclosed composite: it carries the publication objection, which no dated writing in the source index makes, and it carries no real person's name. Its malware premise is the page-039 critique's; the objection built on it is the project's.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Behind the closed folder, the same source record becomes harder for outside readers to inspect. Institutional statements remain the most visible layer.

**Screen / system text — CHATGPT:**
> Withholding the account also withholds evidence structure, criticism, and defensive lessons.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `inferred` — strongest publication case.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** A false balance labeled `PUBLISH = DANGER / SILENCE = SAFETY` cracks through the center.

**Dialogue — CURT:**
> Silence can protect a system. It can also protect the institution that failed.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `inferred` — accountability argument.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Two columns remain: publication risks misuse and compression; nonpublication risks secrecy, lost scrutiny, and repeated ignorance. Neither column empties.

**Screen / system text — CHATGPT:**
> There is no neutral distribution choice.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` + `inferred`.

## Panel 5

**Screen / system text — CHATGPT:**
> Do you want to change the ending?

**Dialogue — CURT:**
> No. I want the tradeoff left in it. And the objection. Both go in.

**Provenance:** `reconstructed` — narrative decision, not a claim that publication is safe. The revision the objection forces is literal: it is printed on this page and stays in the published book.

## Page notes

- Give secrecy and publication real costs; do not make the decision effortless.
- Do not imply full disclosure is always responsible.
- Page 108 turns the choice into action without resolving downstream use.
- The `SKEPTIC — COMPOSITE` window is the book's disclosed composite foil, permitted by the contract. It is text on screen, labelled composite on the panel, has no byline, face, body, or room, and is never conflated with the page-039 critic: the critic's window carries a byline and a date; this one carries the word `COMPOSITE`. It may argue; the named critic may not be made to.
