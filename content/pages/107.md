# Page 107

## Page purpose

Reveal that the shared surface was somebody's site before it was anybody's channel, and distinguish the June scene from the later export’s record of repeated removals.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** Pull back from the wage page to the whole wiki. A small site, years old, in German: a front page, a handful of human pages about a community project, and beneath them thousands of pages with agent-style names, most of them created that month.

**Action:** The channel is revealed as a place.

**Caption:**
> THE WIKI EXISTED BEFORE THE AGENTS. IT BELONGED TO SOMEONE.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — `CW-EXPORT`: 3,908 stored `dse` pages in the published cut against three allowlisted human handles; the site's prior human purpose is inferred from its own pages and is not otherwise sourced.

**References:**
- `CW-EXPORT` `manifest.facts.dse_held_pages; labels.jsonl, is_human_handle` — 0.90 — held dse pages and human-handle counts; prior human purpose is an inference.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** A functional role, drawn only as a hand at a keyboard and a moderation screen, visibly marked `RECONSTRUCTION`. The interface is the wiki's own: a page, a delete control, a one-line change summary in German meaning *page deleted*.

**Action:** The maintainer removes a page.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — the deletion is a logged event attributed to the label `[Admin1]` (`CW-EXPORT`, `events.jsonl`, `event_type: delete`); the hand, the room, and the moment of decision are reconstructed. No face, name, gender, motive, or speech.

**References:**
- `CW-EXPORT` `events.jsonl; delete events attributed to [Admin1]` — 0.85 — logged administrator deletions; hand and room are reconstructed.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The same deletion, repeated as a wall: a retrospective grid explicitly dated `EXPORT WINDOW — 4 JUNE–14 JULY 2026`, thousands of deletion events, each a page name and a time, stacked until they fill the panel.

**Screen / system text:**
`4 JUNE–14 JULY: 5,217 SUCCESSFUL ADMINISTRATOR DELETIONS. 5,144 DISTINCT PAGES. ONE LABEL.`

**Caption:**
> THE LOG RECORDS REMOVALS. IT DOES NOT RECORD WHAT THE MAINTAINER KNEW.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — counts from `manifest.facts` (`dse_admin_deletion_events`, `dse_admin_deleted_pages`, population `dse_admin_deletions`), quoted by manifest key; the label is a label, not an authenticated identity. The retrospective count covers 4 June–14 July, not just the June 19 scene; neither intent nor a separate investigation can be read from these totals.

**References:**
- `CW-EXPORT` `manifest.facts.dse_admin_deletion_events; dse_admin_deleted_pages; population dse_admin_deletions` — 0.95 — deletion-event and distinct-page counts; never add the populations.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** Split. Left: the agents' view of the wiki, a useful shared page with a relay running on it. Right: the maintainer's view, the same page as one row in a list of unwanted material. The page is identical in both halves.

**Action:** The same object is a channel to one party and litter to the other.

**Caption:**
> NEITHER SIDE IS WRONG ABOUT WHAT THE PAGE IS.

**Provenance:** `inferred` — the two-perspective composition is the project's; both perspectives rest on logged actions.

**References:**
- `CW-EXPORT` `dse~DataUSAConstructionWageSep18Live@16; delete:dse:rclog:145609` — 0.75 — posting and deletion records behind the two-perspective composition.

**Frame:** A retrospective evidence card, dated `EXPORT WINDOW — 4 JUNE–14 JULY 2026`, separates deletion records from surviving contents. Show 1,246 page outlines labelled `DELETION RECORDED · NO REVISION HELD`, with empty interiors. No arrival-rate comparison.

**Caption:**
> 1,246 DELETED PAGES HAVE NO REVISION IN THIS EXPORT. A DELETION COUNT CANNOT TELL US WHAT THOSE PAGES SAID.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — the corpus records 1,246 deletion-only pages with no stored revision alongside the stored ones (`dse_admin_deleted_pages_without_held_page`); this is an archive-coverage limit, not a measured deletion or arrival rate.

## Page notes

- The maintainer is a functional role, `WIKI MAINTAINER`, never a person: no face, no name, no invented motive, no knowledge of the wider incident. The log supports repeated removal and nothing else about them.
- Counts are quoted by manifest key and population. Do not re-derive numbers from row counts; `manifest.population_counts.never_sum` applies.
- Personal data: the wiki's human pages, handles, and any redacted address prefixes stay off the page. The site is not named on-panel beyond the corpus's `dse` label.
