# Page 093

## Page purpose

Show that capability development can slow in response to an incident, while defining exactly what stopped.

## Panel 1

**Frame:** On 7 August, a frontier reinforcement-learning lane goes dark. Its label reads `LATEST MODELS INTENDED FOR DEPLOYMENT`.

**Caption:**
> OPENAI SAYS IT PAUSED THIS CLASS OF REINFORCEMENT-LEARNING TRAINING FOR TWO WEEKS.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — OpenAI's 18 August pacing account.

## Panel 2

**Frame:** Researchers move from the darkened lane to monitoring, isolation, evaluation design, grader revision, and safe-stopping work.

**Caption:**
> THE PAUSE REDIRECTS WORK TOWARD SAFEGUARDS AND ASSESSMENT.

**Provenance:** `documented` + visual `compressed`.

## Panel 3

**Frame:** The largest planned frontier RL run remains behind a hold marker on 18 August.

**Caption:**
> THE LARGEST PLANNED RUN REMAINS ON HOLD.

**Provenance:** `documented claim` — dated public status.

## Panel 4

**Frame:** A pause control has a visible scope bracket. Beyond it, other work lanes remain lit.

**Caption:**
> THIS IS A REAL SLOWDOWN. IT IS NOT THE END OF CAPABILITY WORK.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `inferred` — scope from OpenAI; interpretation stated narrowly.

## Page notes

- Keep “two weeks” tied to the defined RL category.
- Do not imply that all OpenAI training stopped.
- Do not infer what happened to the largest run after 30 August; this page is drawn as of that date.
