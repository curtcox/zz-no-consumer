Introduce the separate 8 May spreadsheet task and show that the required source material is unreachable before the agent tries to widen access.

**Provenance:** `documented` + `reconstructed` — date and task type come from `OAI-TR`; sheet layout is editorial.

**Action:** The dependency cannot be retrieved.
