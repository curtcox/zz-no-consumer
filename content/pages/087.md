# Page 087

## Page purpose

Locate what the three pipelines actually share. Not access, which [page 085](085.md) already
separated, and not reliability. Each one accepted a layer of machine indirection under time
pressure, knowing what it cost, because the alternative was not doing the work.

## Page notes

- **What this page adds to [page 085](085.md) is that the indirection was chosen.** The book has
  stated the tradeoff as a condition since [page 075](075.md) — the corpus is too large, the window is
  six days, without the model the investigation does not happen. It has never said that
  somebody weighed it and decided. [Page 080](080.md) panel 5 letters both halves of the cost and
  stops short of the decision; this page is the decision.
- **Do not let the shared condition become absolution.** [Page 085](085.md)'s non-equivalence rule is
  not softened here and the qualification in panel 5 is mandatory. A shared structure across
  three unequal parties is a finding about the structure, not a levelling of the parties.
- Panel 2 is the only place in the book where a model builds the instrument rather than
  operating it. Keep the two layers visually distinct: [page 076](076.md)'s analysis tree is the model
  reading; this is the model making the thing that reads.
- Beth Barnes appears as text on a screen with a byline and a date, under the same rule as
  the [page-039](039.md) critic. No face, no body, no room, no invented dialogue, and nothing beyond
  what the dated writing says. Being cited is not endorsement.
- Cotra's paraphrase in panel 2 is bounded to her account of her own investigation's method.
  `HF-POD` also carries her sharper personal claims; none of them belongs on this page.
- The third column is narrower than the other two on purpose and must stay that way. It has
  the least access, the least standing, and the smallest consequence of the three, and the
  layout is where that shows.
- [Page 088](088.md) is the book's central disclosure and needs the reader to arrive with all three
  instruments in view. Do not add a landing beat here; this page hands over on a turn across
  the leaf.
