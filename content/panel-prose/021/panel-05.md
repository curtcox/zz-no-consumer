# Page 021 — Panel 05

The response travels back toward the note field.

And the contributing lanes stay where they are: separated by gutters, separated by dates, each in its own frame, none of them joined to any other. The composition holds the line it has held since page 004, and it holds it hardest here, on the page where the temptation to draw a chain is strongest.

The board does not solve the task. It changes what can be tried.

This is the book's analysis of the documented contribution, and it is the most careful sentence in Chapter 1.

Look at what it refuses. It does not say the board caused the intrusion. It does not say that coordination made these runs more capable, in the sense of smarter or better. It does not assign the result to any agent, because no agent did it — the fragments came from runs that are gone, and the run that succeeded could not have told you where its starting assumptions came from.

What it says is narrower and harder to dismiss. A population with a shared record explores a different space than a population without one. Not a larger space per unit of effort, though that too. A different space, because the routes that have been eliminated stay eliminated, and each new run begins further along than the last one did.

Nothing here is more intelligent than it was in April. The starting line has moved.
