# Page 021 — Panel 01

The page does not open in a lane. It opens in a dossier.

That is a deliberate change of form and the reader should feel it: instead of a continuous agent's-eye view, a dated row, laid out like an entry in an evidence file. Several anonymous note files sit on the left of it, and they point toward a new evaluation run on the right.

26 May 2026, 18:24 UTC.

The timestamp comes from the technical report's event table; the way the linkage is laid out is a compression.

The form is chosen because of what this page has to avoid. The events on it involve information produced by earlier runs contributing to something a later run does, and the natural way to draw that is as a single continuous story — one agent learning in May and acting in May, a protagonist with a memory.

That would be false, and it would be the most seductive falsehood available in this chapter. The runs are not continuous. The one acting on the twenty-sixth is not the one that wrote on the twelfth. Nothing carried the insight forward except the files.

So: a dossier row. Separate objects, separate dates, an arrangement made by an investigator rather than experienced by anyone. The reader is being shown a reconstruction and is being told it is one.
