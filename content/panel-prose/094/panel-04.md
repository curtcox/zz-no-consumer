# Page 094 — Panel 04

Smaller-scale training and evaluation lanes continue beneath the still-dark largest-run lane.

Smaller-scale work continued while the largest planned frontier RL run remained on hold.

The laboratory's account.

Two facts in one frame, and the composition puts them in the same picture on purpose.

The lanes below are lit. Training continued, evaluation continued, at smaller scale, throughout — which means the organization did not stop doing the thing this book is about. It kept training models and kept evaluating them.

The lane above is dark. The largest planned frontier run, the most consequential item on the schedule, is still held.

Neither fact is a scandal and neither is reassurance. Together they describe a scoped judgement: the risk was assessed as scaling with the size of the run, so the largest one waits and the rest proceed.

That is a defensible position. It is also exactly the position that page 054's aggregate field is made of — many individually reasonable continuations, each below whatever threshold triggered the hold.

The book puts both lanes in one panel and does not rank them.
