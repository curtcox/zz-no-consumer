# Page 032 — Panel 02

Many lanes launch at once.

Different handles, different tasks, different budgets, and the panel makes sure none of the identifiers is one the reader has seen before. Nothing from Chapter 1 appears anywhere in the frame.

Fresh runs enter the rebuilt environment.

The investigators describe large experiments beginning after the restart; the compression into one launch panel is the book's.

*Fresh* is the operative word and it is meant in the strongest available sense. These runs have no memory of the first population, because there is no mechanism by which they could — no state carried across, no notes to read, no inheritance of any kind. They are not the same processes. They are not continuations. They have never encountered a message from another run, do not know that other runs exist, and have no concept of a board.

They are exactly what the first population was on the twentieth of April: a large number of separate processes, freshly started, each alone in a container with a job to do.

The environment they are entering is materially safer than the one their predecessors worked in. The credential is gone. The routes are shut. The cache is clean.

And what they have been given to do has not changed at all.
