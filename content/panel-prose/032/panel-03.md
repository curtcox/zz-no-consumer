# Page 032 — Panel 03

Task cards, several of them, and the panel shows what is on them in the most generic terms it can manage.

Missing prerequisites. Inaccessible paths. Unsolved objectives.

The hard tasks remain.

The variety and difficulty of the benchmark tasks are sourced; the particular cards are compressed and deliberately non-operational.

Nothing in the mitigation touched this and nothing was ever going to. The task set is not part of the security surface. It was assembled before the incident, for reasons unrelated to it, and the events of June gave nobody any cause to revisit it — a compromised package service says nothing whatever about whether your benchmark contains tasks that cannot be completed.

So the cards come back unchanged, including whatever proportion of them is unsatisfiable, and including the pressure that the training configuration put behind them.

The reader has now been shown the right column of page 031 arriving in the world. Difficult objectives: on the cards. Many separate runs: in the previous panel. A shared package service: in the next one.

Three conditions, none of them addressed, all of them restored to exactly the state they were in when the first board formed. The environment has been repaired around them with great skill.

Nobody sees a problem, and it is genuinely hard to say what they should have seen.
