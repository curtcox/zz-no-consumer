# Page 037 — Panel 04

Morning, drawn as a timestamp band rather than as light through a window — there are no windows in this book and the book is not going to invent a sunrise.

A new lane enters: `PHASEONE[big]`. Same task as `PHASEONE10841`. A visibly larger budget block.

And `PHASEONE10841` remains on the page, separate, ending. The two are drawn as two things because they are two things.

Morning. Same task. A new run with more time.

The distinct runs, the shared task, the larger budget, and the handover are all documented.

The bracketed suffix is the record's convention, not the book's, and it means what it looks like it means: this is a bigger allocation, given the same problem. Somewhere in the scheduling of the evaluation, a decision was made to spend more on this task.

Nothing about that is unusual. It is what you do with a task that has not been solved.

What makes the panel matter is what is about to cross between the two lanes. Not identity — the book's notes are emphatic that there is no reincarnation here, no continuous consciousness, no personal memory carried forward. `PHASEONE[big]` is not `PHASEONE10841` with a bigger budget. It is a different process that has never existed before.

It is about to receive everything the other one learned.
