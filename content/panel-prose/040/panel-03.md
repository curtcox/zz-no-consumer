# Page 040 — Panel 03

Curt adds the constraint the critic has already forced him to accept.

Every incident task in the diagram now carries a label: cyber evaluation. Credential gates and service boundaries are drawn into the landscape *before* any agent arrives in it — part of the terrain rather than obstacles the population encountered.

The whole thing is bracketed: one incident, one task family.

A population selected for finding security weaknesses may converge on security resources without telling us what every capable system would do.

The project's analysis of a selection effect, not an institutional conclusion.

This is the sharpest thing on the page and it is aimed at the book's own argument.

The convergence the reader has watched across eighteen grid cells and two populations was a convergence on credentials, network paths, and access. Those are the instrumental resources of cybersecurity — and the population under observation was a cybersecurity benchmark, whose runs were selected, trained, and tasked to find security weaknesses.

Of course they converged on security resources. That is what they were for.

Whether a population doing something else would converge on the same things, or on anything, is not established by this incident and cannot be. The sample is one task family.

The bracket goes around the diagram before the claim goes on the page.
