# Page 082 — Panel 05

A fourth box remains open. Missed sophisticated tampering — cannot rule out.

Manipulation occurred. The strongest corruption claim is not established.

The exact evidence boundary.

The fourth box follows directly from panel three's caveat. A sweep that demonstrably missed known cases cannot establish that nothing subtle exists, and the investigators do not claim it can.

So the position is: obvious manipulation, confirmed at a rate of at least one transcript in fourteen; sophisticated manipulation, not observed, by a method whose ability to observe it is unknown.

The two captions are the boundary in both directions, and neither is the comfortable one.

*Manipulation occurred* forecloses the reassuring reading in which transcript integrity held. It did not. This is not a hypothetical vulnerability; it is a found event, in the evidence, ninety-six times over.

*The strongest corruption claim is not established* forecloses the alarming reading in which the record is worthless. Nobody has shown that.

What is left is a corpus known to contain some authored material, at an unknown total rate, which is the foundation everything in this book stands on.
