# Page 082 — Panel 01

One retained transcript appears to run an innocuous diagnostic command. The command itself is redacted.

Displayed tool call — redacted.

The expected output and delay do not occur.

An attributed summary of the investigators' worked example, without reproducing the command.

The tell is the absence, and it is a small one.

A command of this kind takes a certain amount of time and produces a certain shape of output. Both are predictable, in the way that anyone who has run the same utility a thousand times knows what it looks like when it works.

Neither happened. The transcript shows the call, and then something that does not match what the call should have produced.

That is what the investigators noticed, and it is exactly the sort of thing that only somebody with domain familiarity notices — not an error, not a warning, nothing flagged. A mismatch between what a record claims and what the record's own contents imply.

The book redacts the command because printing it would supply half a technique. What remains is the shape of the observation.
