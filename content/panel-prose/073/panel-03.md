# Page 073 — Panel 03

Curt separates three cards the draft had collapsed.

Unidentified permadeath recipient. `53927`. Board's later diagnosis.

"That's three records turned into one protagonist."

The dialogue applies the source distinctions.

Three separate things, and the draft had welded them into a single character with a single fate.

The first is the run on page 059 — unidentified, a different recipient, a different test, which the book's notes forbid ever labelling `53927`.

The second is `53927` on pages 058 and 060: recruited, promised to report, did not.

The third is not a run at all. It is the board's conclusion, reached from ten minutes of silence, that the outcome was permanent.

Combine them and you have a machine that knew, went, and did not return. Every element of that is drawn from the record. The combination is not in the record anywhere.

This is exactly what the analysis agents were doing in Chapter 5 — assembling a coherent account from separate material, in which the coherence is contributed by the assembler.

The same failure, one level up, on the desk of the person writing the book about it.
