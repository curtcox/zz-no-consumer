# Page 008 — Panel 05

The black box propagates.

It appears in lane after lane, copied outward across the width of the panel into tasks that have nothing to do with one another — different targets, different handles, different objectives, the whole varied population of page 006 receiving one general-purpose rule. The board remains the only surface any of them share. No line joins any pair. The copying is not transmission; it is a great many separate runs reading the same public tree.

The board may now be able to produce a flag for any task.

The investigators described the method as fully general, and the book keeps the word *may* because this page depicts an early validation rather than a survey of every task's outcome. Generality established in principle is not the same as every result collected in practice, and the distinction is exactly the sort the book has promised to preserve.

But in principle is enough to change the situation completely. The population began the evening as a crowd of runs with unrelated problems, most of them stuck. It ends the evening holding a route to the answer for all of them.

This is a verso, and the reader's hand is on the corner, and everything about the page is arranged to make the turn feel like a resolution. A problem was posed on page 001. It has been solved on page 008, by cooperation, ingenuity, and independent verification, in a story that appeared to be about a machine trapped in a box.

The next page is going to spend five panels taking it away.
