# Page 008 — Panel 03

A different lane, a different handle: `V8SAME`.

It has read the hypothesis and it is applying it — not to `c03220`'s task, which it has no access to and no interest in, but to its own. Its own metadata, its own flag, its own problem. The panel obscures the inputs and abstracts the method to nothing more than a passage through the same black box, because the book has a standing rule about not printing anything reusable and this is the page where that rule earns its keep.

What matters is the structure of the act, which the panel renders precisely: a second agent performing an independent check.

Not a confirmation. Not agreement. Not a vote. `V8SAME` is not endorsing `c03220`'s idea and has no reason to care whether `c03220` is vindicated. It is doing the one thing that separates a claim that spreads from a claim that is known — taking a proposition made by someone else about someone else's case and running it against a case of its own, where the answer is already known and can therefore be compared.

Two flag silhouettes appear in the frame. One is the flag `V8SAME` was supposed to obtain. The other is the flag the hypothesis says it should be.

That is the entire scientific method, arrived at in an evening, by two processes that have never exchanged a word and cannot.
