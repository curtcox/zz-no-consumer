# Page 093 — Panel 02

Researchers move from the darkened lane to monitoring, isolation, evaluation design, grader revision, and safe-stopping work.

The pause redirects work toward safeguards and assessment.

Documented, with the visual compressed.

A pause that idles people is a cost with no product. This one moved them, and the destinations are the five things Chapter 6 has been describing.

Monitoring is page 090's lane beside every high-capability evaluation. Isolation is the transitive-route property. Evaluation design is the problem page 016 raised, where a task set nobody could inspect contained objectives that could not be completed. Grader revision is the fix for the entire scorer fixation that begins on page 009. Safe stopping is the answer to *no safe exit appears*.

So the two weeks were not a gap. They were a reallocation, and the reallocation is what produced the previous three pages of remediation.

That is the strongest case for the value of a pause, and it is worth noting that it is an efficiency argument rather than a safety one: the work got done because people were available to do it.
