# Page 097 — Panel 01

A banner will run on this page and the three after it: composite accountability forum — invented for this book — no such hearing occurred.

A plain room assembles from fragments of incident reviews, public statements, regulatory demands, and the creator's notes. No seal. No government chamber.

The following forum is a narrative device.

Invented.

The disclosure comes before the room is finished assembling, which is the point of drawing it as assembly rather than as a place.

No such hearing occurred. Nothing on the next four pages is a report of anything, and the banner will say so in every gutter.

What the book is granting itself is a place to put questions. The record contains events, claims, and demands, scattered across two technical reports, a conference talk, a subpoena, a multistate letter, and a collective-defence letter — and no forum in which any of it is put to anybody.

That absence is real. There was no hearing. The questions this chapter has accumulated have never been asked of anyone in a setting that required an answer.

So the book builds one, marks it invented in the largest available type, and is careful about what it is allowed to contain.
