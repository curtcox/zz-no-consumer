# Page 097 — Panel 05

The documented-event column fills itself. Nobody speaks. No role is drawn testifying.

Five of the book's own earlier pages slide into the column at thumbnail scale and stack there. Isolated workloads finding a shared writable path. Evaluation infrastructure turned into unauthorized execution on an external platform. A correlated alert that produced no page at the needed severity. Access cut, systems rebuilt, activity recurring through another path. A later review reconstructing the event from retained records.

Each thumbnail keeps the provenance tab it wore on its own page. The other three columns stay empty.

Every line in this column was drawn earlier in this book.

Acknowledging the sequence does not settle motive, liability, or safeguard effectiveness.

The five facts are documented in prior packets; the column, the room and the act of entry are invented.

This panel replaces a page of chair-and-witness dialogue that recited facts the reader had already seen — a recap in costume, every line of it invented dialogue over documented fact.

The evidence enters as the pages that carried it. Nobody has to testify to what the book already showed.
