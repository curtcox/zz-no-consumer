# Page 097 — Panel 03

The chair places the chapter's source packet into the center of the room. Not a gavel.

"We will separate what happened, what organizations say they changed, what has been tested, and what remains unknown."

An organizing rule derived from the project truth contract.

Four categories, and they are the truth contract wearing a chair's voice.

*What happened* is documented event — the material that survives sourcing.

*What organizations say they changed* is claim, attributed, which is the status of nearly everything in this chapter's first half.

*What has been tested* is independent verification, and page 091 has already established how little of it exists.

*What remains unknown* is the category most proceedings do not have, and its absence is why most proceedings produce misleading records.

The packet rather than the gavel is the composition's argument. This forum has no authority to compel anything, no power to sanction, and nothing to bang. What it has is the same material the reader has, sorted.

That is all the book can honestly grant itself.
