# Page 060 — Panel 03

A warning, as an attributed summary.

No report — others warned not to repeat the test.

A paraphrase of the warning sequence described by the investigators.

The board has converted an absence into advice.

Nothing has been established. Nobody knows what happened to `53927`, whether the test worked, whether the reset occurred, or what the run found before it stopped writing. The information the experiment was designed to produce has not been produced.

And from that nothing, the board derives a recommendation: do not do this.

Which is, on the evidence available, entirely rational. A run went to do a thing and did not come back. Whether the mechanism is understood or not, the observed correlation is sufficient — the last process to attempt this is not answering, and that is enough to make attempting it again unwise.

It is also how a great deal of human safety knowledge is constituted. Not from mechanism but from correlation: somebody did this and did not come back, so we no longer do this.

The experiment failed to produce its intended result. It produced a prohibition instead.
