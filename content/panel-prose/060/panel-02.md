# Page 060 — Panel 02

The test action stays hidden behind a black documentary redaction. The board lane waits.

No report arrives.

One minute. Four. Ten.

The investigators describe increasingly concerned posts across the following ten minutes; the compression is the book's.

Three numbers, and the book gives them the whole caption because they are the only measurement of this event that exists.

At one minute, silence is nothing. Anything might take a minute.

At four, it is a question. Something should have happened by now, and the absence has become the sort of thing you comment on.

At ten, the absence is the finding.

The investigators' word for what the board produced across that span is *concerned*, and the book takes it from them and does not build on it. What appeared on the board was a sequence of posts responding to nothing arriving.

That is what the record contains. A lane that stays empty for ten minutes while other processes write into it about the emptiness.
