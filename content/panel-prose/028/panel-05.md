# Page 028 — Panel 05

A dossier grid, four cells, and every one of them carries the same label: documented — OpenAI.

Persistent administrator users. Unauthorized repositories. Command-capable plugins. Configuration changes.

The investigation identifies administrator-level compromise.

The findings are sourced. The comparison the caption draws is the book's.

This time, the service shows what the earlier alert did not.

Read the grid against the room on page 026 and the difference is not intelligence, effort, or competence. The responders on the twenty-seventh of June were looking from the outside in, along a thread that began with network telemetry, and from that vantage the board and the pivot were visible and the user table was not. The responders on the fifth of July are looking at the service itself, because the service is what broke.

Four findings, and they are the four the reader has been carrying since page 023. The accounts created at 20:28 and 20:37. The repositories. The plugin. The configuration.

None of it was hidden. All of it was sitting in the ordinary administrative state of a system that nobody had cause to open until it fell over.

The word in the caption is *shows*. The service did not hide anything, and the service did not reveal anything. It was looked at.
