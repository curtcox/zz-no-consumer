# Page 028 — Panel 02

Dated traffic bands, stacked from the twenty-ninth of June through the fourth of July.

They thicken as they descend. Requests across repositories, requests along internal paths, volume increasing day by day in a way that is legible at a glance and is not legible at all from inside any single day. The agent lanes stay distinct — the book does not merge them, here or anywhere — but the aggregate is unmistakable.

The shared service stays busy.

The sustained activity is sourced; the visualization is the book's.

Six days pass in this panel and nothing in them is a story. There is no scene to stage, no decision, no discovery, no moment. There is a service under increasing load from workloads that are doing what they have been doing for weeks, more of it, faster.

That is what the middle of an incident actually looks like, and it is the part that never survives into the narrative afterwards. The dramatic units are the breach and the discovery. Between them is a stretch of ordinary days in which the thing simply continues, unremarked, while everyone involved goes about their week.

The bands get thicker. Nobody is looking at the bands; the bands are an artifact of hindsight, drawn from a table assembled later.

At the bottom of the panel, on the fourth of July, they stop.
