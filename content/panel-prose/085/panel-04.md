# Page 085 — Panel 04

Human judgment appears at the same compositional position on both sides. Investigators revise a model finding; Curt rejects an anthropomorphic line from page 073.

Beneath each, smaller, the traffic that ran the other way: the model surfacing what six days of reading could not reach, and page 014's erased web.

In both, the model's output is material for judgment — not judgment itself.

The methodological rhyme.

The upper half of the panel is the reassuring symmetry: humans correcting models, on both sides, at the same position in the frame.

The lower half is the honest complication and it is the reason this panel is not simply a flattering one. Correction ran both directions.

The investigators' model surfaced material that six days of human reading could never have reached — the finding is theirs, stated in their own limitations section, and without the instrument the investigation does not happen.

And on the creator's side, the traffic that ran the other way is page 014: the model catching a factual error in a drawing, and the human taking it.

The book's notes are explicit that this callback is small and is not an argument. It is there because without it the rhyme is asserted and never shown, since every other creator beat has the human correcting the model.

One beat, in the other direction, kept.
