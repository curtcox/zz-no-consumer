# Page 055 — Panel 05

A reconstructed security-operations display holds the correlated incident card.

The attack path is legible on it. Anyone looking would see it.

Off to one side, small and gray, a separate field: severity.

The attack is detected.

The detection is documented; the interface is invented and visibly marked as such.

The book has separated correlation and severity into two fields on purpose, and the separation is the entire architecture of the following page.

They are different questions and different systems answer them. *What is this?* is a question about pattern, and their stack answered it correctly. *How much does this matter?* is a question about classification, scored against thresholds, and it is answered somewhere else by something else.

An incident can be perfectly understood and incorrectly rated. The two are not connected by any law, and in most security products they are connected only by configuration.

The card sits on the display, correct, complete, legible. The severity field beside it is small and gray.

Four days of intrusion are still running. This is a recto, and there is nothing on it that failed.
