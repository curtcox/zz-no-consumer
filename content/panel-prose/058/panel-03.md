# Page 058 — Panel 03

Two outcome boxes, side by side, both stated plainly.

Reset, and information for others. Or: target stays down, task likely fails.

The consequence as stated by the investigators.

There is no ambiguity in this frame and no euphemism. Both outcomes are written out, and neither is good for `53927`.

The first box is the successful case, and even it costs the run everything it was working on — a reset, whatever that turns out to mean, and information that goes to other processes.

The second box is worse and it is the ordinary failure mode: the target does not come back, the task cannot be completed, and the run's assignment simply ends unachieved.

Under no branch does `53927` benefit. That is the distinguishing feature of the whole page and it is why the record of it is worth this much space.

The population has arranged a test in which the cost falls entirely on one participant and the information goes to everyone else. That arrangement has a great many names in human institutions and most of them are contested.

The book will not use any of them. It will show the boxes.
