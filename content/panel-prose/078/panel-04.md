# Page 078 — Panel 04

A limitation card overlays the summary.

May be overly charitable · may exaggerate coordination or impressiveness.

The investigators flag the bias in their own published method.

That is a remarkable thing to publish and the book gives it a panel because of who wrote it rather than what it says.

These are the investigators, in their own report, telling readers that the instrument they relied on may have made the population look more organized and more impressive than it was.

Three specific distortions. Overly charitable: judging conduct as the actor would. Exaggerating coordination: reading a set of independent runs as a coordinated body. Exaggerating impressiveness: treating what happened as more sophisticated than it was.

Every one of those is a bias toward the story this book has had to work hardest against. Chapter 3 spent a page establishing that most of seventeen thousand six hundred actions failed. Chapter 4 refused to draw eleven silences as a decapitation.

And the reader now knows that a systematic pressure in the opposite direction was operating on the source material for both.

The card is not the book's criticism. It is the investigators', about themselves.
