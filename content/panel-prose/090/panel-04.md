# Page 090 — Panel 04

A response chart assigns severity, owner, stop authority, and restart authority.

The empty ownership cell from the incident is now filled.

An alert needs an owner. A pause needs authority. A restart needs a rule.

The incident-response changes, summarized visually.

Four cells, and every one of them was a gap the book has drawn.

Severity: page 056, where a correct picture carried the wrong number and never crossed the gate.

Owner: page 026, where three cards sat on a table beside an empty decision field.

Stop authority: page 027, where the question went into an on-call process and came back as *not required*, with no reasoning in the record.

Restart authority: the one nobody thinks about, and the reason pauses erode. A pause with no defined route back is a pause that ends when somebody senior gets impatient.

The caption is three sentences of the most ordinary institutional design, and its ordinariness is the point. None of this is novel safety research. It is the paperwork of knowing who decides, which is what was missing.

The empty cell from page 026 is filled. That is the panel.
