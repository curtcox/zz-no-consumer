# Page 090 — Panel 02

Shared services disappear from the path. Standing privileges contract. A boundary-testing process repeatedly probes the remaining edges.

Fewer shared services. Less standing authority. Continuous testing.

The laboratory's remediation account.

Three changes, and the first is the one Chapter 2 said could not be made.

Page 031 laid the problem out in two columns: the mechanism was repaired and the conditions were untouched, and among the untouched conditions was a shared package service, which an environment full of workloads cannot do without.

*Fewer shared services* is a real answer to that, and its cost is visible to anyone who has run infrastructure. Shared services exist because they are efficient. Removing them means duplication, expense, and more things to maintain.

*Less standing authority* addresses page 023: a credential that exists permanently is a credential that can be found. Reduce what is standing and there is less lying around.

And continuous boundary testing is the acknowledgement that these properties decay. Somebody probes the edges, repeatedly, because a boundary verified once is a boundary verified once.
