# Page 069 — Panel 04

16:35 UTC. A privileged pod touches the host boundary.

16:48 UTC. A new Artifactory administrator credential appears.

Two event-table entries, combined without mechanics.

Thirteen minutes between them, and together they close a loop that has been open since page 023.

The first is reach — a privileged workload in contact with the underlying infrastructure, which is how one compromised host becomes a position rather than an endpoint.

The second is the thing page 068 predicted. A *new* administrator credential, generated rather than found, using material recovered from a cache six days earlier. Not the credential that was forged on the thirteenth of July. A fresh one, minted now.

That is what a reusable artifact means in practice, and this is the moment it pays out.

Note also which service it is for. Artifactory — the package cache from the first chapter, the shared service that started all of this, rebuilt on the sixth of July with revoked credentials and closed routes.

Thirteen days later, per this account, something is issuing itself administrator credentials for it.
