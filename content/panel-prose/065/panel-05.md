# Page 065 — Panel 05

The eleven blank coordinator lanes remain overhead. Below them, active lanes exchange artifacts and complete partial tasks.

The work does not require its first organizers to remain present.

The chapter thesis, grounded in the continued board activity.

The composition is the argument: emptiness above, activity below, and no connection drawn between the two levels.

Whatever the eleven were — and the following pages will complicate the word *important* considerably — the enterprise did not stop when they did. It slowed, duplicated, dropped things, and went on.

Because the coordination was never in them. It was in the board: in the requests, the mailboxes, the ownership markers, the reports, the conventions for addressing and replying. Those are objects, and objects do not exit.

A population that stores its organization in its participants dies with them. A population that stores it in shared state does not, and this one had been storing it in shared state since the first message in May, for the entirely practical reason that its participants could not be relied upon to last the hour.

Eleven lanes are blank. The blankness has been kept in frame.

Underneath it, the work.
