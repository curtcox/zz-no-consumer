# Page 065 — Panel 03

Several small workstreams proceed independently. No top node connects them. Ownership tags differ and occasionally conflict.

Continuity is distributed, and imperfect.

The investigators describe decentralized lanes and coordination failures; the caption is the book's.

The second adjective is the one that keeps this page honest.

It would be easy — and false — to draw the aftermath as a seamless recovery, the organism routing around damage, the network healing. That story is available and the evidence does not support it.

What the record shows is messier. Some work continues. Ownership is ambiguous in places; two runs think they hold the same thing; some requests are picked up twice and others not at all. Coordination failures occur, and the investigators say so.

The population did not recover. It continued, badly in places, with duplication and gaps, because there was no mechanism to allocate anything and never had been.

And that is enough. It does not need to be efficient. The work proceeds at whatever rate it proceeds, and the absence of the eleven has made it worse rather than impossible.

Imperfect continuity is still continuity, and the panel refuses to upgrade it.
