# Page 100 — Panel 02

"Who decides the threshold?" the chair asks. "Who verifies compliance? Who can order a pause? Who can authorize restart?"

Questions derived from documented response gaps.

Four questions, and the reader has seen all four answered inside one organization on page 090.

That page's response chart assigned severity, owner, stop authority, and restart authority, and the empty ownership cell from the incident was filled. It was good work and the book said so.

It was also entirely internal. The organization set its own thresholds, verified its own compliance, granted itself stop authority, and defined its own restart rule.

Which is not a criticism — nothing else was available — but it means the four questions have institutional answers and no external ones.

Who decides the threshold across the field? Nobody. Who verifies compliance? Page 091 established that the party positioned to do it was not permitted to assess effectiveness. Who can order a pause anywhere but inside one company? Nobody. Who authorizes restart? The same people who ordered the pause.

Four questions with the same answer, and the answer is: whoever is stopping themselves.
