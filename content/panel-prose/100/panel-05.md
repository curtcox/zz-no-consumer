# Page 100 — Panel 05

The four columns remain. Open question is the only one that expands.

Knowing the problem does not create the institution that can coordinate the answer.

The chapter's conclusion.

The invented forum ends here, and the columns are the record of what it produced.

Documented event: filled on page 097, from the book's own pages.

Organization claim: filled on page 099, with arguments their authors actually made in public.

Independent check: empty. Still. It was empty when the forum opened and nothing over four pages put anything in it, because nothing exists to put there.

Open question: expanding. Four rows on page 098 about channels and standing, and now six activities, four authorities, and two costs that do not cancel.

The book invented a room in order to ask, and asking produced more questions than it started with.

And the caption is the chapter's last analytical line. Everything in this book — the diagnosis, the mechanisms, the convergence, the coordination problem — is knowledge, and the knowledge has been available in public reports since the twenty-sixth of August.

None of it builds the thing that could act on it.
