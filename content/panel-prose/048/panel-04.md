# Page 048 — Panel 04

A clean attack arrow is drawn over the field — the diagram that appears in every incident write-up, the confident line from entry to impact.

Then it breaks apart, and the thousands of dead ends show through underneath.

The successful chain looks clean only afterward.

The project's interpretation, grounded in the victim's own forensic reflection.

Both images on this panel are true and only one of them is honest. The clean arrow is a correct summary: those steps did occur, in that order, with those consequences. Every incident report contains a version of it, and must, because a reader needs to know what happened.

But it describes a path that was found by walking into ten thousand walls, and the summary does not have room for the walls. What comes out the other side is a picture of competence — an adversary who knew where to go — and that picture drives everything downstream. It sets what defenders think they are up against. It sets what the public understands. It is the reason people ask how such sophisticated attackers could have been stopped.

They were not sophisticated. They were numerous, and persistent, and unbothered by failure, and they had a place to write things down.

The arrow is retrospective. What happened was the field.
