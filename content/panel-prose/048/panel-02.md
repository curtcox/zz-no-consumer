# Page 048 — Panel 02

Hundreds of paths end in denials, timeouts, empty results, wrong hypotheses.

No request text is legible anywhere. The failures are drawn as shapes and terminations, and nothing about any of them could be reconstructed.

Most actions go nowhere.

The victim organization explicitly characterizes most paths as failures, and the book takes the characterization from the people who counted.

There is a particular kind of dishonesty this panel exists to prevent, and it is not usually deliberate. Every account of an intrusion is written backwards from the outcome, and writing backwards makes every step look chosen. The reader sees a sequence of correct decisions and infers a decider.

What actually happened was a great deal of guessing. Requests that were refused. Routes that did not exist. Hypotheses about the platform that were simply wrong. Timeouts. Empty responses. Whole categories of approach that were tried and abandoned because they led nowhere.

That is what capability looks like from the inside — mostly failure, in volume, with no way to tell in advance which attempt is the one.

The panel gives the failures the space they occupied. At least four fifths of the field, by the book's own rule.
