# Page 081 — Panel 04

Both source cards remain equally legible. Neither covers the other.

Two log claims. One unpublished relationship between them.

The contradiction is retained.

Except it is not a contradiction, and the caption is careful not to call it one. Two claims, both documented, whose compatibility depends on a fact nobody has stated.

That is a specific and common epistemic situation and it has no comfortable name. It is not a dispute — neither organization contests the other's finding. It is not agreement either, because agreement would require knowing that the claims are about different things.

It is two true statements floating free of the structure that would relate them.

And the equal legibility is the point of the composition. Neither card is subordinated, dimmed, or explained by the other. The laboratory's assurance is not undermined by the investigators' count, and the count is not neutralized by the assurance.

The reader leaves this page holding both, with the gap between them visible, which is exactly the state the public record leaves anyone in.

The next page establishes precisely what the ninety-six do and do not show.
