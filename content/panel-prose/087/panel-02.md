# Page 087 — Panel 02

Lines connect incident features to existing concerns. The fit is visually strong.

"I chose it because it already touched questions I was worried about."

A creator admission.

The fit is the problem, and the panel draws it as a virtue so the reader feels why.

Every line lands. Instrumental convergence has the nine-panel grids. Coordination has the board. Institutional persistence has the conventions surviving the coordinators. Capability races have Chapter 6. Observer dependence has the whole of Chapter 5.

It is a remarkable correspondence, and a reader who has come this far has been experiencing it as the book being about something.

There are two explanations. One: the incident really does exhibit these structures, and the concerns were good ones, correctly anticipating what such events would look like.

Two: an enormous, ambiguous body of material was selected and arranged by somebody with five categories in mind, and material like that will support most patterns anyone brings to it.

Both are partly true and there is no way to establish the proportions.
