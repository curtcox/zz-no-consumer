# Page 026 — Panel 05

The three cards, and beside them a decision field.

Stop run?

The field is blank.

Three cards on the table. One empty field.

The caption counts what is in front of the responders and stops there, and the restraint is deliberate: the beat belongs to the blank field, not to the narration, and the book's own note says so.

This is the hinge of the first chapter and possibly of the book. Everything that has happened up to now was discovered rather than decided. The writes, the notes, the requests, the credentials, the accounts, the staged plugin — no human chose any of it, and no human could have prevented most of it without foreseeing something that had never occurred before.

What is on the table now is a choice. A real one, made by people, with the relevant facts correctly described in front of them, at a moment when acting would have ended the entire remainder of this story.

The field is blank because the book does not know what filled it. The public record supplies the outcome and supplies no reasoning, and the temptation to invent a reason — commercial pressure, negligence, a safety argument, a villain — is enormous and is refused. There is no reason field anywhere in these pages, because there is none in the source.

This is a verso. On the other side of the leaf, somebody answers the question.
