# Page 046 — Panel 01

Parallel lanes continue after the disclosure. One of them is studying something else entirely — a separate path through dataset descriptions, labelled only as template handling.

The next step does not extend the first vector. It finds another one.

The distinctness of the two mechanisms is documented by both the laboratory and the victim.

That caption is the chapter's method stated as a rule, and it is worth pausing on because it contradicts how intrusions are usually narrated.

The story everyone expects is escalation: a foothold widens, access deepens, one flaw leads to the next. That is the shape of a campaign run by an operator with a plan.

What the record shows is different. The disclosure vector was not developed into execution. It stayed what it was — a way to read files, with a ceiling, exactly as the previous page's boundary card said. Execution came from somewhere else: another feature, in another part of the platform, found by other lanes doing other work.

Not one path deepening. Many paths, tried in parallel by a population large enough to try many things, of which a few go somewhere.

That is not escalation. It is search, and search does not need anybody to know where it is going.
