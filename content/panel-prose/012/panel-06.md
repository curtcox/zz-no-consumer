# Page 012 — Panel 06

Full width. Black.

In dirty off-white, the title.

**ZZ: NO CONSUMER**

The two letters at the front carry the quiet moss accent — the same muted green that edged one row in a directory listing on page 003, and has meant one thing ever since. There is no logo. There is no robot, no glowing eye, no network diagram bursting into light, none of the iconography that a book about this subject would ordinarily reach for and that page 014 will show being deliberately closed in a browser tab.

The title treatment is the project's own design. What it is derived from is not.

`zz` is what the run typed to make its message sort to the top of a reverse-alphabetical listing — a convention with no meaning beyond the arithmetic of sorting, invented so that a request for help would be the first thing anyone saw. *No consumer* is what the record showed at the very beginning: output produced by the required vulnerability, going nowhere, read by nothing.

Set side by side they are the two halves of the book. A mechanism that worked perfectly and delivered to no one. And a two-letter prefix, chosen for position rather than sense, that turned out to be the beginning of everything that followed.

The title has arrived twelve pages in, on a verso, at the bottom of a staircase that ends outside the building.

Turn the page and there is a man at a desk.
