# Page 095 — Panel 04

Curt sees only published pages and dates. No boardroom. No leaked notes. No private transcript.

"I can show the pressure in what they published. I cannot claim to know the private decision."

A creator disclosure.

The panel exists because the previous three could easily be read as a reconstruction of somebody's reasoning, and they are not.

What is on the page is a ledger — three dated public actions, in order, with their contents. The pattern a reader sees in them is a pattern in published material.

What produced them is not available. There were meetings; nobody outside was in them. There were arguments; no minutes are public. Whatever weighing occurred between the tenth and the eighteenth of August happened somewhere this book cannot go.

And the temptation is considerable, because a scene in that room would be the most dramatic thing in the chapter and page 053 has already demonstrated that the book knows how to stage a composite.

It staged one, disclosed it in the largest type on the page, and made it explicitly about no meeting in particular.

Here there is not even that. Three dates and what was published on them.
