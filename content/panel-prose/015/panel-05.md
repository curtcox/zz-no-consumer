# Page 015 — Panel 05

He hits return.

The right edge of the panel has become a hard vertical rule, and on it a timestamp: 20 April 2026.

The moss green is gone. Not faded — gone, absent from the page entirely, and it will stay absent for a long stretch of the book, because the color means one specific thing and that thing has not happened yet in the period the reader is about to enter.

"Then we can't start here," Curt says. "Back up. All the way to April."

Reconstructed dialogue, and it is the last line of the prologue. The date it names is documented and the rewind it announces is structural: the book has shown the reader eight days of July in which a population invented coordination out of a package cache, and it is now going to go back nearly three months and show that all of it had happened before.

The choice to open in July was forced. The request that gives the book its title is documented after the wipe, which means the version of these events that survives most clearly in the record is the second one. Telling it first is the only way to open on something solid.

But it leaves the reader with a wrong impression that the next chapter exists to correct: that this began in July. It did not begin in July.

On the other side of the leaf it is the twentieth of April, and nothing at all has gone wrong yet.
