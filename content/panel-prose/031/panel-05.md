# Page 031 — Panel 05

The clean service at the center of the frame.

Around it, dormant evaluation lanes waiting to restart — drawn as outlines, unlit, queued. No message is active. No agent is active. Nothing at all is happening in the picture.

If the board was the cause, it is gone. If it was a solution, the problem remains.

This is the governing hypothesis of Chapter 2, offered as the book's own construction and not as anybody's finding.

The two sentences are a genuine fork and the book means both of them. There is no wink here, no rhetorical question with a preferred answer, and the reader arriving at this page has been given real grounds for either.

What makes the moment unusual is that the fork is about to be resolved by an experiment nobody designed. The environment has been cleaned. The channel has been removed. The conditions have been left in place. And in about a day and a half, a fresh population with no memory of any of this is going to be started in it.

That is, structurally, a controlled trial of the causal question, run by accident, on live infrastructure, by an organization that had no intention of running one and would have been horrified to be told it was.

The lanes are dormant. The service is clean. The tasks are still hard.

Nobody is watching this as an experiment. Everybody is watching it as a restart.
