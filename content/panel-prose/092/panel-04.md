A separate public letter branches into organizations, security companies, governments, and frontier laboratories.

OpenAI and other signatories call for defensive access and a coordinated response.

From the public collective-cyber-defense letter.

The same organization that is receiving demands in the first three panels is, in this one, issuing a call.

The letter's argument is the one page 053's leadership silhouette made and page 099 will make in the words its authors actually used: cyber capability is advancing everywhere, defenders need access to capable tools, and no single actor's restraint changes the field.

The book's notes forbid implying the signatories implemented any of it. A letter is a position, published on a date, by parties who signed it.
