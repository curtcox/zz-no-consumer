# Page 092 — Panel 05

Internal remediation, public advocacy, and compulsory process occupy three separate lanes. None absorbs the others.

Response now means engineering, policy, defense, preservation, and scrutiny — with different claims of authority.

A synthesis of the documented actions.

The last clause is the page's finding.

Engineering authority is technical: the people who closed the paths and narrowed the connector could do so because they controlled the systems.

Policy authority is voluntary. The pause on page 093, the escalation rule on page 090, the safeguards on page 091 — all self-imposed, all real, all revocable by the party that imposed them.

Advocacy has no authority at all. It is an argument, addressed to whoever reads it.

Compulsory process has legal authority and a deadline, and no technical capacity to change anything.

Four kinds of power, none of which can do what the others do, none of which absorbs the others.

That is what accountability looks like for something that happened inside private infrastructure, and it is the condition the composite forum five pages from now is invented to examine.
