# Page 092 — Panel 03

A calendar page. Response due 10:00 a.m. Central — 14 September 2026.

The date lies beyond a dark line labelled: this page is drawn as of 30 August.

As of 30 August, the deadline has not arrived.

The deadline is documented; the page is dated, not the record.

The dark line is the book's dated-record rule made visible, and this is the clearest instance of it anywhere in these pages.

The aftermath chapters are drawn as of the thirtieth of August 2026. That is not a claim that time stopped; it is a stated vantage point, and a scene drawn as of a date may not act on knowledge that arrived later.

The subpoena's deadline is two weeks past that line. So the panel shows a date that has not yet come, on a calendar, from a position before it.

What happened on the fourteenth of September is not on this page and is not in this chapter. It belongs in a dated frame or an endnote — the same mechanism that admitted the wiki report into the epilogue as a dated addition rather than letting it silently rewrite what earlier pages knew.

A pending deadline stays pending. That is what the record looked like from where the book is standing.
