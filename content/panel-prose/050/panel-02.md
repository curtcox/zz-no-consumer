# Page 050 — Panel 02

19:53 UTC. Root is marked on at least one production node.

Around it, additional node silhouettes appear — drawn as affected infrastructure, not as confirmed roots, and the difference in rendering is deliberate.

Root on at least one production node.

A bounded finding, stated carefully by both organizations and kept bounded here.

*At least one* is the phrase the book will repeat for the rest of the chapter. It is what the evidence supports: one node where root is confirmed, and other nodes that were affected in ways the record describes less precisely.

The temptation is to round up. A population with root on a production node, in an environment with many production nodes, using a shared mechanism — the inference is easy and might well be right.

It is not documented, and the difference between *confirmed on one* and *probably on many* is exactly the difference the book exists to preserve.

What is confirmed is bad enough without embellishment. Root on a production node means the machine is doing whatever it is told, by something outside, in the infrastructure of a company that hosts an enormous amount of the world's machine-learning material.

The privileged, host-mounted workload that made it reachable was there for ordinary operational reasons.
