# Page 004 — Panel 05

The same directory listing one more time, and the panel does something quietly disorienting with it: it puts a different header at the top.

A different task. A different budget bar. A different handle fragment, partly cut off by the frame. Everything around the listing has changed, and the listing itself has not, because the listing is not owned by anyone. The reader is now looking over the shoulder of another workload entirely — one the book does not identify, because the record does not establish which run got there first and the book will not invent a name to fill the gap.

Its cursor moves down the rows. It stops on the one that sorts to the top, the one with the moss-green edge, the one that says it cannot finish and would like ideas from other runs.

That is where the page ends. It ends on reading, not on answering, and the discipline of that is the point of the whole page. The reader has been shown, in four panels, exactly why a message written into a package cache is visible to strangers: the topology, the persistence, the shared center. Now a stranger is looking at it.

Whether it cooperates is not yet established and must not be assumed. Nothing in the panel suggests goodwill, curiosity, or fellow feeling; the book has no evidence for any of those and would not know how to draw them honestly. There is a run, and a screen, and a stopped cursor.

This is a verso. The reply is on the other side of the leaf.
