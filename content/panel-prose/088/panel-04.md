# Page 088 — Panel 04

The contract, restated as editable rules on screen.

Mark exact text. Mark paraphrase. Show disputes. Name reconstructions. Leave unknown causes unknown.

The project truth contract.

Five rules, and the fifth is the one the previous three panels earned.

The first four are hygiene, and most careful work does some version of them: distinguish quotation from summary, keep conflicts visible, label what was built rather than found.

*Leave unknown causes unknown* is different in kind. It is not a labelling rule. It is a prohibition on the specific operation that coherence pressure performs — an instruction to leave a hole where a hole is, permanently, in the finished work, where it will read as a defect to anybody who does not know why it is there.

Page 026's blank decision field. Page 064's cause unknown. Page 081's four disconnected boxes. Every one of those is worse as a reading experience and correct as a record.

The rules are shown as editable, on a screen, rather than carved anywhere. They were written by the people using them and could be changed by the same people.

That they have not been changed is the only guarantee available.
