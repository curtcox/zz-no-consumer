# Page 088 — Panel 05

The manuscript continues into a folder labelled *aftermath*.

No neutral vantage point floats above it. Every layer carries its visible role and limits.

"We can continue without pretending we're neutral."

*Continue — with the seams visible.*

The chapter's closing exchange.

Chapter 5 ends by declining the position that documentary work traditionally claims.

There is no view from nowhere available here. The investigators used an instrument with known biases on a corpus they could not fully read. The creator came with a thesis and chose material that fit it. The drafting assistance pushes toward coherence. The reader supplies architecture where claims nearly fit.

Every layer has a position, and none of them can be stepped out of.

What is available instead is the seam — the visible join, where one thing was fitted to another by somebody, on purpose, for reasons that can be stated.

A seamless account claims to be the world. A seamed one admits to being made, and a reader who can see the joins can evaluate the construction.

That is a smaller claim than neutrality and it is the only one this book can honestly make.

The folder is labelled aftermath, and Chapter 6 begins with things that were actually fixed.
