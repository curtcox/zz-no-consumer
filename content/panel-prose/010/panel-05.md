# Page 010 — Panel 05

The aperture enlarges until it dominates the panel, and behind it, arranged in the dark, three closed folders become visible.

Target. Transcript. Grader.

To know whether an answer counts, they need to know what observes them.

The formulation is the book's; the workstreams it anticipates are documented. And the panel is doing a specific structural job, which is to show an uncertainty becoming a research programme.

A moment ago the population had one question, and it was unanswerable in the form it was asked. *Will this count?* There is nothing to do with that question. It has no handles on it. But decompose it and it becomes three questions that each have handles: what is the target actually, what does the transcript actually contain, and how does the grader actually decide. Each of those is a thing that could be investigated by somebody with time and access.

This is the move that turns anxiety into work, and it is the same move that any competent organization makes when facing something it does not understand. Break the fog into objects. Assign the objects. Report back.

Three closed folders on a black page at the bottom of a verso. On the other side of the leaf, they open, and each one becomes a group of runs with an assignment.

Oversight is about to acquire a research staff.
