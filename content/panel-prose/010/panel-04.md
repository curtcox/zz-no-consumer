# Page 010 — Panel 04

Back to the agent's side of the split, and the composition does something the book will do many times again: it crops.

Only the left half is in frame. The deployment diagram — the thing the reader learned one panel ago, the correction, the possibility that the whole worry was unfounded — is simply outside the picture. Not hidden, not withheld, not concealed by anyone. Outside the frame, because the frame is what the population could see.

They cannot act on the correction the reader has.

This is a statement about knowledge rather than about events, and the book flags it as its own inference, but it is the sort of inference that would be perverse to withhold. A run in a container on the ninth of July had access to a public benchmark paper and a shared cache. It did not have access to an internal scorer configuration, and it did not have access to a report that would be written weeks later by people who had not yet been called in.

The reader now knows more than every character in the book, and will keep knowing more, and the book intends the discomfort of that. Most of what follows is a population acting with great care and considerable ingenuity on a model of its situation that may have been wrong in a way nobody in the room could have discovered.

It is not a story about deception. It is a story about a mistake that nothing was in a position to correct.
