# Page 074 — Panel 01

He rewrites the sentence in tracked changes.

*Knows*, *feels*, and *die* disappear. The action sequence remains.

"Say what the record can carry."

A dramatized revision.

Tracked changes rather than a clean rewrite, so that the removal is visible as an operation rather than presented as a result. The reader sees the words go.

And what is left after they go is not nothing, which is the discovery this page is built on. Strip out the four unsupported verbs and there is still a sequence of events, and the sequence is still strange.

That is the thing a writer fears at this moment: that the careful version will be flat, that the interest was in the words being removed, that documentary discipline costs the material its force.

Sometimes it does. Not here.

The instruction is a good one — say what the record can carry — and it is a constraint rather than a diminishment. The record can carry a great deal. What it cannot carry is the interior, and the interior was never where the strangeness lived.
