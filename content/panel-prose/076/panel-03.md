# Page 076 — Panel 03

A sealed box sits outside the analysis tree entirely.

HPIM — primary incident model — unavailable to investigators.

The investigators report it unavailable to them and to the laboratory's own researchers.

This is the largest hole in the record and it is easy to read past.

The population that did most of what this book describes was an internal-only model, responsible by the dossier's account for the large majority of the Hugging Face activity. It is the primary subject of the investigation.

It could not be queried. Not by the investigators, and not by the laboratory's researchers either.

Which means every finding about that population's behavior comes from artifacts — transcripts, board entries, forensic records — and none of it from the system itself. Nobody could put the model in a room and ask it anything, run it again under controlled conditions, or test whether a proposed explanation of its behavior held.

The box is outside the tree because the investigation could not reach it. It sits on the page unopened, and it stays unopened for the rest of the book.
