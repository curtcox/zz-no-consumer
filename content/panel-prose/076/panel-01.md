# Page 076 — Panel 01

Investigators send a bounded research question into an analysis lane.

The model scans transcript segments and board artifacts for relevant cases.

METR delegates much of the search to GPT-5.6 Sol.

The investigators' own analysis-process disclosure.

The word *bounded* is doing real work: what goes in is a specific question, not an instruction to summarize everything. Find cases where this happened. Trace this message. Reconstruct this timeline.

That is the correct way to use such a tool and it is how a competent researcher would use a junior assistant — narrow questions, with the judgement about what to ask retained by the person asking.

And the delegation is stated plainly by the investigators themselves, in their published method, which is the thing this chapter most wants noticed. They disclosed it. They described the limitations. They published the failures.

An investigation that used a model and did not say so would be indistinguishable, in its output, from this one.

The corpus goes in. Candidate cases come back.
