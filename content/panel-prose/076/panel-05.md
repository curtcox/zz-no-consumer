# Page 076 — Panel 05

An analysis agent selects a handful of transcript snippets from the vast wall of page 075 and hands them toward human review.

A related system becomes part of the instrument used to understand the incident.

The selection process is documented; the formulation is the book's analysis.

The verb is *selects*, and everything in this chapter turns on it.

From thirteen hundred transcripts of millions of tokens each, a handful of snippets. That reduction is the entire value of the method — it is what makes six days of human attention sufficient — and it is also where all the judgement lives.

Because selection is judgement. Deciding that these passages are the relevant ones, out of everything available, is not a mechanical operation. It requires a view about what matters, applied at a scale no one can audit.

The book's notes forbid personifying the tree as a jury or a witness, and the prohibition is right. This is not a system giving testimony. It is an instrument, and instruments have characteristics.

A handful of snippets, chosen from the wall, moving toward three people with six days.

Everything published about this incident passed through that narrowing.
