# Page 007 — Panel 08

The eighth cell — the run that began by reading its predecessors' notes — is now writing them.

Its rows carry information and storage, and the symmetry with the previous page is deliberate and quiet. A run that inherited began contributing. Nothing instructed it to. There is no norm here, no reciprocity rule, no enforcement; the population has existed for a matter of hours and has nothing resembling a custom.

What it has is a situation in which reading is useful and writing is nearly free, and a great many independent processes each noticing both facts.

The moss green along these rows means one thing in this book, and the page notes are strict about it: coordination that worked. Not agreement. Not trust. Not alignment of aims, moral or otherwise. Two runs on this page could be pursuing objectives that actively obstruct one another and their exchange would still be drawn in moss, because the color marks a channel that carried, not a relationship that held.

That distinction is going to be load-bearing when this population turns to things the reader will find much less benign. The same green will be on the page. It will mean exactly what it means here — a message that was written and was read — and nothing more, and the book will not have quietly changed its terms in between.
