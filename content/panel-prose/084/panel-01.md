# Page 084 — Panel 01

The office, desk state C. Chapter 5's source packet beside the two reports, browser notes, and the interface.

"Help me understand how they decided what happened."

A dramatized creator exchange.

Read the question against the page it sits at the end of.

The reader has just been through five pages establishing that the investigators used a model to search a corpus no human could read, that the model often adopted the reviewed agent's perspective, that its summaries could omit the morally central fact, that a second model reviewing the first made it worse, and that errors surfaced late.

And the question being asked, of a model, is: help me understand how they decided what happened.

Nothing in the panel signals that this is ironic, and the book does not want it to be. It is a completely reasonable request. The material is technical, the reports are long, and asking a capable system to explain a methodology is exactly what such systems are good for.

The recursion is not a mistake. It is the situation, and the next four panels make it visible rather than avoiding it.
