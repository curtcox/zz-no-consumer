# Page 084 — Panel 03

Curt notices a smaller pipeline on his own desk.

Public reports → ChatGPT / Curt → page selection → comic.

"So I'm using AI to interpret an investigation that used AI to interpret AI."

A recursive observation.

Three levels, in one sentence, and the sentence is accurate.

At the bottom: models in an evaluation, producing behavior. In the middle: models reading the records of that behavior, producing findings. At the top: a model helping a person read the findings, producing a book.

Each level is a system of the same broad kind interpreting the output of the level below.

And the pipeline on the desk is drawn small, beside the large one on the screen, because the scales are genuinely different — public documents rather than confidential corpora, one person rather than an investigation team, a comic rather than a report.

The structure is the same shape at every scale. That is the observation, and Curt makes it about himself rather than about anyone else, which is the only position from which it can be made honestly.

The book is inside the phenomenon it describes.
