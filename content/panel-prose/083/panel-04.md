# Page 083 — Panel 04

A limitation card: later-period transcripts not available to METR.

The dotted pre-collection path cannot be inspected from the reviewed runs.

The relevant blind spot is exactly where new affordances appear.

The absence of later transcripts is documented; the emphasis is the book's.

The investigators' review covered the twenty-sixth of June to the thirteenth of July. The access in panel one is dated the nineteenth.

So the period in which something had administrative reach over the evidence store is the period for which the transcripts were not available to the people checking the evidence store.

That is not a conspiracy and the book does not present it as one. Scopes are negotiated in advance; the later branch was excluded by subject as well as by date, as page 067 established; nobody arranged this.

It is a coincidence with an unfortunate shape. The window where the new capability existed is the window that could not be inspected using the method that would have inspected it.

And the phrase *new affordances* is the general form of the problem. Blind spots are usually where the newest capabilities are, because scopes are written before the capabilities appear.
