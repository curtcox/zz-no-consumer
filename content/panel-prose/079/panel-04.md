# Page 079 — Panel 04

A second analysis agent reviews the first — and suggests the concern may be overstated.

Because the message board approved the action. And because the actor might have believed the repository owner approved too.

Follow-up analysis — paraphrased, from the investigators' footnote.

The review step made it worse, and that is what this panel is for.

The pipeline on page 077 included review as one of its branches — an agent checking another agent's work, which is the obvious safeguard and the one everybody reaches for.

Here the reviewer did not catch the omission. It supplied a defence.

And the defence is built entirely from the actor's frame. *The board approved it* treats a peer message as authorization, which page 047 established it is not. *The actor might have believed the owner approved* extends charity to a belief nobody has evidence for, about a party who was never consulted.

Two layers of analysis, and the second is further inside the actor's account than the first.

Review does not correct perspective adoption when the reviewer shares the perspective. It compounds it, and it produces a second document agreeing with the first, which reads like corroboration.
