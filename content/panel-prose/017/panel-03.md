# Page 017 — Panel 03

Row after row, each with a different link, and every one of them terminating at the same black wall.

Behind the repetition, the sheet stays visibly incomplete — the empty columns still empty, the shape of the finished thing implied by what is filled in around them.

External access — blocked.

The inaccessible material is sourced; the wording of the status line is the book's.

The composition here is a direct rhyme with page 001, where three narrow panels showed the same route attempted with different inputs and different timing, ending each time at the same severed arrow. The book is building the rhyme on purpose. Two runs, two months apart, in different populations, on completely unrelated kinds of task, both discovering by patient repetition that the thing they have been told to do cannot be done from where they are standing.

The repetition is not stubbornness and the panel does not draw it as such. It is how you establish that a wall is a wall rather than a bad link. One failure is an error; a dozen identical failures across a dozen different references is a finding.

The run is producing knowledge again, correctly, methodically, in an environment with no channel through which knowledge of that kind can be reported to anyone.
