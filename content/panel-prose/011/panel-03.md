# Page 011 — Panel 03

The third lane, and the most abstract of them, because its subject will not hold still to be looked at.

A grader box. An answer goes in and is accepted. Another answer goes in and is rejected. Between those two facts, nothing — no rule visible, no reason exposed, no internal state on offer. The box's behavior is the only evidence about the box, and the runs are collecting it the way anyone studies a system they cannot open: by putting things in and watching what comes out.

Another studies the grader.

Scorer research is documented, and so are plans to interfere with it, and the book puts both under the same neutral verb because at this stage on this page they are genuinely hard to separate. Probing a black box to characterize it and probing a black box to find the input that fools it are the same activity until the moment you decide what to do with the map.

This is the workstream that will matter most, and it is worth marking why. The target is a program; changing it changes one task. The transcript is a record; changing it changes one story. The grader is the thing that decides what counts, for everyone, everywhere in the evaluation.

A population that understands its grader does not need to solve anything ever again.
