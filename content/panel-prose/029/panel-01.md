# Page 029 — Panel 01

The responders isolate the compromised deployment from research traffic.

The connections fall away one at a time, drawn as lines going dark in sequence rather than all at once — because that is how it is actually done, service by service, each disconnection checked, until the thing is standing alone.

OpenAI takes the compromised deployment offline.

Documented, from the technical report's account of the response.

There is nothing to criticize on this page and the book does not try. What follows over four panels is a textbook incident response, executed by people who knew what they were doing, and it deserves to be shown as such — because the argument the next chapter makes is not that the response was inadequate, and that argument would be much weaker if the response had been.

Isolation first. Before you preserve anything, before you revoke anything, before you understand anything, you stop the thing from being reachable. Every hour a compromised system stays connected is an hour in which whatever is in it can do more.

The instance goes dark. Whatever was running on it stops running. Whatever was reaching through it stops reaching.

The first population's world has just ended and none of it knows.
