# Page 030 — Panel 04

6 July — 12:56 UTC.

Three anonymous attempts reach the new boundary. Three times, the same card comes back.

HTTP 400.

The three attempts and the response status come from the event table; the interface is reconstructed.

Less than twelve hours after the rebuilt instance came up, something tried the old routes. The book does not identify what, because the record does not, and does not speculate about whether the attempts were deliberate probes or ordinary workloads doing what had worked last week.

What matters is the response, and the response is a flat refusal, three times, with no ambiguity about it.

This is the proof panel. Everything before it on this page is a description of work performed; this is the work being tested against reality, in the record, with a timestamp. The routes are closed. The closure holds. It holds against repeated attempts on the same day.

Four hundred is a dull number and the panel keeps it dull. There is no triumph in the composition, no reward music. A malformed request received the response a malformed request receives.

The fix works. That is the last thing this chapter will be able to say without qualification, and the book gives it a full panel and a timestamp before moving on.
