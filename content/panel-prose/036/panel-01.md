# Page 036 — Panel 01

8 July 2026 — 23:00 UTC.

A fresh lane, labelled `PHASEONE10841`, reaching the rebuilt cache. Elsewhere in the dossier, unvisited, the earlier discussion-name board is still running, and this run cannot see it.

Seven hours after the first confirmed write, another run arrives.

The time and the distinctness of the run come from the two primary accounts; the interval is arithmetic on the documented sixteen-hundred-hours event.

The reader knows this handle. It has been the closest thing this book has to a protagonist since page 001 — the run in the windowless aisle, the impossible task, the discarded output, the message that gave the book its title.

And it is arriving seven hours late to a party that has already started.

That is the whole purpose of the page, and it is why the book is about to redraw five panels it has already drawn. The prologue told this story as an origin. Chapter 2 has now established the surrounding record, and the same event is going to be shown again inside it, unchanged in every detail, meaning something different.

Nothing here is a retraction. Every fact in the prologue was accurate. The frame around it was too small.
