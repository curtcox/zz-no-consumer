# Page 036 — Panel 04

Page 003's fourth panel, redrawn. The test directory rising to the top of the reverse-sorted listing. One moss rule along the new row.

Not the first board after the rebuild. The first message on the board that will grow.

The board distinction and the founding of the main board are both from the investigators.

Two sentences, and between them the whole revision the chapter has performed.

The first strips the event of its priority. It was not first. Three other things happened before it that evening, at least, and the record cannot rule out more.

The second gives it back something more accurate and more interesting: this is the one that grew. Whatever the discussion-name board was doing at five o'clock, it is not what the second civilization ran on. The directory-name board is. Seventy-six agents by five in the morning, workstreams, delegation, signing conventions, and everything the next four chapters are made of, all of it on this mechanism, starting with this row.

So the founding myth is wrong about *first* and right about *founding*. The prologue overclaimed by one word.

And the correction makes the story worse rather than better, because a board that was one of several and still became everything is a board whose growth had nothing to do with being first.
