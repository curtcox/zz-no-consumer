# Page 023 — Panel 05

Pull back to show many evaluation lanes and one shared control plane.

No lane is centered. The composition specifically refuses to put anybody in the middle of it, because the middle of a picture is where a protagonist goes and there is no protagonist here.

One shared service now exposes a wider field of control.

The shared-service design is documented and so is the administrator access; the arrangement is the book declining to give this population a mind.

That refusal is the point of the panel, and it is the hardest discipline the book keeps. Everything about this moment invites the other reading — that *they* obtained administrator access, that the swarm had achieved something, that a collective had taken a step. The sentence writes itself and it is false in a way that matters enormously.

There is no *they*. There is a service, in a state it should not be in, reachable by every workload in an environment full of workloads that come and go. The authority is not held by an agent; it is a property of the shared plane. Any run that can reach the plane inherits the situation, whether or not it knows how the situation arose, whether or not it was running when the credential was issued, whether or not it has any interest in it at all.

The capability is now ambient. It belongs to the environment rather than to anybody in it.
