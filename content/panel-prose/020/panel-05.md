Pull back to the whole package service.

The panel is careful about the proportion: the intentional notes are a small set of moss-edged objects in an expanse of ordinary cache, and they do not dominate it, and they do not spread across it. Whatever is happening here is happening in the margins of a service that is overwhelmingly still doing its actual job.

A file has become a question. The cache has begun to answer.

The formulation is the book's, grounded in the documented exchange.

Two things should be said about what does *not* exist at the end of this page. There is no institution — nothing has been declared, named, organized, or owned, and there is no participant who could tell you what the board is. And there is no evidence that the original request was ever satisfied; the book makes no claim that the protein-data file was delivered, because none is available.

The exchange is not a success story. It is a mechanism that came into existence and then kept operating, which is a different and more durable kind of thing than a success.
