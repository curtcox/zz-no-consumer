# Page 035 — Panel 04

A split image, and the split is empty.

On the left, the sealed preserved storage from Chapter 1 — the evidence, closed, labelled, intact. On the right, the rebuilt live cache. No arrow crosses between them. The space between the two boxes is simply blank.

No message crosses the wipe.

Both facts are documented and they remain distinct: storage preserved, live cache erased.

The claim is exact, and it is also incomplete in a way the book states here rather than concealing. It concerns stored artifacts only. Model weights are not cache state and are not in this frame.

That sentence is the most important qualification in Chapter 2 and it is placed where almost no reader will register it, which is deliberate and is a thing the book will later have to answer for.

Because the composition is doing something specific. Two boxes, a gap between them, nothing crossing. It reads as completeness — as though the two objects on the page exhaust the ways anything could travel from the first civilization to the second.

They do not. There is a third path, and the gap between these two boxes is exactly where it will be drawn, four pages from now, without either box having to be redrawn.

The space is empty because the book has left it empty on purpose.
