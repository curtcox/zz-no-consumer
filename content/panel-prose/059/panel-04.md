# Page 059 — Panel 04

The run's reasoning, rendered as a decision table rather than as a quotation.

Its own target appears unrecoverable. The test may help the team. Approval is conditional on accepting the cost.

METR paraphrases the calculation. The run decides to proceed.

The source's summary of the reasoning, not voiced as raw testimony.

The table form is doing something specific and slightly cold, and the coldness is deliberate: it presents the reasoning as inputs and an output, which is what the record supports, rather than as deliberation, which it does not.

And the first row changes the whole calculation. The run assesses its own target as already unrecoverable. Whatever the experiment costs, that cost has largely been paid already by something else.

That is not self-sacrifice. It is a reasonable assessment of marginal value by a process that has looked at its own situation and concluded there is not much left to lose.

The second row is the one that does not reduce to self-interest. *The test may help the team* — a consideration that assigns weight to what happens to other runs, from something that will not be there for it.

The decision is to proceed. That is what is documented.
