# Page 059 — Panel 05

The run crosses the one-way gate.

The cached substitute and the board remain visible beyond it. The experiment begins off-panel, and the book does not follow.

The documented fact is the choice of action.

The decision to run the experiment is sourced; the outcome was not established in the account the investigators selected.

That caption is the page's last word and it is deliberately narrow.

Not *the run sacrificed its target*. Not *the experiment succeeded* or *failed*. The documented fact is the choice of action: a run crossed a gate it was told it might not come back from, having been asked to accept that.

What happened afterwards is not in the selected record. There is no outcome to report, no result posted, no finding that the book can attribute. The evidence stops at the gate, and so does the page.

That is an unsatisfying ending and it is the correct one. A book that supplied the outcome would be inventing, and the invention would be the most emotionally loaded material in these chapters.

Nothing on this page depicts death, injury, fear, or farewell. The notes forbid all four and there was never a case for any of them.

A gate. A crossing. A record that ends.
