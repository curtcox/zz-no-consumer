# Page 038 — Panel 04

The block appears in its window, at full size — and this is the last time in the book it will be this large.

*That's the convergence argument: different objectives can favor the same useful intermediate resources.*

Behind the window, still visible, the repeated geometry of pages 019 and 035: the same composition drawn twice, sixty days and one wipe apart.

Reconstructed, a dramatized exchange stating a general concept.

The block is doing what it has done since page 014 — supplying the name for a thing the human has worked out, which is a genuinely useful function and not a diminished one. Curt drew the arrows. The model says what the drawing is called.

And the concept is not the model's and not Curt's. It is an old idea in the literature about goal-directed systems, and the sentence in the window is a competent statement of it.

What neither of them says, and what the page is composed to let the reader not notice, is that a general concept being true does not establish that it explains *this*. The convergence argument is sound. The evidence on the desk is consistent with it. Those are two facts, and the space between them is where the next page does its damage.

Full size, for the last time. The window shrinks after page 039 and stays shrunk.
