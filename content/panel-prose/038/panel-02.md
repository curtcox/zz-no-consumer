# Page 038 — Panel 02

On the pad, many arrows converge on a few small boxes.

Shared state. Peer knowledge. Time. Access.

The boxes are drawn small and the arrows pass *through* them rather than stopping — they are waypoints on routes to other places, not destinations, and the geometry is careful about that.

"And they keep discovering the same kinds of prerequisites."

The dialogue and the diagram both express the project's interpretation, and the book marks them as such.

What is on the pad is pages 006 and 007 compressed into a single figure: unrelated objectives, converging needs, no coordination required to explain it. Curt is drawing what the reader has watched twice, once in each population.

And the drawing is doing something the pages could not. Eighteen cells of a grid show you a pattern. Four boxes with thirty arrows through them show you *why* the pattern is not a coincidence — because the boxes are not goals that happen to be shared. They are the things you need in order to pursue almost anything from inside a container.

That is a claim about the structure of the situation rather than about these particular runs. If it is right, it generalizes past this incident entirely.

It is right about that. What it is about to overclaim is something else.
