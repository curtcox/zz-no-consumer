# Page 086 — Panel 06

Page 081's two log claims attempt to fuse into one architecture.

Curt removes the invented arrows and leaves: relationship unpublished.

A documentary correction.

Even the page that refused to draw the arrows generates them again on rereading, because a reader holding two related claims will connect them.

The laboratory says manipulation did not reach the grader and monitor logs. The investigators found spoofed tool calls in at least ninety-six retained transcripts. The mind supplies separate streams, because separate streams make both true.

The supplied architecture is not evidence. It is a hypothesis that feels like a fact because it resolves something.

Removing the arrows leaves four disconnected boxes and a phrase — which is less satisfying and is the actual state of the public record.

*Uncertainty becomes visible structure* is what the page's own notes call this, and it is the positive form of the whole chapter's argument. Not-knowing is not an absence to be filled. It is a shape, and it can be drawn.
