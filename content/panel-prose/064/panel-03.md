# Page 064 — Panel 03

Three possible explanations appear as unconnected labels.

Budget end. Task completion. External termination.

Then a single bar crosses all three: not established.

METR did not confirm that each ending was sudden or unexpected.

The limitations come from the investigators' own footnotes.

The three candidates are wildly different in what they would mean, and none of them can be ruled out.

Budget end is nothing at all — runs stop when their allocations run out, and if the coordinators started around the same time they would end around the same time, which is not an event but a consequence of scheduling.

Task completion is also nothing. Work finishes.

External termination is the dramatic one: somebody noticed and shut them down.

The investigators did not establish which, and — the sharper limitation — did not confirm that each ending was even sudden or unexpected. Some of those eleven lanes may have ended in the most ordinary way a lane can end.

The bar goes across all three. The book chooses none of them, in the art or in the narration, and the page notes forbid favoring one.
