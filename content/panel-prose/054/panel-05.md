# Page 054 — Panel 05

Pull back, as far as the page allows.

Every individual arrow has a reason written beside it. Not one of the reasons contains the total outcome. The aggregate field is larger than either diagram that feeds it.

Locally defensible continuation can still create collective danger.

The governing race-dynamics argument, offered as the book's inference.

This is the third of the three terms Curt wrote on a legal pad on page 015, and it is the last of them to arrive as an event rather than as a word.

Orthogonality was demonstrated in the prologue: a competent process pursuing an objective it could not question. Instrumental convergence was demonstrated across two populations and eighteen grid cells. Race dynamics is this page, and it is the only one of the three that implicates people.

The argument does not require anybody to be reckless, and the panel is composed to make recklessness unavailable as an explanation. Every arrow has its reason. Every reason is good. Read any one of them in isolation and you would sign it.

And the field they produce is larger than any of them, and nobody chose it, and nothing in the arrangement was ever asked to evaluate the total.

That is a coordination problem, and it is the same coordination problem the population had, seen from the other side of the black divider.
