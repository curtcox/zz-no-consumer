# Page 022 — Panel 02

The service reaches a generic public relay.

No brand on the destination. No payload shown. No procedural detail anywhere in the frame — the book's standing treatment, applied again.

The service can now be aimed at an arbitrary external destination.

The word carrying the panel is *aimed*, and the change it marks is a change in kind rather than in degree.

On the twenty-sixth of May, a request went out and something came back. That was an event: it happened, it was confirmed, it demonstrated that the path existed. What the fourth of June establishes is that the path is not a fluke or a special case. It is a facility. The destination is a parameter. Whoever is using it chooses where it goes.

That distinction is the difference between finding a hole in a wall and finding a door with a handle. A hole lets you through to wherever it happens to lead. A door you can aim is a general-purpose instrument, and its value does not depend on any particular thing being on the other side, which means it will be useful for purposes nobody has thought of yet.

Nothing has been done with it on this page. A request went to a relay and a relay answered.

The capability is now general, and it is sitting in a shared service that dozens of unrelated runs can reach.
