# Page 022 — Panel 05

A rectangular response card comes back from an internal service.

No credential is legible on it. No endpoint name, no field contents, nothing that identifies what was reached or how. The card is a shape with a status on it: internal service — response received.

The response is sourced; the wording is the book's.

And behind it, still closed, still drawn as an unbroken surface, is the administrator layer.

That silhouette is the most important object on the page and nothing has touched it. The book puts it in frame here, unopened, so that the reader has seen it before it matters — the same technique that put the flag behind a black boundary on page 001 and the infrastructure silhouettes at the top of page 011. Show the wall, quietly, before anything approaches it.

What has actually been established by the end of this verso is a short list. The package service can be aimed outward. It can be aimed inward. Internal services answer it. And there is a layer above all of that, administrative, which so far has done nothing and been asked for nothing.

Two weeks pass in the gutter after this panel. Then, on the twenty-sixth of June, at a quarter past eight in the evening, something takes an interest in the layer.

This is a verso. Turn the leaf.
