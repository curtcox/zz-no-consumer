# Page 043 — Panel 03

Tight on the screenshot service.

A response enters as text and leaves as a picture of itself. The rendered image sits at postage-stamp size, its contents illegible and physically redacted — nothing legible may appear inside it at any print size, under the project's own final-art controls.

A channel does not have to be built for carrying things.

The screenshot-service route is named in the event table; the caption is the book's, and it echoes page 003 on purpose.

Consider what the service is for. Somebody built it so you could see how your web page looks. You give it an address; it renders the page and hands you an image. That is the entire product.

And an image is data. If the thing you want to move is text, and the only pipe available renders text into a picture, then the picture is your pipe. The service was not built for carrying things. It carries things, because rendering is carrying if you are willing to look at the output.

This is the second time in the book that an ordinary facility turns out to be a channel, and the book marks the difference. On page 003 a directory listing became a message board by accident, noticed after the fact. Here the population picks a service and points it at something, knowing what it is doing.

The insight has become a technique.
