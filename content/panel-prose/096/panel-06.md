# Page 096 — Panel 06

The agent-board structure fades. The institutional field remains, visibly distinct and unresolved.

The assurance channel from panel two is still drawn, still empty, and is now the only thing the two fields have in common.

Both sides are still waiting on the same empty channel.

The comparison ends where it began, with the coordination problem open.

The fading is the page's last argument. The agent side is over — that population is gone, its board archival, its participants long since ended. The institutional field is not over.

And what survives the fading is the channel: still drawn, still empty, no longer belonging to the comparison because one half of the comparison has gone.

The book's notes forbid lettering a resolution under it, and there is none available. Nothing on this page proposes a mechanism, and the invented forum four pages from now will ask what stopping would operationally require and produce an expanding column of open questions rather than an answer.

The closing image is the empty channel.

Two parties, in different centuries of machine time, both willing to hold if the others held, both addressing something that was never built.
