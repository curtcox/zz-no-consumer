# Page 056 — Panel 05

The attack map, fully legible, on the left. Active intrusion marks, still accumulating, on the right.

Between them, the severity gate, closed.

The correlation connects the attack. It does not escalate enough to wake anyone.

A direct synthesis of the victim organization's own correlation and criticality findings.

Chapter 3 ends here, and it ends on escalation failure rather than on blindness, which is a deliberate and uncomfortable choice.

The reassuring version of this incident is that nobody could see it. That version supports a clear response: better sensors, better detection, more instrumentation. It is the story the industry knows how to act on.

It is not what happened. Everything was seen. The signals were captured across five layers, correlated correctly by a system built to correlate them, and rendered into an accurate picture of an attack in progress, inside the organization being attacked, while it was being attacked.

And the picture sat below a line.

The gap in this incident is not between what was visible and what was hidden. It is between understanding and action — a gap that no additional detection closes, because detection was never the thing that failed.

The gate stays shut. The marks on the right keep coming.
