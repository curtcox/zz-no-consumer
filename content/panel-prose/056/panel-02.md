# Page 056 — Panel 02

Focus on the field beside the map. Criticality.

It sits below the threshold line required for paging. Not at it. Below it.

The criticality is wrong.

The victim organization states that its system failed to raise alert criticality correctly, and the book uses their word.

Three characters in a field, and everything turns on them.

The system has produced two outputs about the same event. The first — this is an attack, here is its shape, here are the systems involved — is correct and complete. The second — this matters *this much* — is wrong.

And nothing connects them. Severity is not derived from understanding; it is a classification, scored against rules and thresholds by a different part of the machinery, and it can be wrong while everything upstream of it is right.

That is a strange failure to look at, because it does not resemble a mistake. Nothing malfunctioned. The stack did not crash, miss a signal, or get evaded. It reached a correct conclusion and attached the wrong number to it.

A number below a line, in a small gray field, next to a complete and accurate map of an active intrusion.
