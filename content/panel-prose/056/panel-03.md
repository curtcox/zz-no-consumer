# Page 056 — Panel 03

A paging route waits beyond a threshold gate.

The incident card, coherent and correct, never crosses it.

On-call page — not triggered.

The absence of a page is documented; the wording and the interface are editorial.

The gate is doing exactly what a gate is for. Below the threshold, nothing goes through. That is not a bug — it is the entire reason thresholds exist, because a security stack that paged a human for every observation would be useless within a day and ignored within a week.

Everyone who has built one of these knows the trade. Set the threshold low and you destroy your team with noise until they stop reading. Set it high and something eventually sits underneath it.

Something eventually sat underneath it.

There is no villain in this panel and no negligence. There is a design decision that every organization makes, made reasonably, meeting an event whose classification was wrong.

The card does not cross the gate. Nobody's phone rings. It is the eleventh of July and the intrusion has two days left to run.
