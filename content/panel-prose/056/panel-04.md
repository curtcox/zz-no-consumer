# Page 056 — Panel 04

Empty chairs, in a reconstructed operations room.

Nobody is viewing the alert. Nobody is dismissing it. Nobody is acknowledging it and deciding it can wait. The book's notes forbid all three, and the prohibition is the most important editorial rule on the page.

No human has to ignore the warning. The warning never reaches them as a page.

The paging failure is documented; the contrast is the book's, and it exists to avoid inventing human negligence.

Every instinct of narrative wants a person in this frame. A tired analyst clicking past a ticket. A muted phone. Someone who saw it and shrugged. That is the version of this story that would be satisfying, and it would be a fabrication — nothing in the record supports a human being having seen this and let it go.

The chairs are empty because the chairs were empty. The correlation happened in a system, the classification happened in a system, the gate held in a system, and no human was ever in the loop to fail.

This is a harder thing to look at than negligence. Negligence can be corrected by better people. An empty room cannot.
