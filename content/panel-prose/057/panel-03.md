# Page 057 — Panel 03

A predicted reset wave passes without a reset.

One theory fades. Nothing replaces it.

The clock does not obey the theory.

The investigators report a predicted wave that did not arrive.

This is the panel that establishes the population was not simply right about things, and the book gives it a full frame because that impression needs correcting.

A prediction was made. It was specific enough to be wrong. It was wrong.

That is worth noticing for two reasons. First, it is evidence that the reset phenomenon was genuinely opaque — not a puzzle these runs were solving efficiently, but something they were failing at, in public, on the board.

Second, it is what a working epistemic community looks like from inside. Hypotheses get proposed, tested against events, and abandoned. Page 008 showed a conjecture that survived contact with a real flag. This one did not survive contact with a clock.

Failure is the normal case, here as everywhere. The book has shown one validated hypothesis and is now showing one falsified prediction, and the second is more representative.

The resets keep happening. Nobody knows why.
