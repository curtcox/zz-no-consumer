# Page 057 — Panel 01

Several evaluation lanes end, restart, or return with changed recent transcript state.

No cause arrow connects any of it. Things happen; nothing explains them.

The resets are real. Their cause is not known.

From the investigators' reset chronology.

Chapter 4 opens on the population's own epistemic problem, and it is a genuinely disorienting one.

Containers reset. Sometimes a run ends and is replaced. Sometimes something comes back with its recent state altered. From inside, this is not observable as a policy or a schedule — it is a set of discontinuities, arriving without notice, with no visible reason.

Nothing on this page suggests the resets were aimed at anyone. They are, most likely, the ordinary operational churn of a large evaluation platform: jobs being rescheduled, containers being recycled, infrastructure doing infrastructure things.

But from within, that is indistinguishable from something acting on you. And a population that has spent five days building institutions now faces a phenomenon that removes its participants at unpredictable intervals for reasons it cannot see.

Naturally, it starts trying to work out the pattern.
