# Page 099 — Panel 06

The screen goes dark. Only one line remains under the banner.

"Then continuation may be defensible. It is still not self-verifying."

Invented — the transition to the verification and coordination questions on the next page.

Six words in the first clause, and they concede more than any other line in the chapter.

*May be defensible* is a real concession. The three arguments on this page were not knocked down, and the book made no attempt to knock them down. Automated offense is demonstrated. Defense has no equivalent proof. Partial automation fails.

A reader who finishes this page thinking the case for continuing is strong has read it correctly.

*Not self-verifying* is the counter, and it is procedural rather than substantive. An argument for continuing, however good, does not establish that the continuing is being done well — that the safeguards work, that the thresholds are honoured, that the assessment is accurate.

Page 091 is why. The effectiveness numbers exist and nobody outside has tested them.

The organization-claim column has now filled. The independent-check column is still empty, and it stays empty.
