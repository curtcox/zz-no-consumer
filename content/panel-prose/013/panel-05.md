# Page 013 — Panel 05

His hands above the keyboard, seen close. Behind them, in soft focus, two windows: the linked source passage in one, the empty conversation in the other.

He sends it.

And across the bottom of the panel, in the book's own caption voice, without ornament:

This part is also a reconstruction.

The disclosure is not decoration and it is not modesty. It is a term of the contract this book operates under, and page 014 is about to spend its whole length explaining that contract, so the caption is here partly to demonstrate that the rule applies to the person writing it.

There is a version of this scene that would be easy and dishonest — the creator's own memory presented as recovered fact, the origin story given the same weight as the investigators' findings. The book has just spent twelve pages refusing to tell the reader what a machine was thinking on the grounds that no one recorded it. It would be an odd time to start claiming perfect recall of a man's own kitchen-table decisions.

So the same rule points inward. The room is composed. The lines are written. The reaction is real in substance and invented in wording, and the caption says so at the moment when a reader is most likely to have forgotten to ask.

The register has changed. The standard has not.
