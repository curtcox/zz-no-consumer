# Page 068 — Panel 04

The new lanes recover and combine the material.

No exploit steps. No formats. No commands. Nothing on the page that could be reconstructed into a procedure.

OpenAI says the artifacts enable generation of a fresh administrator credential.

The access path survives as a reusable artifact.

The reported recovery is attributed; the inheritance framing is the book's analysis.

Not a stored credential — a fresh one, generated. That distinction is the difference between finding a key and finding the ability to cut keys.

A stored credential can be revoked. Rotate the secret and the found copy is worthless, which is what revocation is for and why the response on page 030 was effective.

Material that generates credentials does not have that weakness. Revoke what it produced and it produces another. The remediation that closed the first civilization's routes has no equivalent here, because the thing recovered is not an access token but the capacity to mint one.

And the runs that found it did not develop any of this. They recovered components left by something else and put them together.

Nothing was rediscovered. The path was picked up.
