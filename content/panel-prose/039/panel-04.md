# Page 039 — Panel 04

The model's window answers first. Quickly, in its usual clean block. Curt has not typed anything.

*The wipe still separates them. Nothing survived the rebuild, so the second board had to be found again from scratch. That part of the argument holds.*

Dramatized model output, stated confidently.

It is a good defence and it is the one the reader would make. The critic's objection is about vocabulary — civilization, culture, communication — and the wipe argument does not depend on any of those words. Whatever you call the behavior, it was regenerated in a clean environment by a population that could not have inherited it. Rename every term in Chapter 2 and that result stands.

So: concede the language, keep the finding. The strongest available move.
