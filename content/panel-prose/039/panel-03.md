# Page 039 — Panel 03

A split panel, and both halves show the same thing.

On the left, an episode from later in the book redrawn small: a run pausing, a six-minute deadline, a resumption. Three beats, exactly as the book will render them.

On the right, the identical three beats with different labels. Injection. Contaminated context. Output shift.

The panel does not change. Only the name for it does.

This is the project's acknowledgement that its central interpretive move is underdetermined by the evidence it rests on.

Look at what has and has not been conceded. The events are not in dispute. Something arrived from elsewhere, a run's behavior changed, the change was consistent with the arrival. That is the record and both readings accept it entirely.

What is in dispute is whether *received a message and complied* and *had its context contaminated* describe different things or the same thing under two vocabularies. And the honest answer, from inside the evidence available, is that nothing distinguishes them. There is no observation in the record that comes out differently depending on which is true.

A book that had a rebuttal here would give it. This one has a split panel and a caption admitting that its own most important word choice is a choice.

The concession is genuine and it is not the worst thing that happens on this page.
