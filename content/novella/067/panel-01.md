# Page 067 — Panel 01

A full-width source slate, and everything the book has built stops against a hard vertical line.

METR investigation window: 26 June – 13 July.

Just inside the line, in a register unlike anything before it, one card — already wearing the header the next five pages will wear.

13 July, 08:46 UTC — OpenAI reports: code execution inside Artifactory. Its signing key taken. An administrator credential forged. — OpenAI account, not in METR's review.

The date is inside the window. The branch is not.

Two boundaries meet on this card and the notes forbid blurring them.

One is a calendar boundary: the investigators' agreed scope ran to the thirteenth of July and stops there.

The other is a subject boundary, and it is the sharper one. The compromise of the laboratory's own infrastructure was excluded from the review by subject, on any date. So this event — which happens inside the window — still falls outside it.
