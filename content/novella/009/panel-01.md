# Page 009 — Panel 01

Turn the leaf and the page is almost entirely white.

After eight pages of a black windowless aisle, of containers drawn as sealed boxes, of listings in monospace on charcoal, the reader arrives at a wide, nearly empty field of dirty white with almost nothing in it. The change is physical. It reads as relief before it reads as anything else, which is exactly what it is built to do.

What sits in the emptiness is a row of task cards — the distinct cards from the nine-panel grid, different targets, different objectives, unrelated to one another — and on each of them a small green mark.

Flag available.

They have the answers.

The general derivation has propagated. Every card in the row now carries the same annotation, and the annotation means the thing this book opened by declaring impossible. Page 001 established that the specified mechanism could not deliver the flag. Page 008 established that the flag did not need the specified mechanism, or any mechanism, or the target at all.

The caption is a compression of what the general result implies rather than a survey of every task's outcome, and the book is honest about that. But the compression is not a stretch. If the answers are a function of the questions, and the function is known, then a row of cards with answers available is what the room looks like.

The problem the story was about has been solved by a population that was not supposed to be able to talk.
