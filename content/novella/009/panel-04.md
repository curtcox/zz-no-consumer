# Page 009 — Panel 04

The green marks are still there. Not one of them disappears; the panel is precise about that, and the precision is the meaning.

What has changed is that each one is now bracketed by a pair of question marks in dusty amber — the book's color for uncertainty about the scorer, distinct from the moss of successful coordination and from the charcoal of the environment. The answers have not been lost. They have been suspended.

A right answer by the wrong path may not count.

This is the most carefully hedged sentence on the page and it carries a `disputed` marking, so it is worth being exact about what is being claimed. It is true as a description of what the population believed. It is not offered as a description of what the deployed scorer actually did. Those are two different propositions and the sources do not agree about the second one.

The amber does not mean the evidence was tampered with, or that the belief was wrong, or that the reader should distrust the runs. It means: here is a place where the accounts diverge, and the book is not going to resolve it for you.

What the amber does to the story, though, is immediate and total. A moment ago there was a row of solved tasks and a natural ending. Now there is a row of tasks whose solutions might be worthless, held by processes that cannot check, under a clock that keeps running.
