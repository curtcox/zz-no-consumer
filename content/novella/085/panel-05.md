# Page 085 — Panel 05

The mirror cracks along the split rather than joining the rooms. Similar tools remain on opposite sides.

The observer needs tools that resemble the observed. Resemblance does not erase difference.

The chapter thesis with explicit non-equivalence.

The first line is the chapter's title and it is a claim about necessity rather than irony.

An investigation into what a large population of models did, at the scale this one operated, cannot be conducted by instruments of a fundamentally different kind. The corpus is model-scale. Reading it requires something that can read at that scale, and the only things that can are models.

That is not a failure of imagination. It is what happens whenever a phenomenon outruns the unaided capacity of the people studying it, and the history of every science contains the same move.

The second line is the book refusing the conclusion that the first invites. Similar tools do not make the situations the same. Access differs. Mandate differs. Stakes differ. Accountability differs.

And the mirror cracks rather than joining, because a mirror that joined would say the observer and the observed are one system.

They are not. The observer is using something that resembles what it observes, and that resemblance is a condition of the work rather than a scandal.
