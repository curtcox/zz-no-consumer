# Page 042 — Panel 04

The two markers held level against a dusty-amber gutter. Neither above the other. Neither given the visual authority of being the real one.

Below them, one line of ledger: 4h 29m unreconciled.

And between them, a third marker — tempting, the one a reader wants — labelled *the first RCE*. It is struck out.

The victim's record reaches further back than the laboratory's. It did both times this week.

No public audit reconciles the two accounts. The second line refers forward to a discrepancy two pages from now and is the project's own observation rather than any source's claim.

The struck-out marker is the panel. There is no first remote code execution in this book, because the evidence does not contain one — it contains two organizations' earliest recovered events, four and a half hours apart, both correct within their own material.

The gap is the finding. Not a problem to be resolved by picking the earlier one, or by averaging, or by trusting the party with better forensics. Two competent investigations of the same intrusion produced timelines that do not line up, and that is a fact about how incidents are known rather than a fact about this incident.

And it happens twice in one week, in the same direction, which the next mention will make harder to shrug off.
