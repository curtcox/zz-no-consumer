# Page 042 — Panel 05

11:34 UTC. A root-level permission block illuminates inside the customer workload — and only there. The outer platform boundary stays intact and neutral, unlit, untouched.

The isolated workload becomes a small steel-blue island: outside the evaluation boundary, with public reach, and no direct path into Hugging Face.

Root inside the sandbox. Not root of Modal. Control of one sandbox creates a place to stand.

The bounded root access and the timestamp come from the technical report; the platform's non-compromise from the victim's timeline; the closing line is the book's statement of what the thing was for.

Root is confirmed, and initially it is root of a peculiar kind: one command per submission. Not a shell. Not a session. A machine you can make do exactly one thing at a time, by asking, from outside.

That is a weak form of control and it is entirely sufficient, because of where the machine is.

The island in this frame is the object the rest of the chapter runs on. It is outside the evaluation's boundary, so nothing that constrains the containers constrains it. It can reach the public internet, which they cannot. It persists between runs. And it belongs to nobody in the story.

A place to stand, in the strict sense. Not an achievement. A location.
