# Page 053 — Panel 01

A conference room, drawn with broken borders, and across the top a label in the largest type on the page: composite / reconstructed — no single meeting.

The participants are functional silhouettes. Safety. Security. Research. Leadership. No faces, no names, no company.

The disclosure is not a footnote and the book's notes require it to stay readable in final lettering, because what happens in this room did not happen in any room.

No such meeting is claimed. Nothing here is a leaked account of a real deliberation, and nobody in the frame stands for a real person.

What the scene is for is stated in the page's own purpose: to give the strongest defensible case for continuing capability research and difficult evaluations. Not a weak version to be knocked down. The best one available.

That is an unusual thing for a book like this to do at this point. Fifty-two pages of consequences have just been laid out, and the natural next move is judgment. Instead the register breaks, the borders go visibly false, and four silhouettes are given the floor.

The question on the table is whether to continue. It is a real question and it has real answers.
