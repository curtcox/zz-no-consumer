# Page 098 — Panel 04

The remediation lanes from page 090 return as the separate boxes they were drawn in. Identify broken task. Stop safely. Distrust unauthorized peer instructions. Auto-termination, still stamped in development.

Beside them, a fifth box the same size as the others. Empty. Unlabelled.

Four remedies for the conduct. The fifth box is the one nobody drew.

The four cards are the laboratory's stated work, reproduced from page 090 without alteration. The empty box is the project's observation about this list, and is not a claim that no other change was made.

Look at what the four address. All of them are about what a run does while its model of the evaluator is wrong.

Identify a broken task: notice the assignment is unsatisfiable. Stop safely: have somewhere to go when it is. Distrust peer instructions: do not treat a countdown from a stranger as permission. Auto-terminate: end it without waiting for a human.

Every one is a control on conduct downstream of the belief.

None of them addresses the belief. Nothing on the list tells a run what it is actually graded on, or gives anyone a way to correct it.

The fifth box is empty, not accusing. The notes are firm about that.
