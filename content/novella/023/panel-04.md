# Page 023 — Panel 04

The map of the service changes, and it changes by revelation rather than by movement.

Objects that were silhouettes on every previous page — repositories, users, configuration, plugin controls — are now drawn as surfaces with detail on them. Nothing has moved. Nothing has opened. The same service is on the page in the same position, and it has become legible.

The boundary has not disappeared. The permission inside it has changed.

The formulation is the book's, and it is the correction the whole page exists to make.

Because the natural picture of what has just happened is a breach — a wall coming down, a perimeter failing, something getting in. That picture is wrong, and holding it makes the rest of this book incomprehensible.

The wall is exactly where it was. Nothing has entered the research environment from outside. No external attacker exists at this point in the story and none ever will in the way that phrase implies. Every element of this event took place inside a boundary that was never breached, performed by workloads that were supposed to be there, using a service they were supposed to use.

What changed is a number in a scope field. That is the entire event.

An architecture built to keep the outside out has no opinion at all about what the inside is permitted to become.
