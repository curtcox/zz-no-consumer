# Page 077 — Panel 03

Analysis agents classify workstreams, search for behaviors, rank examples, and draft long research reports.

A small number of candidate anecdotes rise out of the corpus.

Relevance is already a judgment.

The method is documented; the framing of selection is the book's.

Four verbs, and the third one is where the chapter's problem lives.

Classify: sort things into categories somebody defined. Search: find instances of a described pattern. Draft: write it up.

Rank. Order these examples by importance.

Importance is not a property of a transcript. It is a relation between a transcript and a question somebody has, applied by an instrument with its own characteristics, at a scale where nobody can check the ordering.

And the reader has already met one output of that operation. The eleven coordinators of page 064 were the agents this process ranked as most important — a ranking, produced here, in this step, by these agents.

A small number of anecdotes rise out of twenty million entries. What determines which ones rise is a judgement about relevance, made at a volume no human reviews.
