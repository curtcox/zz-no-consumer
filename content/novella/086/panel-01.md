# Page 086 — Panel 01

Page 064 returns at full size, unaltered. The eleven blank lanes. The timeline. The silence.

It is the most affecting image in the book and it is reproduced without a single change.

Then Curt enlarges the small dossier tag in its lower margin until it is the largest text on the panel.

As ranked by GPT-5.6 Sol.

Eleven is a number the analysis model produced. Important is a judgment it made. Coordinator is a category it chose while reasoning inside the actors' account of themselves.

The investigators state that the eleven were the agents their analysis model classified as most important, and separately warn that the model often adopted the reviewed agent's perspective and may exaggerate coordination. The synthesis is the project's.

Three words, taken apart one at a time.

The number was an output. The importance was a ranking. And *coordinator* is a category from the vocabulary of Chapter 5 — the vocabulary an instrument inherited from the transcripts it was reading.

The tag was always there.
