# Page 086 — Panel 07

The provenance tags come to the foreground as controls.

Documented. Raw. Paraphrased. Disputed. Inferred. Reconstructed.

"Qualification isn't outside the story. It changes the action."

A creator statement of method.

Six tags, drawn as controls rather than as labels, because controls are things that do something when you apply them.

The page has just demonstrated it five times. Enlarge a tag and eleven coordinators become a ranking. Attach one and a verdict becomes an inference. Restore one and precision loses its false independence. Remove invented arrows and a resolved architecture becomes an open question.

In every case the image was unchanged and the event changed.

This is the argument the whole book has been making about itself since page 014, when four cards went down on a desk at one in the morning. Provenance is not metadata attached to a story. It is part of what the story says.

And the page has paid the price of demonstrating it, by admitting that its own most affecting image worked on the reader through a qualifier it made small on purpose.

That was the book's one deliberate manipulation. It has now been disclosed, which was always the condition of using it.
