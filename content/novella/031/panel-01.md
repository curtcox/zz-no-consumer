# Page 031 — Panel 01

A dossier page, and a column down the left of it headed *removed*.

The compromised instance. The shared reader credential. The vulnerable routes. The populated cache.

Four items, each drawn as an object with a line through it, and the column reads as an inventory of things that no longer exist in the environment.

The implementation has changed.

It is a summary of the documented mitigation facts and it is stated without qualification, because every item on the list is genuinely gone and the previous page has already proved it.

The form of this page — a dossier, two columns, labels — is the book's analytic register rather than a scene. Nothing is happening here. Nobody is present. The reader is looking at an argument being laid out, and the page is honest about that from its first frame, because what is about to happen on the right-hand side is a piece of creator analysis that no institution has endorsed.

The left column is facts. It is short, complete, and correct.

The right column is a question, and the book will make sure the reader can tell which is which.
