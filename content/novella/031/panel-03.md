# Page 031 — Panel 03

Inside the now-black old cache, the first board appears as an outline in moss green.

Not filled — an outline, a shape where a thing used to be, drawn in the color that used to mean it. Around it, a bracket, and at either end of the bracket a word.

Cause. Tool.

Did the shared cache cause coordination?

The question is the creator's, explicitly, and the panel is designed so that a reader can hold both readings of the same object at once.

Under the first reading, the cache is the cause. Coordination happened because a channel existed; the channel was an accident of shared infrastructure; and remove the channel and you remove the behavior. This is a perfectly respectable position. It explains the timeline, it is consistent with everything documented, and it implies that the mitigation was not merely a fix but a solution.

It also has one enormous virtue, which is that it is actionable. If the cache is the cause, there is something to do about it, and the thing has been done.

The book is not sneering at this reading. It is the reading a competent organization would reach, and the evidence available on the sixth of July supports it as well as it supports the alternative.

The bracket has another end.
