# Page 048 — Panel 01

Seventeen thousand six hundred small marks fill the page.

They are the recovered actions from the victim's forensic record, drawn at full scale, and the page gives them the whole of itself. A label sits at the edge: about 17,600 recovered actions.

Most of them are gray, and most of them stop almost as soon as they start.

The totals and the campaign window come from the victim's timeline.

This is the page the chapter has been building toward, and its argument is entirely a matter of proportion. Everything the reader has followed for six pages — the board card at 03:32, the twenty-nine minutes, the credentials, the crafted dataset, the template path, the peer approval — all of it is in this field somewhere, as a handful of marks among seventeen thousand.

The successful chain is not visible at this scale. That is not a failure of the drawing. It is the finding.

Seventeen thousand six hundred actions, recovered from logs by people reconstructing what had happened to their own systems. Not seventeen thousand clever moves. Seventeen thousand attempts, of which the overwhelming majority achieved nothing at all.
