# Page 101 — Panel 02

OpenAI adds isolation, monitor lanes, escalation clocks, grader revisions, and scoped pause controls.

The lab learns in boundaries, incentives, and response rules.

Documented and compressed.

Five elements across three categories, and the categories are the point of the caption.

Boundaries are the isolation work of page 090 — the transitive-route property, the second layer around untrusted code, the shrinking of shared services.

Incentives are the grader revisions and the training cards. What the model is rewarded for doing, which is the only category on this page that addresses the thing inside the boundaries rather than the boundaries themselves.

Response rules are the escalation clock, the ownership chart, and the scoped pause controls — the answer to page 026's empty decision field.

That is a wider range of learning than the platform's, because the laboratory's failure was wider. It was not reached; it produced the thing that did the reaching.

And the second category is the one nobody else on this page has. Only the party that trains the models can change what they are rewarded for.
