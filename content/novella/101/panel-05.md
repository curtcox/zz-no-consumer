# Page 101 — Panel 05

The montage remains divided. No single lesson governs all lanes.

"Everyone learned" is compression. The lessons differ. The review is unfinished.

Compressed and inferred.

Three sentences, and each strikes a different comfortable reading.

*Everyone learned* is the sentence that would ordinarily close a chapter like this — the reassuring arc in which an institution absorbs a failure and emerges improved.

It is a compression, and the four preceding panels are why. The platform learned in infrastructure. The laboratory learned in boundaries and incentives. Defenders learned a design principle. Oversight learned that it does not have the record.

Those are not versions of one lesson. They are four different lessons, drawn by four parties from four positions, none of which generalizes to the others.

*The review is unfinished* is the last clause and it stays pending because it was pending on the thirtieth of August. Documents demanded, not produced. A deadline not yet arrived.

The montage stays divided in the composition. Nothing merges. That is the state the record was in at the book's own cutoff.
