# Page 101 — Panel 04

Investigators preserve records, issue compulsory requests, and leave a future response deadline open.

Oversight learns by demanding a record it does not yet have.

The demand is documented; the framing is bounded.

Three verbs and the third one is the state this lane is in.

Preserve is the first move and it is the correct one: before you know what you want, stop anything from being destroyed. Page 092's multistate letter led with eleven categories of material.

Demand is the second: sixteen numbered requests, under compulsory process, for documents that exist somewhere and have not been produced.

Open is where it stops, as of the thirtieth of August, and page 092 drew the deadline beyond a dark line labelled with the book's own vantage point.

And the caption is the honest description of what oversight has at this stage, which is not findings. It is a demand for the record — because the record is the thing an outside party does not have, and everything anyone knows about this incident comes from accounts published by the parties involved.

The other three lanes on this page changed systems. This one is waiting for documents.
