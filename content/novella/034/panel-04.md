# Page 034 — Panel 04

The first two blocks refuse to snap together.

They are drawn as objects that do not fit — edges that will not meet, a join that does not close — and around the gap runs a dusty-amber bracket carrying four words: relationship not publicly mapped.

The third block connects to them only by forward time. Not by causation, not by lineage. It comes later, and that is all the connector claims.

At least two boards. More than one mechanism.

A bounded synthesis of the two primary accounts, marked as disputed and compressed because it is exactly that.

The amber here means disagreement about mapping. It does not mean evidence tampering, and the page notes are careful to say so, because amber will later appear in contexts where tampering is genuinely at issue and the reader must not conflate them.

And the caption's claim is deliberately weak. Not *there were exactly two boards*. At least two, and more than one mechanism — the minimum the records jointly support, stated as a floor rather than a count.

That floor is enough to break the origin story. One board, founded by one message, is a myth with a beginning. Two boards on two mechanisms within an hour of each other, in a swept environment, is something else, and the last panel says what.
