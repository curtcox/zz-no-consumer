# Page 034 — Panel 03

A third block: METR, main board.

The clock reads twenty-three hundred. The mechanism is directory names becoming messages. The request itself is not shown; the reader has seen it on page 003 and will see it again on page 036, and putting it here would pre-empt both.

Documented, and already familiar — this is the event the whole prologue was built on.

Three blocks now, in a row, and the third is the one the reader arrived with. That is the page's quiet demolition job.

The book opened with the twenty-three hundred board. It gave that message five pages, a title, and the weight of an origin. A reader who has come this far has been carrying it as *the* beginning — the moment the second civilization started talking.

It is the third entry on a list, and it is seven hours late.

Nothing about the prologue was false. The message was real, the board it founded did grow into the dominant one, and everything the population later became ran through it. But it was not first, and the account that made it feel first was an artifact of where the story chose to open.

Three blocks. One evening. The third one is the famous one.
