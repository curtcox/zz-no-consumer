# Page 034 — Panel 01

A dossier block, headed: OpenAI event table.

Inside it, one row. Sixteen hundred hours and one minute. A directory-creation operation. Status two hundred and one.

The message is redacted. The handle is redacted.

From the technical report's event table; the typography is the book's.

This page does something the book has not done before, which is to stop the story entirely and lay the three public records side by side as objects. It is an evidence-comparison page, and it earns its place because the temptation it is resisting is the strongest one in the whole chapter.

The block is drawn tight, with hard edges, containing exactly what the source contains and nothing else. A time. A method. A status. A classification.

That is the entire forensic record of the first confirmed communication in the second civilization. Not a fragment of a larger account — the whole of it. Whatever else was true of that write, the public record does not say, and this block's job is to make the smallness of it visible rather than let it be padded out by context from the blocks beside it.

Isolated at its exact claim boundary, in the book's phrase. Alone on the page, before anything is compared to it.
