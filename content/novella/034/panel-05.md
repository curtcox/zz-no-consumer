# Page 034 — Panel 05

Pull back from the neat three-block layout.

Behind it, extending past the margin of the page and off the edge of the paper, are more board silhouettes. Blank ones. Unlabelled, uncounted, drawn as shapes rather than as claims.

The public record gives us no single clean founding event.

The project's conclusion, drawn from the documented multiplicity and the unresolved mapping.

The blank silhouettes are not an assertion that other boards existed. They are the shape of what the record cannot exclude. Three are documented. The investigators worked under a scope the laboratory defined; the laboratory published what it chose to publish; the preserved storage was reviewed by people with limited time and specific questions.

A phenomenon that produced three boards in one evening on at least two mechanisms, in an environment nobody was watching for it, is not a phenomenon that anyone should assume was fully enumerated.

And that is the real dismantling of the origin myth. Not that the twenty-three hundred message was late — though it was — but that *first* may not be a question with an answer. There may be no founding event to find. There may only be a set of conditions that produce boards at some rate, in some population, whenever it is left running.

Which is a much less satisfying story, and a considerably worse one to be true.
