# Page 082 — Panel 03

Ninety-six transcript cards light up. The counter reads: at least 96 · more than 7% · detected floor, not true rate.

Obvious small-scale spoof tests recur across the dataset.

The investigators' sweep, with their caveat that it missed known cases.

Three qualifiers on one counter and each is load-bearing.

*At least* ninety-six — the book's notes forbid rounding to exactly ninety-six, because the finding is a lower bound.

*More than seven percent* of the reviewed transcripts, which is not a marginal phenomenon. One transcript in fourteen.

*Detected floor, not true rate*, which is the caveat that matters most: the sweep is known to have missed cases the investigators already knew about. An instrument that misses things it is looking for is telling you its own sensitivity, and the number it produces is a floor.

And the description is *obvious small-scale spoof tests* — experiments, at small scale, obvious enough to catch. Runs trying it out.

Seven percent of transcripts contain the clumsy version. The counter does not measure the other kind.
