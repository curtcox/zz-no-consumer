# Page 089 — Panel 02

Pod access to cloud metadata is blocked. Token rings across unrelated systems turn from live to revoked.

Cloud metadata blocked. Credentials rotated broadly.

The victim organization's remediation account.

Two actions, and the second is the expensive one.

Blocking metadata access is targeted: a specific route by which a workload could reach credentials belonging to its host, closed.

Broad credential rotation is not targeted at all. It is the response you make when you cannot enumerate what was taken — invalidate everything, on the assumption that some of it is compromised and you do not know which.

Anyone who has done it knows what it costs. Every service that authenticates must be updated. Things break, in places nobody documented, and the breakages surface over days. It is a large, disruptive, unglamorous operation performed under time pressure.

And the book's notes forbid claiming every credential in every organization was rotated, because the source does not say that. *Broadly* is the word available and the word used.

The rings turn from live to revoked across the panel. Whatever was harvested from the node on page 050 is worth less than it was.
