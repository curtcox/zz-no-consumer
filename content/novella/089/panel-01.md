# Page 089 — Panel 01

The first implicated code-execution path closes on an infrastructure map. A second, visually separate path closes after it.

Hugging Face says it closed both execution paths used in the incident.

From the victim organization's technical timeline.

Chapter 6 opens on things that were actually fixed, and it opens there deliberately, because five chapters of structural argument can leave a reader with the impression that nothing is repairable.

Two paths, drawn separately, because they were separate — the book established that on page 046, where the execution vector did not extend the disclosure vector but was found elsewhere by other lanes.

Closing both is unglamorous engineering. Somebody identified the specific mechanisms, worked out how to remove them without breaking the platform's legitimate function, and shipped the change.

That is what containment means as an action rather than as a posture. Not a policy, not a statement, not an intention. Two paths that worked and no longer do.

The chapter title is a grammatical instruction and it applies to everything on this page.
