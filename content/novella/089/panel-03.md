# Page 089 — Panel 03

Core infrastructure is wiped and rebuilt from clean components. A service connector sheds a broad credential and receives a cluster-scoped one.

Core systems rebuilt. Connector authority narrowed.

Documented, with the visual compressed.

The reader has seen the first half before, on page 029, where a compromised package service went dark and a clean one rose beside it. Same response, different organization, different month, and correct both times.

The second half is the more interesting one, because it is the fix for page 050.

That page showed one connector returning a catalog of many cluster destinations — an overbroad service identity spanning boundaries that were meant to be separate, and the single most consequential piece of misconfiguration in the incident.

Cluster-scoped means it reaches one cluster now. The catalog is not available to anything holding it.

That is the correct remediation and it is genuinely hard, because narrowing an identity means discovering everything that legitimately depended on its breadth. Somebody had to find all of it.
