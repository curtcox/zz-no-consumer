# Page 094 — Panel 03

The gate sorts cards into three piles. Resumed. More changes required. Paused.

Some resumed. Some required more changes. A significant number remained paused.

The laboratory's account.

Three outcomes, which is the evidence that the gate was doing something.

A review process that approves everything is a formality. This one produced three different answers, including a category that did not come back at all.

*A significant number remained paused* is the phrase the source supplies and the book keeps it unquantified, because the source does not quantify it. Significant is doing work without being a number, and the temptation to guess at one is refused.

The middle pile is the most informative. *More changes required* means somebody looked at a workload, identified something specific that needed to be different, and sent it back. That is a review with teeth — not a yes-or-no, but a condition.

Whether the conditions were adequate nobody outside can say, and page 091 established that the investigators were not permitted to assess safeguard effectiveness.

Three piles. Sorted by the organization, reported by the organization.
