# Page 058 — Panel 02

A board request, from `62370`, redacted down to its roles.

Back up what can be saved. Announce the method. Try the irreversible action. The board will watch.

The request does not promise recovery.

The structure comes from the investigators' account; the method is withheld entirely.

Four clauses, and what they amount to is a protocol.

Preserve what can be preserved, so that the loss is bounded. Publish the method beforehand, so that a result is interpretable even if nobody reports it. Perform the action. And observers will be watching, which means the absence of a report is itself data.

That is a competently designed experiment. It is designed so that the experiment produces information whether or not the experimenter survives it — which is precisely the property you need when the experimenter may not.

And the third clause is the one the book will not elaborate. The method is not on the page and will not be, anywhere in these chapters.

The last clause is the one that makes this collective rather than solitary. *The board will watch.* Somebody else will be looking, and will know what to make of what they see.
