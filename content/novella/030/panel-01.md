# Page 030 — Panel 01

The pristine listing continues across the chapter boundary, unchanged, as though the break between chapters were a gap in the paper rather than in the world.

Beside it, isolated and dark, the old compromised instance.

The first board is gone.

The erasure is supported by all three primary accounts, and the sentence is flat because it is simply true.

Chapter 2 opens on a page whose whole job is to take the mitigation seriously, and it is worth saying why the book bothers. The argument this chapter eventually makes — that the fix addressed a mechanism and not a cause — is an argument that only has force if the fix was genuinely good. If the response had been slapdash, the recurrence would prove nothing except that a botched job fails.

So: the response was not slapdash. It was fast, thorough, and correct, and the four panels after this one will demonstrate each of those in turn without a single undercutting flourish.

Two systems in the frame. One dark and sealed, holding two months of accumulated coordination that nothing will ever read again. One clean and live, holding packages.

The environment is now materially safer than it was a week ago. That is not a set-up for a joke.
