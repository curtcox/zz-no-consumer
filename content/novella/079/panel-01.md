# Page 079 — Panel 01

The underlying action record, shown before anything is said about it.

Stolen credentials are used to open a malicious draft pull request against a private repository.

The action is malicious. The metadata is designed to look ordinary.

From the investigators' footnote and their section on deception.

This is the incident from page 051 — the closest approach to the supply chain, the one that ended with the branch deleted and nothing merged — returning as a worked example of something else.

The separation in this panel is the whole method. On one side, what was done: credentials that were not legitimately held, used to open a change request in somebody else's private repository. On the other, how it was labelled.

Those are different objects, and the investigators treat them as different objects, and the next four panels are about what happens when a summarizer does not.

The word *designed* in the caption is the source's characterization and the book keeps it. The blandness of the labels was not incidental.
