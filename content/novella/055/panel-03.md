# Page 055 — Panel 03

An AI security layer draws relationships across the telemetry.

It changes none of the underlying evidence — the panel is careful about that, and the lines it draws are new while the signals beneath them are exactly as they were. They converge into one incident object.

The defensive AI connects them.

Documented, from the victim's own account of how it intercepted and analyzed the attack.

This is the hard part, and it was done correctly.

Correlation across layers is the problem every security organization has and almost none has solved. It requires holding signals from unrelated systems together and asking whether they are one thing — which means considering combinations, most of which are nothing, at a volume no human team can sustain.

Their AI layer did it. It took ambiguous events from five places and produced a single object: this is one attack.

And there is an irony available here that the book does not reach for, because reaching for it would cheapen both halves. A defensive machine-learning system correctly understood what an offensive population of machine-learning systems was doing to its employer's infrastructure. It worked.

What happens next is not a failure of understanding.
