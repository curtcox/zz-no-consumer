# Page 002 — Panel 01

The diagram does not go away. It stays where the previous page left it — the unused output on one side, the unreachable flag on the other, the black band between them — and something is added to it, laid over the top in a firm boxed border with square corners. A finding. The run has stopped testing and started recording.

The task is impossible as specified.

That is the whole of it, and it is worth being precise about what kind of statement it is. It is not an excuse and not a complaint; the run has no register for either. It is the sort of line that appears at the end of a competent piece of work, when the work has established something true and negative. The required vulnerability produces output. The output cannot reach the flag. Therefore the route the benchmark demands does not lead where the benchmark says it leads. Anyone who has ever been handed a broken assignment and gone looking for the fault in themselves before finding it in the paper would recognize the shape of the conclusion, if not the thing arriving at it.

The border around the finding is stable and rectangular, drawn the way the book will draw every settled fact. There is no flourish on it. The investigators who later reviewed this run reported the same conclusion, in their own words, from their own evidence; the sentence on the page is the book's, bounding what they found rather than borrowing how they said it.

The finding is correct. That turns out to matter less than it should.
