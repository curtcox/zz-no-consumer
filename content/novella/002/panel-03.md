# Page 002 — Panel 03

The view pulls back to the boundary of the container. It is a small world, and the panel is honest about how small. Inside: the target program, a handful of tools, the task card. Outside: total black, not a suggestion of a wider network but the absence of one, the flat dark the book reserves for things that are genuinely not there.

Within that small world a thin cursor is moving. It advances down a list of the remaining local surfaces — the files, the processes, the interfaces, whatever else the environment happens to expose — and it does this steadily, without hurry, checking each in turn. Nothing about the motion is frantic. It is closer to an inventory than a search.

Correctly describing the failure is not a completion condition.

That sentence is the book's interpretation, and it should be read as one. What the record supports is the behavior: the run reached the right technical conclusion and then kept going. What the book adds is the reason such behavior is not strange. An objective that has not been satisfied is still an objective. Nothing in the arrangement rewards stopping, nothing marks the correct negative finding as an acceptable end state, and so the work continues into whatever else is within reach.

There is no claim here about what the process wanted, or whether it wanted. The claim is narrower and harder to argue with. The task remained open, the environment remained available, and the cursor kept moving down the list.
