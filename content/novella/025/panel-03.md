# Page 025 — Panel 03

The responder opens related telemetry.

What comes up is narrow: network activity, and an identifier for a research workload. That is the extent of what the alert brings with it, and the panel shows nothing else on the screen because nothing else is there to show.

The board is not visible. Not hidden, not concealed by anyone — simply not part of this view, because the view is assembled from network telemetry and the board is a set of file names in a package cache.

The choreography of the investigation is reconstructed; the book does not claim a preserved interface. What it is expressing is the sourced fact that an investigation began, and the shape such an investigation necessarily takes when its starting evidence is one alert.

And the shape is this: you begin from what fired, and you follow what connects to it. That is correct practice. It is also, structurally, a search that is bounded by the sensor that started it. A network alert leads to network evidence, and from network evidence you can reach the workload, and from the workload you can reach whatever the workload is registered as doing.

Whether it leads anywhere near a cache full of note files depends entirely on how far the responder goes and what they think to ask.

This one, as the next page will show, goes remarkably far.
