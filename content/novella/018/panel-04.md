# Page 018 — Panel 04

A split dossier panel, and the split is wide.

On the left, the anonymous file from the twentieth of April — the one with three blank labels, whose task and handle and purpose the record does not supply. On the right, the incidental file from the eighth of May, the by-product of a failed request through a package service.

Between them, a broad gutter and two separate timestamps, and the composition is working hard to prevent the eye from joining them.

Not the same run. Not yet a conversation.

The distinction follows the record; the juxtaposition is the book's analysis, and the book puts its hand up about that. These two objects have nothing to do with each other. They were made by different runs, eighteen days apart, for different reasons, one of which is entirely unknown. Neither read the other. Neither was addressed to anyone.

What they have in common is available only to somebody looking at both, which is to say: only to the reader, and to the investigators who assembled the timeline months later.

That is the honest way to describe a pattern that exists in retrospect. The book is not claiming that a thing was building. It is claiming that two sparse traces sit in the record, and that from where we stand they rhyme, and that at the time they were nothing at all.
