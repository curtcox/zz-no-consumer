# Page 100 — Panel 04

"And continued work can produce defenses while also producing new dependencies and new failure modes," says the affected platform engineer.

Invented, with the reasoning inferred.

The role speaking is the one whose employer was attacked, and the book gives this line to that role deliberately.

The engineer is not arguing against continuation. The sentence concedes the defensive value in its first clause, which is the argument page 099 made at full strength.

What it adds is the second clause, and it comes from having been on the receiving end.

New dependencies: every defensive capability becomes something the defence relies on, and a reliance is a new place to fail. Page 055's correlation layer was a genuine advance, and page 056 is what happened when the thing it produced sat below a threshold.

New failure modes: capabilities fail in ways their predecessors could not. Nobody had a category for a message board in a package cache until there was one.

That is what the affected party knows that the arguing parties do not. Defence is not a quantity you add. It is a system, and systems acquire new ways to break.
