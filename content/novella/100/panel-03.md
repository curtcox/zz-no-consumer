# Page 100 — Panel 03

"A voluntary pause can reduce one actor's risk," says the independent investigator. "It cannot, by itself, bind the field."

Invented, with the reasoning inferred.

Both halves, and the first is not a throwaway.

A voluntary pause does reduce risk. Page 093's two weeks were real: a training class stopped, the largest planned run was still held eleven days later, and researchers moved onto safeguards that produced the remediation in this chapter. Whatever risk that run carried, it was not carried during those weeks.

That matters and the panel does not sneer at it.

The second half is the assurance problem from page 096 stated by a different role in a different room. One actor stopping binds one actor. The field is not a thing anyone can pause, because it is not an entity — it is a set of organizations in several jurisdictions with their own boards, incentives, and views.

Which is precisely why the agent on page 096 said *I will hold if the others hold* and the institutional role said the same thing in different words.

Neither could issue the assurance the other was waiting for. Neither can here.
