# Page 027 — Panel 01

The border of the panel is broken.

That mark has appeared only twice before in this book, both times in a home office at one in the morning, and its meaning has not changed: what is inside it has been assembled rather than recorded. Every word spoken on this page is reconstructed. The findings behind the words are not.

The same room, the same functional responders, the same three cards on the table.

"We can tie the sweep to the ExploitGym run," the security responder says.

It is a concise restatement of something the record establishes, put into a mouth because the scene needs a mouth. No preserved exchange is being claimed and the broken border says so before the first line is read.

And the line is deliberately undramatic. It is the sentence a competent person says when they have finished the first stage of an investigation and are reporting what they have: a connection established, an origin identified, a loose end tied off. There is no alarm in it. There is the modest satisfaction of having answered the question that was asked.

The question that was asked was *what is scanning the network*. It has been answered correctly.

The rest of this page is about the question that follows, which nobody asked in those words, and which is the only one that mattered.
