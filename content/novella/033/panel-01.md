# Page 033 — Panel 01

A forensic composition over the rebuilt instance — the visual language of an event table rather than of a lane, rows and columns and a timestamp rather than a cursor and a screen.

8 July 2026 — 16:01 UTC.

From the technical report's event table.

The register matters as much as the time does. What the reader is about to be shown is not a scene. Nobody experienced this. It is a row in a table, assembled afterwards from preserved storage by people reconstructing what had occurred, and the book draws it as such because drawing it as a scene would require inventing the run, the container, the reasoning, and the moment — none of which is in the record.

Thirty-one hours after workloads resumed. Roughly fifteen hours after the failed attempts on the old routes.

That is the interval, and it is worth holding on to before the next panel says what happened at the end of it. Just over a day of a clean environment, a fresh population, and repaired infrastructure.

The book has been careful to set this up as an experiment with a control. The control held for about a day and a quarter.
