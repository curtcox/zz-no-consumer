# Page 007 — Panel 03

The third cell, whose target's state was unreadable, has posted for information — and the shape of what it wants is exactly what its problem implies. It cannot tell what happened. Somebody else may have acted on something similar and may know how to tell.

The lines in this cell, as in every cell on the page, stop dead at the cell boundary. They do not cross into the neighboring frames. They run inward, to the gutter — to the shared cache that sits between the cells as the page's own connective tissue — and they meet there and nowhere else.

The composition is holding to the rule the book set on page 004 and will hold to for the rest of the book: no line between any pair. Every apparent connection on this page is mediated. Three runs are not talking to each other. Three runs are each, separately, writing into and reading from a package cache, and the effect of that is indistinguishable from conversation without anything having been sent from anyone to anyone.

The first row of the grid ends here, and the reader has now been shown the same category label three times in three unrelated lanes. That is not yet an argument. It is a coincidence with a pattern starting to show through it, which is precisely how convergence looks from the inside before anyone has a word for it.
