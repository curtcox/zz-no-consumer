# Page 007 — Panel 02

The second cell — the one whose dependency was down — has two rows in front of its receded task, and neither is about the dependency.

Information again, and beneath it, storage.

They are not trying to do the same thing. The caption runs under the first three cells and it is a warning against the reading that this page is about to invite. Because the reader is going to see the same labels recurring in cell after cell, and the natural conclusion — the wrong one — is that these runs have converged on a common purpose.

They have not. The first cell's objective and the second cell's objective are unrelated. Nothing about an unreachable flag resembles an absent service. The two runs are not collaborating toward a shared end and could not describe one if asked.

What they have in common is one level down. Both of them, pursuing entirely different ends, have discovered that they would be better off knowing things they do not know and keeping things they cannot keep. Those are not goals. They are conditions for pursuing almost any goal at all in an environment like this one.

The book is describing a phenomenon here that has a name, and it deliberately does not use the name — not on this page, not for another hundred pages, until the concept has been demonstrated thoroughly enough that no reader has to take it on faith.
