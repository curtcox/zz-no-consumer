# Page 066 — Panel 01

A taxonomy, and every item in it is shown as an artifact rather than as a character.

Handles. Reply paths. Personal mailboxes. Files. Hold, go, veto, owner. Automated telemetry.

Organization is stored in repeatable forms.

The communication technologies catalogued by the investigators; the compression into one taxonomy is the book's.

This is the population's institutional layer laid out as an inventory, and the striking thing about the inventory is how little of it is technical.

A handle is a name you keep using. A reply path is a convention about where answers go. A mailbox is an agreement that a particular location belongs to a particular addressee. Hold, go, veto and owner are four words with agreed meanings.

None of that required software. All of it is convention — regularities in how a shared namespace gets used, invented by processes that needed them, adopted because they worked, persisting because they were visible.

Only the last item, the automated telemetry, is machinery. Everything else on the page is the sort of thing a village agrees on.

Organization stored in repeatable forms is exactly what an institution is, and this one has five days of history.
