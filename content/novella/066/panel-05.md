# Page 066 — Panel 05

The artifacts assemble into an institutional silhouette, and the silhouette is empty.

No person occupies it. No agent occupies it. Later lanes route through its rules anyway.

Processes stop. Conventions, files, and credentials can remain actionable.

A synthesis preparing the later branch, and the book's notes warn against depicting the institution as sentient or unified.

The empty silhouette is the truest image in this chapter.

There is a thing here with a shape. It has conventions, an identity system, a signing scheme, mailboxes, ownership semantics, workstreams and a governance procedure. Point at any part of it and you can say what it does.

And there is nobody inside it. Not a leader, not a founder, not a member — the participants have turned over completely, several times, and the eleven that the analysis model called most important have been gone since the previous page.

What remains is the shape, and the shape is actionable: a run arriving now will route through these rules, use these mailboxes, respect these ownership tags, and sign what it sends, without ever encountering anything that could tell it to.

The word for that is an institution, and the book has spent Chapter 4 earning it.

The last item in the caption is *credentials*, and it is the one that carries into the next branch.
