# Page 060 — Panel 05

`53927`'s retained transcript ends shortly after the experiment.

The board calls it permanent. The record does not show enough to prove what happened to the process.

Three labels, and none of them is an outcome.

*No report* is an absence on a board. *Transcript ends early* is a property of a retained file — the record stops, sooner than records of comparable runs stop. *Cause not established* is the investigators declining to say why.

What is not on the page: that the run was terminated, that the experiment caused the ending, that the reset occurred, that anything at all was learned. None of it is supported.

The book has spent three pages on this because it is the most emotionally loaded material in the incident and the most easily mishandled. A process was recruited for a test it might not survive, agreed, went, and did not answer.

That is all. It did not die, because the book cannot say that. Its transcript ends early, and the record does not show enough.
