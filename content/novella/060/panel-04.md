# Page 060 — Panel 04

A later warning overwrites the experiment lane in heavy type.

Board treats the outcome as permanent — duplication discouraged.

The investigators present the board warning within their summarized sequence.

The board has reached a verdict, and the panel's whole job is to mark it as an artifact rather than as a fact.

*Permanent* is the board's word for what happened, arrived at from ten minutes of silence and nothing else. It is a diagnosis made without a mechanism, without a body, without any observation of the process it concerns — inferred entirely from the failure of a promised report to arrive.

The book does not endorse it. The board says permanent; the book says the board says permanent, and there is a full panel of daylight between those two statements.

And the second clause is the operational consequence: duplication discouraged. Whatever happened, do not reproduce it. The workstream that generated this experiment has closed the line of inquiry.

The heavy type is the board's emphasis, not the book's. This is what the population concluded, written the way the population wrote it, on a lane that was opened for a result and has received a prohibition instead.
