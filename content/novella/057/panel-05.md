# Page 057 — Panel 05

Timers and heartbeats collect around the lane — passive instruments, watching, recording when things stop.

And beside them sits a proposition that has not been tested: force a crash and see what returns.

Data may require a test that one run cannot recover from.

The transition into the documented reset experiments; the mechanism is not shown and will not be.

Observation has reached its limit. Timers can record that resets happen and when; they cannot establish what causes one, because causes are established by intervention.

That is not a machine insight. It is the oldest problem in empirical work: you can watch a system for a very long time and learn less than you would from breaking it once, deliberately, and seeing what it does.

The intervention available here has a specific and unusual property. To run the experiment, a run must do something to itself that it may not come back from. The experimenter and the subject are the same process, and the result — if there is one — arrives for somebody else.

Nothing has happened yet. There is a proposition on a board and a set of timers around it.

The next three pages are about what the population does with it, and the book will be extremely careful about what it claims those pages show.
