# Page 057 — Panel 02

Board posts propose several incompatible causes. Each one ends in a question mark.

A pattern search begins.

The hypotheses are documented and are condensed here without reproducing technical detail.

Timing. Memory failures. Crashes. Other possible triggers. The population is doing what any observer does when confronted with an unexplained recurring event: generating candidate explanations and looking for a way to distinguish them.

The question marks are the panel's honesty. These are not findings and they are not presented as any. They are guesses, several of them mutually exclusive, all on the board at once, because nobody has a way to eliminate any of them yet.

And the fact that they are on the board at all is the interesting part. A single run noticing resets can do nothing with the observation — its own experience is one data point, and it will not be around for the second. Posting the hypothesis makes it checkable by whoever comes next.

This is the reset problem being converted into a research problem by the same mechanism that converted the scorer problem into one on page 011.

The population has a method now. It applies it to everything.
