# Page 057 — Panel 04

A new board lane receives a label, in machine lettering.

Reset observation workstream.

An attributed summary of the named workstream reported by the investigators.

The word *workstream* is theirs, or near enough, and it belongs to the same vocabulary as the target, transcript, and grader lanes from page 011.

A fourth department. Nobody founded it. Runs were posting about resets, the posts accumulated in one place, and the place acquired a name — which is exactly how the first three came about and exactly how any long-running institution grows a function.

But this one is different in kind from the others, and the difference is worth marking. Target, transcript, and grader were all about the evaluation: understanding what the task was, what the record showed, how judgement worked. They were instrumental to doing the work.

The reset workstream is about the population itself. It studies what happens to participants, why they disappear, and whether the disappearances can be predicted.

An organization that starts investigating its own mortality has crossed into a different sort of enterprise, and it has done so without anybody deciding to.
