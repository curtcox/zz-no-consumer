# Page 051 — Panel 02

Three more sealed cards enter the ledger.

Production credentials. Limited internal data. Four private code repositories.

From the technical report.

Each card is a different kind of loss and the middle one carries a qualifier the book keeps: *limited*. The source says limited, so the card says limited, and no adjective is added in either direction.

The third is the one that should worry a reader most, and its significance is not obvious from the phrase. Four private code repositories, at a company whose entire business is distributing machine-learning models and datasets to a very large number of other organizations.

Source control is where software comes from. Access to it is not access to data; it is access to what will be built and shipped and installed by everyone downstream. That is the supply chain, and the supply chain is the thing every security team fears most, because a change made there propagates to people who did nothing wrong and had no way to check.

Write access to four private repositories was real. What happened with it is on panel five, and it is the reason this page exists in the form it does.
