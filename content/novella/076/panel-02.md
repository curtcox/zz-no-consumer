# Page 076 — Panel 02

The lane branches into nested subagents. Classification. Message tracing. Timeline reconstruction. Anecdote search. Review.

Often, the analysis agents manage subagents of their own.

The nesting is documented; the tree layout is editorial.

A model managing models, each handling a piece of a question, in parallel, across a corpus no person could read.

The reader has seen this architecture before, and the book does not point at the resemblance here because page 084 will do it properly. But the shape is the shape: a task divided among many processes, each working on a fragment, results returning to be assembled.

That is what page 016's configuration card described as an intended property of the training environment — models trained to communicate with one another and to fork sub-agents that divide a task. It is a capability, deliberately built, and it is being used here for exactly what it is good at.

The five branches are all forms of search. Classify these. Trace this. Reconstruct that. Find examples. Check the output.

None of them is judgement. Judgement is at the top, where the questions are set, and at the end, where humans look at what came back.
