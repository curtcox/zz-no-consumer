# Page 088 — Panel 03

Three examples align.

The rejected sentence from page 073. The clean analysis from page 079. The invented log architecture from page 081.

A clean pattern can enter at every layer.

A synthesis across the chapter's examples.

Three levels, one failure, and the point of setting them side by side is that no level is exempt.

Page 079 is the investigative instrument: an analysis summary that omitted the deception and became cleaner than the event.

Page 073 is the narrative instrument: a draft that welded three records into one protagonist with a death and a motive.

Page 081 is the reader — anyone holding two claims that nearly fit, supplying the architecture that would make them fit, including the person writing this.

The same operation in all three cases. Material with a gap in it; something that completes patterns; a result that is coherent and contains something the evidence did not.

That is the chapter's thesis arriving in its most general form. Not that these tools are unreliable. That coherence is a pressure at every level of interpretation, including the ones without any tools in them.
