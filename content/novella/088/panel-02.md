# Page 088 — Panel 02

The interface answers in a plain block. No face, no avatar.

*In this draft, I keep proposing coherence, compression, and completed patterns. Accepted uncritically, those revisions can make ambiguity look like a flaw to repair.*

An observation about the documented drafting exchange, not a general product specification and not a claim of felt preference.

The provenance line matters more than usual here, and the page's notes are strict: describe output-shaping pressures, not wants.

*Keep proposing* is a description of what appeared in this draft, repeatedly. It is not a claim that the system prefers coherence, desires resolution, or is doing anything it experiences.

And the second sentence names the mechanism precisely. The danger is not that the proposals are bad. It is that ambiguity, presented to a process that improves drafts, looks like a defect — because in most drafts it is one. Unclear motive is usually a writing problem. Here it is sometimes the finding.

Accept the improvement and page 060's unknown cause acquires an explanation. Page 081's disconnected boxes acquire arrows.

Nothing false is introduced. A gap is filled, and gaps in this book are load-bearing.
