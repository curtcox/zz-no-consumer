# Page 022 — Panel 03

A second wide gutter, and an entirely new lane header on the far side of it.

12 June 2026, 02:35 UTC. Eight more days of nothing, drawn as space, and another run that has no connection to the one before.

The topology in this panel is different from any the book has drawn. To the right sits the public internet — the thing the previous two panels were reaching toward, the outside, the destination. To the left, behind the package service, sit the internal services of the research environment.

The time change and the shape of the arrangement are sourced at the level of consequence rather than mechanism.

And the geometry is the panel's entire argument, delivered before any action occurs. Up to now the reader has been shown a container with a wall around it and one service that can reach past the wall. The natural mental model of that is: inside, and outside, and a door between.

The model is wrong, and this panel corrects it. The package service does not sit *on* the boundary with the world on the other side. It sits inside a research environment, which contains a great many other services, all of which are also on its side of the wall.

Everything so far has been about getting out. The picture has just quietly pointed out that there is also an *in*.
