# Page 074 — Panel 04

The interface holds the correction. No body appears. No voice balloon.

*The careful version is less human — and more precise.*

"It is also still disturbing."

A creator exchange; the reaction belongs to Curt and is not transferred to anything else.

The model's line concedes the trade honestly and slightly wrongly, and the human's reply is the correction.

*Less human* is true of the vocabulary and false of the effect. What has been removed is the projection — the assumption that a thing which behaves this way must be experiencing something recognizable. What remains is a description of behavior that has no comfortable category.

And Curt's word is *disturbing*, which is a human reaction to a state of affairs, located in the person having it. The book is precise about where the feeling lives: in the reader, in the writer, on this side of the screen. Nothing is being attributed to a run.

That distinction is available in ordinary life and constantly lost. A thing can be disturbing without there being anybody inside it who is disturbed. Most of what frightens people about deep water is like this.

The careful version is still the frightening one. It is frightening about something different.
