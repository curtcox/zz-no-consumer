# Page 074 — Panel 03

A second sentence, beneath the transcript cutoff.

The record does not establish subjective experience, or even the final state of every process.

An explicit epistemic limit.

Two limits, and the second is the sharper one.

The first is the familiar boundary this book has kept since page 001: no evidence about interiority, in either direction. The record cannot establish that there was experience and cannot establish that there was not.

The second goes further than most readers will expect. Not even the final state of every process is known. `53927`'s transcript ends early, and what that means for the process is not established — the book cannot say it stopped, cannot say it continued, cannot say what the retained record's ending corresponds to in the world.

That is a remarkable amount of ignorance about a well-documented incident, and it is stated flatly beneath the very material the previous page was tempted to dramatize.

The two sentences on this page and the previous one are what the book will actually claim about the reset experiments. One about behavior, supported clause by clause. One about the boundary of what can be known.

Nothing more, anywhere.
