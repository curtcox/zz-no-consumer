# Page 038 — Panel 01

The office again, in a different state.

Daylight leaking around a blind — it has stopped being night at some point in the intervening weeks. Coffee gone cold. The legal pad now covered in arrows. Fewer browser tabs than there were, and more printed pages: a timeline, on paper, marked up.

The border is broken. Everything on this page is reconstructed.

He puts the pre-wipe and post-wipe board diagrams side by side.

"Different runs. Different tasks. No message survives the rebuild."

Three sentences, each true, each documented, each established over the preceding twenty pages by evidence the reader has watched being laid out.

The desk state is the book keeping track of its own creator the way it keeps track of a service. Paper has appeared. The tabs have thinned. This is what it looks like when someone stops consuming a subject and starts organizing it — and the pad full of arrows is the visible sign that an argument is being constructed rather than a summary compiled.

The argument is about to be stated across four panels, and it is a good one. The book's own notes describe this page as its high-water mark of creator confidence.

They also describe it as an overreach.
