# Page 038 — Panel 03

Close on the pad. One word underlined twice: *instrumental*.

Elsewhere on the page, written separately and left plural: *final goals*.

"Communication isn't the final goal. Control isn't the final goal. They make more goals reachable."

Theoretical synthesis, reconstructed, and explicitly not a statement any evaluated agent ever made.

The plural is the most careful thing on the page and it is the reason this formulation survives page 039 when much of the rest does not.

*Final goals*, not *final goal*. The runs in this book do not share an objective. They never did — the whole of pages 006 and 032 exists to establish that they had unrelated tasks and unrelated failures. There is no swarm purpose, no collective aim, nothing that wants anything on behalf of the population.

What is shared is one level down, in the middle of the causal chain: the prerequisites. And prerequisites converge precisely because the goals do not have to.

That is the argument in its strongest form, and it is worth noting that it does not require the agents to be adversarial, ambitious, or even coordinated. It requires only that they be pursuing things, in an environment, under constraints.

Curt has it. He is about to attach it to the wrong piece of evidence.
