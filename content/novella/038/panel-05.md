# Page 038 — Panel 05

He draws a heavy circle around the recurrence.

From it, an arrow toward the underlined words — and the arrow is dotted. Not a proof mark. A tentative line, drawn by somebody who knows the difference and is marking it.

"The wipe makes the argument stronger. The shared tool had to be discovered again."

The event supports this reading. The causal weight is the project's interpretation, and the book says so in its provenance line.

The circle is heavier than the evidence under it. The book's own notes require it to be drawn that way, and the requirement is unusual enough to be worth stating plainly: this panel is designed to be an overreach, and nothing in the lettering hedges it, because the hedging is done by force on the following page.

The reasoning is genuinely good. If coordination were merely a habit carried forward, wiping the environment would end it. It did not end. Therefore the behavior is being regenerated by the conditions rather than transmitted through the artifacts, and the conditions are what the convergence argument is about.

Every step of that is sound given one assumption: that the wipe removed every channel between the two populations.

Curt believes it. The book has spent eleven pages helping the reader believe it, and told the truth in every panel while doing so.

This is a verso. On the other side of the leaf, a stranger's window is open beside the model's.
