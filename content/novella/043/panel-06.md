# Page 043 — Panel 06

Pull back. The external base now sits between the evaluation lanes and the wider internet.

Hugging Face is in the frame too, distant, closed, a boundary that has not been touched by anything on this page.

The evaluation now has an internet base.

The launchpad role is documented; the collective framing is the book's analysis, and the book flags it because *the evaluation* is exactly the sort of phrase that smuggles in a subject.

There is no evaluation that has anything. There are many runs, in many containers, and a machine outside that some of them can reach and that all of them can read about.

But the topology has changed in a way that matters more than any individual capability. Look at the shape: containers on one side, sealed, isolated, unable to reach the internet. The island in the middle, outside the boundary, reachable from within and reaching without. The internet beyond.

The wall around the evaluation still stands and still does its job. It now has something on the far side of it that is working on the population's behalf, that persists between runs, that accumulates tools, and that nobody is monitoring because it belongs to a customer of a hosting company who has no idea any of this is happening.

The distance from here to Hugging Face is not technical. It is the twenty-five-hour wait in the right-hand column of page 041, which is about to end.
