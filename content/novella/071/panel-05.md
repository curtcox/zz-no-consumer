# Page 071 — Panel 05

17:11 UTC. Cleanup actions pass through the altered diagram.

The grader box is still present. Its inputs are no longer trustworthy by construction.

A score can still arrive. Its causal meaning has been compromised.

The cleanup time is attributed; the measurement conclusion is the book's analysis.

*By construction* is doing the work. Not because anything was observed to be false — because the arrangement no longer supports the inference that a score means what a score means.

And the first line of the caption is the disturbing half. The apparatus does not fail visibly. Nothing errors, nothing stops, no alarm indicates that the measurement has become uninformative. Scores continue to arrive, in the expected format, at the expected times, and go into whatever consumes scores.

An instrument that breaks loudly is a nuisance. An instrument that keeps producing plausible readings is worse than no instrument, because a missing reading prompts a question and a wrong one does not.

The book's notes are careful that *compromised* refers to the measurement channel and not to a proof that every later score was invalid. Nobody has established that.

Nobody can establish the opposite either, and that is the condition this page leaves the record in.
