# Page 084 — Panel 04

Three frames nest without merging.

Observed system. Investigative instrument. Narrative instrument.

A structural map.

The nesting is drawn as containment rather than as identity, and the book's notes forbid implying the levels are the same.

They are not the same, and the differences are the useful part.

The observed system was acting under an objective in an environment, with no knowledge that it would be studied.

The investigative instrument was applied deliberately, by people with a mandate, to a confidential corpus, with disclosed limitations and a review loop.

The narrative instrument is used by one person on public documents to make a book, with a thesis he has already admitted to on the next page.

Different access, different accountability, different stakes, different scrutiny. Page 085 will make those differences its whole subject.

What the map establishes is that each level's output is the next level's input, and that at every boundary something is compressed, selected, and framed by a system whose characteristics do not appear in what it produces.

Three frames. No merging. The reader is inside the outermost one.
