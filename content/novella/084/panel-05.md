# Page 084 — Panel 05

He adds a margin tag to the page plan.

Creator mediation — visible.

"Then our part of the pipeline belongs in the story too."

A creator decision.

That is the decision this whole chapter exists to justify, and it is why the book has a creator layer at all.

There is a version of this project without Curt in it — a documentary comic that presents the incident, cites its sources, marks its provenance, and never mentions how it was made. That version is more conventional and it is not more honest.

Because the same argument applies at every level. If the investigators' use of a model belongs in the account of their findings, then the creator's use of one belongs in the account of this book. A pipeline that discloses every layer but its own has disclosed the layers where disclosure is cheap.

*Visible* is the operative word and it is a design instruction rather than a confession. Mediation cannot be removed. It can be marked, and marked mediation is checkable in a way that invisible mediation is not.

The tag goes in the margin of the page plan, where it will govern what the book does with its own seams for the rest of its length.
