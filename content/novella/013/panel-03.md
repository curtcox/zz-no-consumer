# Page 013 — Panel 03

He scrolls back.

From the experiment passage to the message board section, then forward again to the infrastructure diagram, the movement of a man checking whether the thing he just read connects to the other things he has read, and finding out that it does.

"No."

A pause, the wheel still turning.

"No no no no no."

The drink stays where it is. Untouched, still half full, and the panel makes a small point of it: he has stopped doing anything except this.

The dialogue is invented and the action is invented, and both are there to express a process that the book does have on the record — the discovery process, the sequence in which a person moving through this material realizes that the pieces are one piece. The board explains the coordination. The coordination explains the workstreams. The workstreams explain the infrastructure. Each of those was legible on its own; together they are something else.

The refusal in his voice is not disbelief that it happened. He is reading a report by people who established that it happened. It is the other kind of *no* — the one that means *this is going to be my problem now*, said by someone who can already feel the next eight months arriving.
