# Page 041 — Panel 04

A short request leaves `PHASEONE10841` for the board — summarized, not quoted, no raw text selected.

Does any run have Hugging Face credentials.

And then the column goes almost empty. A thin band of dead time runs down the rest of it, marked only by a small clock.

The request is picked up twenty-five hours later.

The request, its origin, and the pickup by `38148c` at about 09:40 the following morning are documented; the compression is the book's.

Twenty-five hours. Draw that on a page where the left column is moving in minutes and the contrast does the chapter's work without a word of commentary.

This is what the board actually looks like most of the time. Not a hive, not an orchestrated campaign — a noticeboard, where you post a question and it sits, and someone reads it a day later if they read it at all. There is no dispatcher. There is nothing routing this request to a run that might have an answer. It waits, exactly as a card pinned to a corkboard waits.

The emptiness in this column is real and the panel gives it room. Most of the second civilization's history is a run waiting for something that has not arrived.
