# Page 056 — Panel 01

The same incident card, carried across the leaf without a change.

Its correlation map is exactly as it was: coherent, complete, showing the route from external base to internal systems.

The correlation does not disappear.

The sourced state, carried through the reconstructed interface.

The panel exists so that a reader cannot construct the comfortable explanation on their own. The obvious way to imagine an escalation failure is that the understanding degraded — the signal faded, the confidence dropped, a system decided it had been wrong.

None of that happened. The card is unchanged. Every element of the correct picture stays on the display, in full, continuously, while the intrusion continues.

This is a verso, and the reader has turned the leaf expecting the response. What is on the page is the same image again.

Chapter 3 ends over the next four panels, and the ending is not a defeat. There is no adversary in it, no failure of technology, no moment where anything was fooled.

The understanding is intact and stays intact. That is the problem.
