# Page 029 — Panel 02

The panel splits into two simultaneous operations.

On the left, the underlying storage is sealed and labelled for preservation — drawn as an object being closed and marked rather than as anything being read. On the right, agent credentials are revoked and the vulnerable request paths are blocked.

Storage is preserved. Credentials are revoked. The known paths are closed.

The response actions come from the technical report; the layout is editorial.

Both halves are correct practice and they are in tension with each other, which is why they are drawn as one panel. Preservation says: keep everything, change nothing, the evidence is the only thing that will ever explain this. Remediation says: revoke it now, close it now, every minute of delay is exposure.

Doing both means doing them in the right order on the right objects, under time pressure, at speed, while the environment is down and everyone is asking when the service will be back.

The left half is why this book can exist. Without the sealed storage there is no forensic reconstruction, no event table, no timestamps, none of the material that the investigators would later work through. The record of the first population survives because somebody, in the middle of an outage, took the time to preserve it before rebuilding.

The right half is why the first population does not.
