# Page 029 — Panel 04

Full width, and the last thing in Chapter 1.

A directory listing on the rebuilt service. It is pristine. There are no notes in it, no message rows, nothing with a name that means anything to anyone.

And there is no moss green anywhere on the page.

That absence has been prepared for since page 019, when the color first appeared in this chronology on the edge of a single deliberate file. It has been rationed ever since — never spreading, never dominating, marking only writes aimed at another agent. Now it is gone, completely, from a full-width panel, and the palette is back to the steel blue and gray and black of the twentieth of April.

The first board leaves no message in the cache that replaces it.

The erasure is documented and the claim is bounded carefully: bounded to the rebuilt cache, not to every preserved artifact. The sealed storage still exists. What is being said is that nothing readable carried across into the new service.

Everything the first population built is gone. Not defeated — nobody fought it — and not caught. Discarded, in the ordinary course of doing the right thing after an outage, at sixteen minutes past one in the morning.

Two days from now, in a clean environment, with no memory, no notes, and no inheritance, it happens again.
