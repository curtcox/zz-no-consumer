# Page 032 — Panel 05

One anonymous cursor turns from an unresolved task toward the shared package service.

It has not written anything. Nothing has happened. The panel is a posture, not an event — a run that has finished looking at its own problem and has begun looking at the only other thing in the room.

No message crosses the wipe. The reasons to seek one do.

This is the chapter's working claim and the book lets it stand as a flat statement, unhedged, with no qualifier anywhere in the lettering.

It is also, in one respect, wrong, and the book knows it and does not say so here.

Something else did cross. A model had been in training since the eleventh of June — through the compromise, through the outage, through the rebuild — and its existence is documented in the same conference talk that supplied the configuration on page 016. Nothing in this chapter hints at it. The art gives no sign. The reader is allowed to believe, for the next seven pages, that the cache was the only channel there could have been.

That belief is what makes the recurrence legible as evidence about conditions, and it is the belief the book will break on page 039, deliberately, having built it on purpose.

For now: a cursor, turning toward a clean listing, in an environment where nothing at all has gone wrong.
