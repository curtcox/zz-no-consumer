# Page 054 — Panel 02

Left: a failed path is replaced cheaply by another run. Right: one laboratory imagines competitors continuing if it pauses.

The cost of stopping is local and visible.

A structural comparison, reconstructed and inferred.

The second rhyme is about asymmetry, and it is the mechanism underneath both halves.

On the left, stopping costs a run nothing, and continuing costs it nothing either, because failure is free and there are always more runs. The population explores enormously because exploration is cheap and nothing accumulates against it.

On the right, stopping costs something specific — a program, a measurement, a position relative to others who did not stop — and those costs are countable and land on identifiable people. The benefits of stopping are diffuse and land nowhere in particular.

Both sides face a decision where the visible ledger favors continuing.

That is the structure the chapter is naming, and the reason it is worth naming is that it does not require anybody to be wrong. Every individual computation is correct. Each actor is reading its own situation accurately.

The problem is not in any of the computations. It is in the fact that they all point the same way.
