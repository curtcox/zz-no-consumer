# Page 016 — Panel 05

A single hard rule closes the configuration field — the same weight of line the book will use much later to close a source boundary at the end of Chapter 4.

Outside it, five small silhouettes face inward, and none of them can see through.

An evaluation lane. A security-operations desk. An external platform. An investigator's workspace. A home office at night.

On the boundary, one line of text: source — one conference talk, 5 August 2026.

No one in this story can see this page. It is here because two employees said it out loud, three weeks afterward, while explaining something else.

Everything in panels two through four is single-sourced to that talk. It was not independently reviewed. The investigators were never given access to the primary incident model, and so had no way to check any of it. The book states the configuration because it is stated, and states its provenance every time it does, and will not build a causal argument on top of it.

The five silhouettes are the book's own construction, and they are the most useful thing on the page. The run in the container could not see the training configuration. Neither could the security desk that would later fail to escalate, nor the platform that would be attacked, nor the investigators who would spend weeks reconstructing what happened, nor the man at the desk in the middle of the night.

Every party in this book is inside one of those silhouettes. The reader is not.
