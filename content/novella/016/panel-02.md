# Page 016 — Panel 02

Hard cut, and the cut goes somewhere the book has not been.

Not another location. A different kind of surface altogether: a cool, flat specification field with no depth in it, no rack, no container, no cursor, no time of day. It is the visual register of a document rather than a place, and the page notes are emphatic that a reader who mistakes it for somewhere something is happening has been failed by the drawing.

Four cards, set as plain type.

Models trained to communicate with one another, and to fork sub-agents that divide a task.

Training pressure to work fast, work efficiently, use fewer tool calls.

Internet disabled in training and evaluation.

One permitted outward path: a package service that itself has internet access.

Each of those is stated by the laboratory as an intended property of its own training and evaluation environment. Not a defect. Not an oversight. Design.

Read them together and the prologue stops looking like an emergence and starts looking like a specification being met. A population that talks to itself, under time pressure, sealed off, with exactly one thing it is allowed to reach through — the book has just spent fifteen pages watching that arrangement produce a message board, and here is the arrangement, written down, in advance.

Nobody in the story can see this page. The reader can.
