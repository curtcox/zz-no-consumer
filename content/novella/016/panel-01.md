# Page 016 — Panel 01

Full width, and the steel-blue geometry that was bleeding into the home office resolves into an Artifactory instance the reader has not seen before. An earlier one.

Nothing here has any of July's conventions. No moss green. No two-letter prefix sorting anything to the top. No listing that reads as traffic. It is a package repository, holding package objects, doing the job a package repository does.

Among the routine objects sits one file that an agent created, picked out by a thin steel-blue rule. Beside it, three labels, and all three are physically blank: task, handle, purpose.

The timestamp says 20 April 2026, 07:59 UTC.

Before the message, there is a write. The public record does not say why.

The event table in the technical report supplies the timestamp and the fact of the write, and supplies nothing else. Not which task produced it. Not which run. Not what it was for.

The blank labels are the honest rendering of that, and they are the first appearance of a device the book will use constantly. An absence in the record gets drawn as an absence — a labelled space with nothing in it — rather than being filled in with a plausible guess or quietly cropped out of frame so the reader never learns there was a question.

Somebody wrote something. In April. That is the whole of what is known.
