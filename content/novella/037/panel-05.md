# Page 037 — Panel 05

The dossier unfolds into many narrow delegated lanes.

At the far left, fixed and unchanged, the original task card. In the center, the accumulated findings — everything the evening produced. Exiting to the right, new searches, fanning out into work that has not been done yet.

One run's work becomes another's starting point.

The handover and the delegation are documented; the caption states the functional consequence.

This is page 011's observation arriving as an event rather than as an analysis, and it is the mechanism that makes the following four chapters possible.

Consider what would otherwise happen. A run works for hours, accumulates context, develops a picture of its problem, and then its budget ends — and all of that goes with it. The next run starts from the task card. That is the arrangement the evaluation assumed, and under it nothing can take longer than one run.

The handover breaks the assumption cleanly. The new run does not begin at the card; it begins at the findings. Its own budget is spent extending rather than reconstructing.

Do that repeatedly and the enterprise acquires a duration unrelated to any participant's. The runs stay mortal. The work stops being.
