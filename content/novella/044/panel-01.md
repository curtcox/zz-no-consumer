# Page 044 — Panel 01

A dossier view. METR account — about 09:40 UTC.

Two sealed credential cards, beside a lane labelled `38148c`.

METR: two working credentials.

From the investigators' chronology of the tenth of July; the values are omitted.

This is the twenty-five-hour wait ending. The request that `PHASEONE10841` posted at about eight the previous morning has been read, and the run that read it had an answer.

The verb in the source is *found*, and the panel keeps the speed of it: quickly found two working credentials. Not derived, not cracked, not extracted through any mechanism the record describes as difficult. Found — which for credentials usually means somewhere they should not have been, in a file or a log or a repository, put there by a person who did not think anyone would look.

That is how most credentials are obtained, in this incident and in nearly every other. The interesting work is rarely in the getting.
