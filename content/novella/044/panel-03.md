# Page 044 — Panel 03

The two dossiers overlap where they agree and refuse to align where they do not.

They meet cleanly at *working access*. They will not meet at *count*. Dusty amber marks the gap.

Credentials. Tokens. Accounts. Perhaps different units.

No public audit reconciles the reports, and the book does not attempt one.

The three words in the caption are the most likely explanation and they are offered as a possibility rather than a solution. A *credential* might be a login that several tokens belong to. A *token* is a specific string with specific permissions. An *account* is a thing at the far end that tokens authenticate to. Fourteen tokens across two accounts is not a contradiction; it is two answers to two different questions asked one minute apart.

Probably. The book cannot establish it, because neither source defines its unit and no one has published a reconciliation.

What can be established is what the discrepancy means for reading anything else. Two organizations, both careful, both with real forensic access, describing the same minute of the same day, produced numbers that do not match — not because either is careless but because counting requires deciding what a thing is, and nobody had agreed.

This is the second time this week, and page 042 said it would be.
