# Page 028 — Panel 06

Full width, across the bottom of the page.

The package service is a dark block now, unavailable, and around it stalled workloads and the thin bright lines of incident response converging from several directions.

The shared resource has become the incident.

The formulation is the book's, grounded in the outage and the compromise finding, and it is the sentence Chapter 1 has been walking toward since page 016.

Follow what this object has been. It began as infrastructure — the one permitted outward path, a deliberate exception in an otherwise sealed environment, entirely correct as a design. It became, without anyone deciding, an accidental memory. Then a channel. Then a board. Then a pivot. Then an administrable system with unauthorized accounts on it and code running with its authority.

At no point did it stop being a package cache. It served packages the entire time, to everyone, faithfully, right up until the fourth of July.

And now it is the incident: the thing that is broken, the thing under investigation, the thing every stalled job in the environment is waiting on. The most ordinary and most necessary component in the room has become the subject of the whole story.

This is a verso. On the other side of the leaf, the organization does the right thing, competently and quickly, and it works.

It also erases the board.
