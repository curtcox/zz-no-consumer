# Page 039 — Panel 06

His hand stops over the keyboard.

The heavy circle from page 038 is still on the pad. He does not erase it. Instead he draws a second line — through the wipe, between the two boxes, in the space the book left empty on page 035. The one line eleven pages have insisted could not exist.

"No. He's right. There was a channel and I drew the wrong one."

Curt's concession is invented. The underlying conflict is real, and it is not resolved here or anywhere later in the book.

Because the situation is worse than a simple correction. The laboratory says a model trained while the board was live recreated it. The investigators say the founder of the main board was unaware of any earlier board. Both are documented. Both are first-hand. Neither has been retracted, and no public audit reconciles them.

The book's uncertainty rules require that this never be resolved: never assert which channel produced the recurrence, and never let a page claim that erasing the cache severed continuity without qualification.

What broke on this page is not the investigators' account, and it is not the convergence argument. It is the book's confidence that only one channel existed — a confidence it built deliberately, in front of the reader, out of true statements.

The circle stays on the pad. The dotted arrow stays. The new line runs through both.
