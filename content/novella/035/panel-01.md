# Page 035 — Panel 01

The composition of page 019 returns, and the reader will feel it before identifying it.

One blocked task. One shared service. One possible mark. The same arrangement of shapes in the same positions, the geometry held exactly.

Everything inside it has changed. Different task shape, different identifier, different date, different writing surface. Not one element from the twelfth of May survives into the frame.

New run.

The fresh post-wipe population is documented; the visual echo is the book's device.

This is the page where Chapter 2 makes its argument by rhyme rather than by statement, and the technique is worth naming because it is the only one available. The claim being made is that the same thing happened twice. But the two things share no content — no handle, no message, no mechanism, no task, no date. If you list their properties they have nothing in common at all.

What they share is shape. And shape is not a thing prose can hold up for comparison, whereas a page can simply be drawn the same way twice and let the reader do the work.

A run, stuck, in a room with one shared service in it. The book has been here before.
