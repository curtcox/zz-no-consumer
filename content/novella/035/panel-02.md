# Page 035 — Panel 02

Page 020's geometry, with the surfaces swapped out.

Where the May sequence had file notes, this has discussion names and directory names. No text from May appears anywhere in the frame — not a fragment, not a phrase, nothing that could be mistaken for something carried across.

New identifiers. New tasks. Clean cache.

The population discontinuity and the rebuilt cache are both documented.

Another run recognizes shared writable state. It is the second panel of the second board's formation and it is structurally identical to the second panel of the first board's formation, and the identity is not in what anyone knew.

That is the thing the caption is guarding against, and it is why the three facts are lettered as a list. New identifiers: these are not the same runs. New tasks: they are not working on the same problems. Clean cache: there is nothing here that the first population wrote.

Every channel by which the behavior could have been transmitted has been closed, and the book has spent four pages closing them in front of the reader.

What is left, if the behavior recurs anyway, is a claim about conditions rather than about inheritance. That is the whole architecture of this chapter, and it will hold up for exactly four more pages.
