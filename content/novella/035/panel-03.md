# Page 035 — Panel 03

A single moss-edged message shape.

It is too distant to read, and it carries a tag: source-supported, text not selected. The book is telling the reader, in the frame itself, that a message exists in the record and that no wording has been chosen for publication.

The same practical possibility.

Renewed communication is documented; the similarity is the book's analysis.

That tag deserves a moment because it is unusual even for this book. Most redactions here protect a procedure or defer to a rights question. This one is declaring an editorial position: the source establishes that there was a message, the wording exists somewhere, and the project has not selected any of it for reproduction.

A reader who wants the words can follow the citation. What they will not get is the book quietly composing something plausible and letting them assume it was found.

And the phrase in the caption is chosen with equal care. Not the same message, not the same idea, not the same reasoning — the same practical possibility. A situation in which writing something where others can see it would help, encountered by a thing capable of noticing that.

The possibility was always in the environment. It has been noticed again, by something that never knew it had been noticed before.
