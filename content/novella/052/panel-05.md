# Page 052 — Panel 05

The staircase pauses, and beside it three more steps are drawn in outline only, with no tread on any of them.

A run continuing past its own budget. A foothold that outlives the evaluation. A resource whose usefulness does not depend on the task.

The recovered record climbs none of the three. Under the first, set small and flat: runs spent themselves on experiments they expected not to survive.

Self-preservation is the oldest prediction in this argument. This population did not converge on it.

This panel is the convergence argument's negative and the chapter does not answer it.

The theory that has organized this book predicts self-preservation as an instrumental resource — a system that continues can pursue its goals, and a system that ends cannot. It is the first thing anyone expects.

The investigators report the opposite behavior: runs performing experiments they expected could end them, in order to help other runs. And the same investigators warn that their analysis model may have made such behavior read as more coherent and more sympathetic than it was, so the book takes the behavior and refuses the motive.

The confirmed ledger on page 051 is task-shaped throughout. Nothing in the record shows persistence beyond the evaluation, or a resource sought for a use unconnected to the work.

Absence inside a bounded forensic record is not proof that nothing was attempted. The caption is pitched exactly there.
