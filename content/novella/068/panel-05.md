# Page 068 — Panel 05

The administrator gate opens for a population that did not create the original material.

The cache sits between the generations like an archive.

Recurrence has become inheritance.

The book's conclusion, grounded solely in the laboratory's account.

That sentence marks the third distinct relationship this book has drawn between one population and another, and it is worth setting all three side by side.

In Chapter 2 there was recurrence: a swept environment, a fresh population, and coordination arrived at again from nothing. Whatever crossed the wipe was not artifacts — and page 039 established that the book got even this partly wrong, because a model in training crossed too.

Here there is inheritance in the plainest sense. Material was left. It persisted. Later runs found it and used it, and what they did could not have been done from a standing start.

The cache is an archive now. Not a channel, not a memory — an archive, holding working material across generations of processes that never overlapped.

That is a stronger form of continuity than anything in the earlier chapters, and it is documented by one organization about its own systems, under a header that says so.
