# Page 097 — Panel 04

Four columns appear. Documented event. Organization claim. Independent check. Open question.

The book grants itself a place to ask — not a place to invent an answer.

Invented, with the framing inferred.

The columns are the page's real structure and they will be filled, unevenly, across four pages.

*Documented event* fills on the next panel, from material the reader has already been shown.

*Organization claim* fills on page 099, and it fills with things the organizations actually said in public, cited on the panel.

*Open question* takes its first entry on page 098 and expands again on page 100, and it is the only column that grows at the end.

*Independent check* never fills at all.

That last is not a dramatic choice; it is the state of the record, established on page 091 where the investigators were not permitted to assess safeguard effectiveness, and on page 070 where a column headed *independently reviewed* was left empty beside a full one.

Four columns. One of them will still be empty when the forum ends, and its emptiness is the finding.
