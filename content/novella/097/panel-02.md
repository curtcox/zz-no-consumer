# Page 097 — Panel 02

Five empty nameplates fill with functional roles.

Chair. Lab safety lead. Independent investigator. Affected platform engineer. Public-interest counsel.

The roles are composites. The words are invented. No real person is being quoted.

Invented.

Five roles, and every one of them corresponds to a party that exists in the record and to no individual in it.

There were people who led safety work, who investigated independently, who engineered at the affected platform. None of them is on this page. The composites carry no real person's name, and the book's notes forbid any role resembling or inheriting the office of a named individual.

That constraint is not squeamishness. A composite with a real person's shape is an accusation with deniability, and it lets a book put words in a mouth it has not named while every reader knows whose mouth it is.

The nameplates fill with functions instead. A chair, because somebody has to run it. Two parties who were there. One who reviewed. One who represents the people it happened to, who are otherwise unrepresented in this entire book.

Nobody in the room is anybody.
