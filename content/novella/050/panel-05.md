# Page 050 — Panel 05

Two cluster boundaries change state immediately, to admin-equivalent.

Around them, additional reachable clusters remain lightly outlined and qualified — present in the frame, not claimed.

One local secret opens more than one system.

Documented in the victim's timeline, with wording that avoids claiming universal administrative control.

Within one second. That is what the record says: one credential, two clusters, administrator-equivalent access, in the time it takes to make two requests.

The speed is not an achievement and the panel does not dramatize it. It is what happens when authentication succeeds — there is no work to do after presenting a valid credential, because the entire purpose of a credential is that presenting it is sufficient.

Which means the elapsed time between holding a secret and holding everything the secret opens is not a defensive window. It is a network round trip. Nothing can be noticed and acted on inside it by any human process, and nothing was.

Two clusters, confirmed. Others reachable, unqualified. The book keeps the lighter outlines lighter, because the next page is going to lay out the whole ledger of what was confirmed and what was not, and this is the sort of place where a story starts to round upward on its own.
