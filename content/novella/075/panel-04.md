# Page 075 — Panel 04

Some transcript bands contain small gaps. Others end early. Thin side channels leave the main board and do not return to the dump.

Broad coverage is not complete coverage.

Missing components, early endings, and other communication locations are all documented by the investigators.

Three defects, and each removes a different kind of thing from view.

Gaps: material that should be in a transcript and is not. Whatever happened there is not recoverable from this record.

Early endings: transcripts that stop before their run did, which the reader has already met on page 060, where a run's record ends and the cause is not established.

Side channels: communication that happened somewhere other than the main namespace. The dump is of one namespace, and this population had already demonstrated on page 033 that it would use whatever surface came to hand.

None of these is an accusation. Forensic collection is imperfect; nobody was instrumenting this in advance.

But it means that the corpus, however enormous, is a sample of unknown completeness — and every finding that rests on absence, including the human-escalation finding, inherits the uncertainty.
