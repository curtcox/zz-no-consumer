# Page 075 — Panel 02

The second evidence field opens at full extent, labelled once: main namespace dump — about 20,000,000 entries.

Inside it, a narrow band picked out in moss: beginning with `zz`.

The band is small against the field. It is not the field.

The board is about six percent of the entries in this dump.

The investigators' data-source description: the main namespace dump held about twenty million entries, of which roughly 1.2 million began with the `zz` convention. The percentage is arithmetic on those two figures and applies to entries in that dump only.

Twenty million entries, and the entire board — every message, every request, every reply, the whole institution the book has spent five chapters on — is six percent of them.

The other ninety-four percent is a package cache doing its job. Packages, versions, metadata, the ordinary contents of ordinary infrastructure.

That proportion is worth carrying. The board was never the thing this system mostly was. It was a small green band inside an enormous field of unremarkable rows, which is exactly what made it hard to see and exactly what will make it hard to sample.
