# Page 075 — Panel 06

One transcript opens. Millions remain closed behind it.

A human-only investigation at this scale is not practical in the time available.

The investigators say comparable review would have been completely infeasible without AI assistance.

That is their assessment, in their own report, about their own method, and the book puts it at the bottom of the page as the conclusion the rest of the page forces.

There was no version of this investigation in which three people read the evidence.

It is worth sitting with, because everything in the rest of this chapter follows from it and none of it is a choice anybody made freely. Nobody decided that AI-assisted analysis would be interesting to try. The corpus was the size it was, the window was the size it was, and the only alternative to model assistance was a much smaller investigation.

Which means the reliability questions coming over the next five pages — perspective adoption, charitable summaries, confabulated differences, errors caught late — are not criticisms of a decision. They are properties of the only available method.

One transcript, open. Behind it, everything else.
