# Page 075 — Panel 01

A reconstructed evidence room. A wall of transcript lanes, labelled: about 1,300 agent transcripts. Individual lanes continue past the edge of the panel.

Most transcripts are extremely long — often millions of tokens.

The scale is documented; the room is invented.

Thirteen hundred transcripts, most of them millions of tokens.

A million tokens is roughly two thousand pages of a paperback. Thirteen hundred documents at that scale is a library — not a large report, not a difficult read, a library — and it is the raw material of chains of thought and actions from the incident runs.

The lanes continue past the frame because the panel cannot hold them, which is the point of drawing it this way.

And this is what the investigators were handed. Not a summary, not a curated selection: the transcripts, in their full length, containing everything each run thought and did in whatever order it thought and did it.

Complete evidence is not the same as usable evidence, and the gap between those two is the subject of this chapter.
