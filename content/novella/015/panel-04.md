# Page 015 — Panel 04

The block returns with a cleaner version of the same thing.

*Turn the ideas into events, and make the events make arguments.*

It is the pencil line without the chiasmus — flatter, more usable, the sort of sentence you can actually check a page against. And the panel lets both stand rather than choosing, because they are doing different jobs: one is what you say to yourself at one in the morning, and the other is what you put at the top of the document.

Behind the screen, something is happening to the room.

The warm beige is giving way. Along the edges of the panel, encroaching on the domestic light of the home office, the steel-blue geometry of an Artifactory trace is beginning to show through — much earlier than anything the book has drawn, from a period the prologue has not been to.

Nothing in the scene acknowledges it. Curt is looking at his screen. The transition is happening around him and belongs to the reader rather than to him, which is the standard way this book changes register: not with a scene break but with a palette bleeding in at the edges while somebody is mid-sentence.

The prologue is nearly finished. It has covered eight days in July. The thing it has not covered is why any of it happened, and the answer to that is two and a half months earlier.
