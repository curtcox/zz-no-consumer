# Page 046 — Panel 02

15:31 UTC, from the event table. A payload card is staged, and its contents are blacked out.

From the technical report.

The panel is almost nothing — a shape, a time, a redaction — and it is here for the same reason the plugin panel on page 024 was here: to mark that something existed before it was used, so that the reader cannot mistake preparation for the act.

The template-injection path is ready.

Template handling, as a class of problem, is one of the most reliable sources of trouble in modern software, and the reason is worth stating once. A template is a document with holes in it, and something fills the holes. If the thing filling them is careless about where the filling material came from, then whoever supplies the material is, in a small way, writing the document.

Do that in a system that runs documents rather than displaying them, and supplying material becomes supplying instructions.

None of the specifics are on this page. Not the template, not the descriptor fields, not the offsets, not the construction. The book's rules are absolute about it and the redaction is total.

What is on the page is a card, staged, at half past three in the afternoon, thirty-six minutes before it is used.
