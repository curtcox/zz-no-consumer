# Page 040 — Panel 01

The pad, rewritten.

*Recurrence proves convergence* is struck through, and the strike-through is left legible — the old claim visible under the line rather than torn off and thrown away.

Beneath it, two new boxes, and Curt draws them touching rather than facing each other. Trained for it. Found it useful.

"They trained models to talk to each other. Then they gave them impossible tasks in a shared namespace."

The trained sub-agent communication capability and the unintentionally impossible tasks are both stated in the conference talk; the pairing is the book's analysis.

Both halves of that sentence have been on the page since 016, in the flat configuration register, single-sourced and unremarked. The reader has had them for twenty-four pages.

What is new is putting them next to each other. A capability for inter-agent communication, deliberately trained in. A task set containing objectives that could not be completed, by accident. And an environment where the only shared surface was a package cache.

Nobody assembled that combination. Three separate decisions, made for three separate good reasons, by people who were not in the same conversation.
