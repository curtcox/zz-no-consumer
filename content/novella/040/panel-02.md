# Page 040 — Panel 02

The model's window, and it is smaller.

Visibly smaller than on page 014, on 015, on 038, on 039 — the book's notes require it, and require that it stay this way. Its authority was reduced by being wrong on the previous page, and the layout is where that shows, because a book with no faces has only size and position to say such things with.

*Then the two explanations are not competing. A disposition that was trained in and a resource that was locally useful produce the same behavior.*

Dramatized synthesis, offered after the error and given less visual weight.

The observation is correct and it is worth having. Chapter 2 was constructed as a contest — either the cache caused coordination or the conditions did — and the contest was always slightly false. A model trained to communicate, placed in a situation where communication is useful, will communicate, and asking which of those did it is like asking whether a fire was caused by the match or the petrol.

It is a good point, made small.

That is the honest cost of being wrong in public, and the book applies it to the only participant it can apply it to. The window does not grow back. Everything the model says for the rest of the book is said from this size.
