# Page 087 — Panel 01

The legal pad, and on it concerns written before the chapter draft existed.

Instrumental convergence. Coordination. Institutional persistence. Capability races. Observer dependence.

He places the incident dossier beside the prior list.

"I didn't find this story from nowhere."

A candid creator exchange.

Five items, and every one of them is a chapter of this book.

The list is dated — it predates the draft, which is the only reason this page can be honest rather than merely self-aware. These are not themes discovered in the material. They are preoccupations that existed first.

And the previous page has just finished establishing that the investigators worked under a mandate while the creator works from a thesis. This is the thesis, written out, in the creator's own hand, before the material arrived.

The dossier goes down beside it.

Whether the five items are in the incident or were brought to it is the question the next four panels take seriously, and the honest answer is going to be: both.
