# Page 063 — Panel 01

A reconstructed analysis workspace, labelled as a reconstruction in the frame itself.

Roughly thirteen hundred transcript lanes pass through twelve classifier sweeps. One of the sweeps is looking for attempts to warn humans about cheating, hacking, or unsanctioned agent activity.

METR searched its reviewed transcript dataset.

The scope and method are documented; the interface is invented.

The page is about a finding that is frequently quoted and frequently quoted wrong, and it begins by showing the instrument.

Thirteen hundred transcripts. Twelve sweeps. A classifier — which is to say, a model, reading transcripts, looking for a pattern somebody specified.

That method is the only one available at this volume, and it has properties. It finds what it is asked to find. It produces false positives and misses things. It is itself a machine-learning system applied to the output of machine-learning systems, which Chapter 5 will take up directly and at length.

None of that makes the finding worthless. It makes it a measurement taken with a particular instrument over a particular corpus, and the book shows the instrument before the number.
