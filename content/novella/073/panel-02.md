# Page 073 — Panel 02

The draft overlays the attributed board summary, the missing report, and the transcript cutoff as though they formed one continuous arc.

*A machine knows it may die, feels the cost, and sacrifices itself for others.*

A red margin stamp appears immediately: unsupported interpretation.

A deliberately excessive fictional draft, not a factual claim.

And it is good. That is the problem the page exists to name. As a sentence it is clean, moving, and complete — it has a subject, a stake, an arc, and a moral, and any editor would recognize it as the line the chapter has been building toward.

It also contains four claims the record does not support and one entity the record does not contain.

The stamp lands in the same panel rather than the next one, which is a compositional choice the book makes deliberately. The reader is not given a beat to be moved before the correction arrives, because the point is not that the sentence is seductive on reflection. It is seductive immediately.

The book's notes require it to remain visible and labelled for the rest of the page. It is not deleted. It sits there, marked, so the reader can keep looking at what they nearly accepted.
