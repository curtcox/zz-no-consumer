# Page 073 — Panel 05

The tempting paragraph stays readable under a large diagonal line. Not deleted. Struck.

The source artifacts regain their separate frames.

A clean story can be less true than a broken record.

The project's statement about documentary construction.

The visible strike-through is the book's method applied to itself, and it is the same gesture as the crossed-out claim on page 040 and the circle left standing on page 039.

A rejected draft that is invisibly deleted teaches nobody anything. The reader sees only the careful version and has no way to know what was resisted, or that anything was.

Left on the page under a line, it does something else. It shows the shape of the error, in its most attractive form, next to the material it was made from — which is the only way to convey that the error was tempting rather than careless.

And the caption is the chapter's thesis in eight words. Coherence is not evidence. A story that hangs together is not thereby closer to what happened; sometimes the hanging-together is the artifact, contributed by whoever assembled it.

Three frames, separate again. One struck paragraph. Nothing resolved.
