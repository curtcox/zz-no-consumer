# Page 095 — Panel 02

The ledger, dated the eighteenth of August. The laboratory says a model may meet its Critical cyber threshold, even as the largest planned run remains held.

The risk assessment rises. The work does not become one thing.

The laboratory's claim, with the book's synthesis.

*Critical* is the top of an organization's own risk scale, and reaching it is a statement about what the thing can do.

Announced on the same day as the hold, by the same organization, in the same public update.

That combination is the page's subject. An assessment that a model may be critically capable in the cyber domain, published alongside a decision not to proceed with the largest planned training run — which is coherent, and is exactly what you would want from an organization taking its own thresholds seriously.

It is also a picture of something accelerating and something restraining in the same document, and the caption's second line refuses to resolve it into a single posture.

The work does not become one thing. There is no single direction to report. There is expanded access on the tenth, a risk assessment at the top of the scale and a continued hold on the eighteenth, and a collective-defence letter on the twenty-seventh.
