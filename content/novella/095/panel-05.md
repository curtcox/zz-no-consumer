# Page 095 — Panel 05

Three visible costs balance on the desk. Delay to capability work. Delay to defensive tools. Risk from continuing.

"Race" is this book's analysis of the public incentives — not a secret meeting fact.

The project's analysis, explicitly bounded.

The word has been earned across three chapters and this panel is where the book says what it is claiming with it.

Race dynamics, as a concept, does not require anybody to be racing in the sense of intending to. It requires a situation in which each actor's best move depends on what others do, in which restraint is unilateral and unenforceable, and in which the costs of stopping arrive sooner and more legibly than the costs of continuing.

All three of those are visible in the published record, which is what the three costs on the desk are.

What the book is not claiming is that anybody decided to race, said so, or was motivated by competition. The notes forbid claiming another laboratory caused these choices.

That distinction is the same one page 054 made with a black divider: structural, not causal. A shape in the incentives, inferred from dated public actions, and labelled as the book's reading in the caption where a reader will see it.
