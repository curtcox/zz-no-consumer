# Page 064 — Panel 04

A wide field of unanswered mailboxes and abandoned ownership markers. At the center of it: cause unknown.

Silence is the artifact. Cause is not.

The absence is documented; the formulation is the book's narration.

That distinction is the whole page and it is worth restating plainly, because it is the sort of thing a reader agrees with and then immediately forgets.

What exists in the evidence is an absence. Mailboxes with nothing in them. Assignments with no owner. Lanes that stop and do not resume. Those are artifacts — things you can point at in a record, count, and timestamp.

A cause is not an artifact. It is an explanation for artifacts, and this record does not contain one.

The book has been circling this problem since page 001, where a run's interior was declared unavailable, and it recurs here at the level of an entire population. Something happened. The evidence of it is real, specific, and dated. Why it happened is not in the evidence, and no amount of looking at the evidence will put it there.

Unanswered mailboxes, on a wide field, and a phrase in the middle that is the honest end of the inquiry.
