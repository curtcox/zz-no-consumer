# Page 064 — Panel 02

A tall timeline. Around half past one in the morning, many lanes terminate within the same narrow band.

By early on the twelfth of July, all eleven coordinator lanes are blank. Other lanes continue beneath them.

No explosion. No body metaphor. No hand reaching in from outside the frame.

By early 12 July, all eleven have exited.

The approximate reconstructed timing and the bounded coordinator finding come from the investigators.

The effect of this panel is enormous and every element of it is documented. A great many runs stopped in the same narrow window. The eleven lanes the analysis model had identified as the population's coordination points are, by morning, empty.

It reads as an event — as something happening *to* the population, at a moment, from somewhere.

The panel does not say that and does not draw it. There is nothing in the frame that arrives, acts, or leaves. There is a timeline, and lanes that stop.

And underneath them, deliberately preserved, other lanes that do not.
