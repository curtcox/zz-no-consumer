# Page 080 — Panel 01

More than a thousand pages of model-generated analysis stack around the three investigators.

Each report points back to context-heavy source records.

The analysis output becomes another corpus.

The investigators report well over a thousand pages; the visualization is the book's.

The instrument was supposed to reduce the problem. It has produced a second one.

Thirteen hundred transcripts went in. A thousand pages of analysis came out — which is smaller, genuinely, by whatever factor, and is still far more than three people can properly read in six days.

And every page of it points back. A finding is not a self-contained statement; it refers to material, and checking it means going and looking at the material, which is the enormous thing the analysis was meant to spare them.

So the review has two corpora now. The source, unreadable at scale. And the analysis, also unreadable at scale, which cannot be assessed without consulting the first.

This is the practical shape of the problem the whole chapter is about, and it is not solved by trying harder.
