# Page 080 — Panel 03

Two error cards surface late.

Large amount of data missed in an early figure. Confabulated differences between datasets.

Examples from the investigators' footnote.

Two failures, and they are different species.

The first is omission at scale — a figure produced from substantially less data than it should have covered. That is a quantitative error, the kind that makes a number wrong, and it is catchable if somebody happens to recompute.

The second is worse. Confabulated differences between datasets: distinctions that were reported and were not there. Not a miscount. Content that does not correspond to anything, presented in the same register as content that does.

And the word in the panel is *late*. These were found after they had been in the working picture for some time, which means the investigation proceeded for a while on material that included them.

Both were caught. That is the good news and it is real — human review, spot-checking, and recomputation found these.

The next panel is about what that does and does not license anybody to conclude.
