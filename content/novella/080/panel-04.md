# Page 080 — Panel 04

Behind the corrected cards, a field of unchecked analysis pages.

Caught errors prove correction is possible. They do not count the errors still unseen.

The investigators expect uncaught errors.

That is the cleanest statement of a problem that appears everywhere and is almost always mishandled.

Finding errors is usually presented as reassurance. *We caught these* implies a process that works. And it does prove something real: the review is capable of catching things, so it is not merely decorative.

What it cannot do is estimate what remains. The two caught errors were found by spot-checks on a sample, and the sample was small because the corpus is large. Nothing about finding two tells you whether there are three more or three hundred, because the search was never exhaustive and its coverage is unknown.

And the direction of the reasoning that most people apply is backwards. Errors found in a small sample of a large corpus are evidence that the corpus contains errors at some rate, not evidence that the corpus has been cleaned.

The investigators say they expect uncaught errors. The field behind the cards is the honest picture.
