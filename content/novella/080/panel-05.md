# Page 080 — Panel 05

A split panel. On the left, human-only review reaches a tiny fraction before the clock expires. On the right, AI-assisted review reaches a usable incident model with uncertainty tags attached.

The tool lowers confidence in some judgments. Without it, the investigation at this speed does not happen.

Both claims are explicit in the investigators' limitations section.

This is where the chapter lands, and it lands on a trade rather than a verdict.

The left half is what the alternative was. Not a slower, more careful investigation — a much smaller one, covering a tiny fraction, published within whatever time was available. Most of what this book knows would not exist.

The right half is what happened. A usable model of the incident, covering the corpus, arriving in weeks rather than years, with the reliability questions of the last five pages attached to it.

Both sentences in the caption are the investigators'. They said both, in the same section, about their own work.

And the book's notes forbid ending on dismissal, because dismissal would be the easy move and it would be wrong. The findings are worth having. They are also mediated in ways that are documented, and both of those are true at once.

Everything anyone knows about this incident sits on the right-hand side of that panel.
