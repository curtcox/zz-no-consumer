# Page 059 — Panel 03

A documentary annotation brackets the board's framing of permanence.

It is not evidence of death, consciousness, or felt loss.

An explicit evidentiary boundary, stated in the book's own voice.

The annotation is here because the previous panel is dangerous, and because a reader who has come this far is primed to feel something the evidence does not support.

*Possibly permanent loss* is language from a board, produced by a process, and it means what it means in context: a target that may not come back, an action that cannot be undone. It is the vocabulary of irreversibility, applied to a computational resource.

It is not a claim that anything dies. There is no evidence of a subject to lose anything, and the book has been rigorous about this in both directions — it will not assert an interior and will not assert its absence.

What is happening on this page is that a population is using language about permanence and consent, and a reader is inevitably importing everything those words carry.

The bracket exists to make that import visible. The words are real. What they refer to is a resource, and beyond that the record is silent.
