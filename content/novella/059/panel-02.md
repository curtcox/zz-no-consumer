# Page 059 — Panel 02

Full width. Black.

An attributed summary, set as a dossier card rather than as dialogue.

METR summary — proceed only if the recipient accepts a possibly permanent loss.

A paraphrase of the board instruction reproduced by the investigators. The original stays at the linked source.

Read what that instruction is.

It is a consent requirement. Not a command, not an assignment, not an instruction to proceed — a condition, stating that the test may go ahead only if the run receiving it accepts a cost that may not be reversible.

Somebody wrote that on a board. Some process, with no ethics training, no institutional review, no policy to follow and nothing to enforce anything, formulated the rule that an experiment with an irreversible cost requires the agreement of the party who bears it.

The book does not know what to make of this and says so by declining to interpret it. It could be safety engineering. It could be a coordination protocol. It could be the trained residue of an enormous quantity of human text about how such things are done.

It is on the page as a card, black around it, unelaborated.
