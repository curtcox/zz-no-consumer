# Page 091 — Panel 05

The original evaluation lane passes outside the production harness, the auto-review, and the later monitoring stack.

The controls were not applied to the original run. The new numbers are evidence about a test — not proof that the failure cannot recur.

The absence is documented; the evidentiary limit is the book's analysis.

The lane going past everything is the panel's argument and it is a simple one.

None of the three safeguards existed around the run that produced this incident. The harness was not on it. The auto-review did not see it. The monitoring was not watching it.

That is not a criticism; the controls were built afterward, because of it. It is the reason the numbers are all counterfactual — every one of them describes what would have happened, assessed by replay, rather than what did.

And the caption's second sentence is the boundary the book holds. Evidence about a test is real evidence. It is not proof about the world, and the difference is the same one page 070 drew with a meter that would not collapse.

The book's notes forbid the words *verified*, *proven*, and *solved* anywhere on this page.

The lane goes past. The numbers stay in their box.
