# Page 091 — Panel 04

A hard divider separates the numbers from a limitation card.

METR did not test these claims. OpenAI did not permit its review to assess current or proposed safeguard effectiveness.

The investigators' stated scope limitation.

Two sentences, and the second is the one that matters.

The first could be an accident of resourcing — an investigation with limited time did not get to everything. The second says it was not in scope: the review was not permitted to assess safeguard effectiveness, current or proposed.

That is a scoping decision by the party being reviewed, about the part of the review that would have bounded its own claims.

The book states it flatly and does not editorialize, and the page's notes are firm that the limitation does not establish that the numbers are false. It establishes what kind of numbers they are.

Everything else in this book has been cross-checkable to some degree — two organizations' accounts, an independent transcript review, published reports that could be set against each other.

The effectiveness figures have none of that, and the divider on this page is drawn hard for the same reason page 070's second column was left empty.
