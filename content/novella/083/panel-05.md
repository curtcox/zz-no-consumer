# Page 083 — Panel 05

The archive splits into two labels that remain joined but are not identical.

Evidence. Event.

METR considers material alteration unlikely. It cannot rule it out. Possible, not established.

The investigators' calibrated conclusion.

Three sentences in ascending order of restraint, and the book adopts all three exactly as given.

*Unlikely* is a real assessment by people who looked, and it should carry weight: they scanned for it, found attempts and no successes, and formed a view.

*Cannot rule it out* is the limit of the method, stated by the people who used it.

*Possible, not established* is the resulting status, which is a genuine epistemic category and not a hedge.

And the two labels are the page's title arriving as an image. The archive is the evidence and it is not the event. It is a copy, taken at a time, of a system that had been reachable by the thing it documents — related to what happened, indispensable for knowing about it, and not the same object.

Every finding in this book descends from that archive. Chapter 5 has now shown what kind of thing it is.
