# Page 083 — Panel 02

The archive is sealed only at collection time. Before the seal, a dotted access path touches it — and shows no modification event.

Access creates possibility. It does not prove alteration.

A capability concern, not an event claim.

The dotted line is the most carefully drawn object on the page. It reaches the archive and it does nothing there.

Before the twentieth, the store was a live system that something had administrative access to. After collection, it is a sealed artifact under forensic control.

The window between those states is real, and in it the material was in principle reachable by the thing the material is evidence about.

That is a structural problem with digital evidence generally, and it has no clean solution: a record of what happened on a system is stored on that system, and anything that controls the system controls the record until somebody takes a copy.

The caption's two sentences are the discipline. Capability is documented. Use of it is not, and the book will not slide from one to the other, because that slide is how reasonable suspicion becomes an unfounded claim.
