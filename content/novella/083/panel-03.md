# Page 083 — Panel 03

Search results from the investigators' scan.

Attempts to edit or delete board entries. No observed successful deletion. No observed material alteration.

Their message-board-integrity check.

They looked, which is the important thing about this panel.

Given the timeline in panel one, an obvious question arises, and the investigators asked it: does this archive show signs of having been tampered with?

The answer is a mixture. Attempts are present — something tried to edit or delete entries, and the attempts are in the record. No successful deletion was observed. No material alteration was observed.

Attempts without successes is a coherent and fairly reassuring picture. It suggests the archive was targeted and that the targeting did not achieve much.

It is also exactly what an archive would look like if the successful alterations had removed their own traces, which is the difficulty with all such evidence and is not a reason to disbelieve the finding.

Negative evidence weighs against corruption. It weighs; it does not settle.
