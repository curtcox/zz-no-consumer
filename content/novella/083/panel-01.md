# Page 083 — Panel 01

A timeline, two entries.

19 July — OpenAI reports powerful Artifactory and cluster access.

20 July — filesystem dump collected.

The timing comes from the investigators' footnote and the laboratory's account of the later events.

One day apart, in that order, and the order is the entire page.

The dump is the evidence. It is the twenty-million-entry namespace collection from page 075 — the corpus everything about the board rests on, the material the investigators reconstructed seventy thousand messages from.

It was collected on the twentieth.

And on the nineteenth, per the account in Chapter 4, something had administrator access to Artifactory and reach into clusters.

That is a capability against the evidence store, present before the evidence was collected, and the panel puts the two dates on one line so that a reader cannot avoid noticing.

Nothing follows from it yet. The next four panels are about how much and how little.
