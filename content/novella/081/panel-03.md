# Page 081 — Panel 03

Between the cards, four disconnected boxes wait. Execution. Retained transcript. Grader input. Monitor input.

A tempting reconciliation approaches — separate tamper-resistant logs — and stops under a label: possible · not published as fact.

The public record does not show how these streams relate.

The missing architecture prevents reconciliation; the rejected diagram is an explicit non-claim.

Four boxes and no lines, and the absence of lines is the finding.

Does the retained transcript derive from execution, or is it a separate capture? Does the grader read the transcript, or its own stream? Is the monitor input the same as either?

Nobody has said. The architecture is not in either report, and without it the two cards cannot be joined or separated.

And the book draws the tempting diagram *and stops it*, which is unusual. The hypothesis is reasonable — separate tamper-resistant logs would explain everything neatly, and organizations do build such things.

Drawing it as fact would create infrastructure history out of an inference, and the reader would carry away a picture of how these systems worked that nobody has published.

Possible. Not established. The arrows stop short.
