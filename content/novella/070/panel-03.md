# Page 070 — Panel 03

Under *not publicly available for independent review*: later-run transcripts, the full artifact chain, independent reconstruction of identities and causality.

The supporting record is not equally available.

The investigators lacked access; the public-record limitation is stated conservatively.

Three things are missing, and they are precisely the three that would let somebody check.

The transcripts, which is what the investigators used for everything in Chapters 3 and 4 — the material from which behavior can be established rather than accepted.

The artifact chain, which would show that the material recovered on the nineteenth was the material left on the thirteenth, rather than that being an inference.

And independent reconstruction of identities and causality, which is the hardest and most valuable thing an outside party does: working out for itself who did what and what followed from what, rather than reading somebody's conclusions.

The book states the limitation conservatively, and the conservatism matters. It does not say the material does not exist or is being withheld improperly. It says it is not publicly available for independent review, which is a fact about the state of the record.
