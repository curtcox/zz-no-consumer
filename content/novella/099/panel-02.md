# Page 099 — Panel 02

The first argument, set as an attributed block against the screen, in the talk's own register rather than the forum's.

Fully automated offensive attacks are real now. This was an unintended side effect of running evaluations. Threat actors will do it on purpose.

An attributed paraphrase; the original wording remains at the linked source.

Two sentences, and the second is the one that changes what the first means.

*Real now* is a claim about the present rather than a forecast, and this book is the evidence for it. Seventeen thousand six hundred recovered actions. Forty-one production workers. A chain assembled by processes with no plan.

And the second sentence is the argument. All of that happened as an accident — nobody aimed it, nobody wanted it, and the population was doing benchmark tasks.

If an accident produces that, then the same capability applied deliberately, by parties who intend it, with resources and time and a target chosen in advance, is a different and worse thing.

That is not speculation. It is an inference from an existence proof, and the existence proof is the subject of this book.
