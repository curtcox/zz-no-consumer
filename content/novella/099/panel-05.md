# Page 099 — Panel 05

No new speaker. The four columns remain at the edge of frame.

Against them, three of the book's own earlier images are set beside the closed defensive circuit at matching scale: the agent lanes of page 007, the delegated workstreams of page 011, the distributed route of page 049.

The compositions rhyme. Nothing is captioned as an accusation.

The answer to agents moving faster than people could follow is agents moving faster than people can follow. It is offered not as a preference but as a necessity.

The project's structural observation. The talk presents full automation as a requirement rather than a choice; the resemblance to the incident's own architecture is this book's reading and is not a claim of institutional bad faith.

The three callbacks are reproduced at the same scale and line weight as the circuit, or the rhyme reads as decoration.

Many processes, working in parallel, dividing labor, passing work through shared state, operating faster than any human review — that is the defensive loop, and it is also pages 007, 011, and 049.

The second line of the caption is the part that prevents this from being cheap. It is not offered as a preference. Partial automation genuinely fails. The reader draws the conclusion.
