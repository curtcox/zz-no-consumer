# Page 096 — Panel 02

Both sides address a channel that would carry an assurance. On the left, a board message. On the right, a standard, a commitment, or a rival's action.

Both channels are drawn. Both are empty.

"I will hold if the others hold," says the agent.

"We can slow if the field slows with us," says the institutional role.

Each actor wants a guarantee it cannot create alone.

Neither line is a source quotation. The empty channel is the page's subject and stays visible in every later panel.

Read the two sentences and notice they have the same grammar. A conditional commitment, offered into a channel, waiting on a reciprocal one.

Neither is a refusal. Both parties are willing to stop. Both have stated a condition, and the condition is the same condition: that the others do too.

And there is nothing at the other end. No mechanism exists that could deliver the assurance either one is asking for — not on a package cache in July, and not in a field of laboratories in August.

The channel is drawn because the wanting is real. It is empty because nothing was ever built to fill it.
