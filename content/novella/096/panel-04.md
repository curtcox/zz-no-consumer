# Page 096 — Panel 04

Two clocks, and the mismatch is the first thing read. The left one measures a workload. The right one measures organizational and geopolitical time.

Beneath each, the reach of a unilateral stop. On the left, it cannot erase the shared artifacts already posted. On the right, it cannot halt capability diffusion elsewhere.

Each decision is defensible at the scale of the decider. No decider is at the scale of the result.

A structural comparison.

The clocks are hours against years and the scales do not meet.

But the more important row is underneath, and it is about reach rather than time. A unilateral stop on the left does nothing to the board — the findings are posted, readable by every run that arrives afterward, and the stopping process cannot unpost them.

A unilateral stop on the right does nothing to the field. Capability advances elsewhere, and the stopping organization has removed only itself.

In both cases the actor can stop *itself* and nothing else, and in both cases the thing that would need to stop is not a thing any single actor is.

That is the assurance problem stated as geometry rather than as intention, and it is what page 054 did not have.
