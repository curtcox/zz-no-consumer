# Page 096 — Panel 01

A banner runs in every gutter of this page: structural comparison — not moral or causal equivalence — both scenes compressed or invented.

The page splits. On the left, a compressed agent-board scene from the earlier collective. On the right, an unnamed institutional composite with no logos and no identifiable people.

Different actors. Different responsibility. Different stakes.

The comparison architecture; the anti-equivalence caption is mandatory and opens the page.

The book has done a version of this before, on page 054, and the notes for this page are unusually firm that it must not repeat it.

Page 054 owns the rhyme itself — that more capability creates more options, that the cost of stopping is local and visible, that locally defensible continuation can create collective danger. Those propositions belong there, adjacent to documented agent action, and they stay there.

What this page owns is different and narrower: the shape of an assurance. A guarantee each side wants and neither can issue.

The caption comes first rather than last. Both scenes are compressed or invented, and the banner says so in every gutter.
