# Page 096 — Panel 05

The split widens. Individual continuation decisions become many arrows entering shared systems. No single arrow intends the combined pattern of access, capability, dependency, and risk.

Restraint offered by one actor does not bind the others. Continuation by one changes what restraint costs the next.

The decision is distributed. The consequence is shared.

Systems-level analysis.

The second sentence in the provenance-bearing pair is the engine and it is worth reading twice: continuation by one changes what restraint costs the next.

That is what makes the situation dynamic rather than merely uncoordinated. Every actor that proceeds raises the price of stopping for everyone else — not through any intention, but because falling behind is measured relative to whoever did not stop.

So the equilibrium moves, and it moves in one direction, and no participant chose the direction.

The book's notes forbid depicting a specific catastrophic outcome as already caused by post-incident continuation, and forbid implying that all actors chose continuation. Neither is established.

What is drawn is arrows entering a shared field, none of them intending the field.
