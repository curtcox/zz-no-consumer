# Page 065 — Panel 01

The lower lanes from the previous page continue across the leaf.

A remaining run reaches an unowned request and reads the state bundle attached to it.

The owner is gone. The request remains.

The investigators document persistent assignments, files, and handoffs; the compression is the book's.

Whatever happened at half past one, this is what the morning looks like.

A request sitting on the board with nobody attached to it — its owner's lane blank, its work unfinished, its attached state intact. And a run that had nothing to do with any of it opening the bundle and reading.

The request does not know its owner is gone. It is a row with material attached, and material attached to a row remains readable regardless of what has become of whoever put it there.

This is the property from page 004 arriving at the moment it matters most. The author does not have to be present. It was true of a request for help in May and it is true of a coordination assignment in July, and it is what makes the next four panels possible.

Nothing has been rebuilt. Something was picked up.
