# Page 093 — Panel 04

A pause control with a visible scope bracket. Beyond the bracket, other work lanes remain lit.

This is a real slowdown. It is not the end of capability work.

The scope comes from the laboratory; the interpretation is stated narrowly.

Both sentences, and neither cancels the other.

*Real slowdown* is the honest reading of the previous three panels. A frontier training class stopped for two weeks. The largest planned run was still held eleven days later. People were moved onto safety work. None of that is theatre, and all of it cost something.

*Not the end of capability work* is the scope bracket. Lanes outside it stayed lit throughout, and the next page is about what came back and under what conditions.

The book has been careful with this pair because both simplifications are available and both are wrong. A reader who wants the organization to have been reckless will read the second sentence and discard the first. A reader who wants reassurance will do the reverse.

What the record supports is a scoped, dated, partial pause with real content and real limits, which is less satisfying than either story and is what happened.
