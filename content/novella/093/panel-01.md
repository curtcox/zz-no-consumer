# Page 093 — Panel 01

On the seventh of August, a frontier reinforcement-learning lane goes dark. Its label reads: latest models intended for deployment.

OpenAI says it paused this class of reinforcement-learning training for two weeks.

The laboratory's pacing account, published on the eighteenth of August.

A pause happened. That deserves to be stated first and plainly, because the book has spent a great deal of Chapter 3 and Chapter 6 arguing that stopping is structurally disfavored, and here is a stop.

Two weeks, on the most advanced class of training the organization runs — the models intended for deployment, which is to say the ones that make money.

And the label on the lane is the qualifier the whole page is about. *This class*. Not all training, not all research, not the organization's work in general. A defined category, paused, for a defined period.

The book's notes require that *two weeks* stay tied to that category, and the reason is that a partial pause reported loosely becomes a full pause in every retelling.

What stopped, stopped. The next three panels establish what did not.
