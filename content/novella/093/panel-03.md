# Page 093 — Panel 03

On the eighteenth of August, the largest planned frontier run remains behind a hold marker.

The largest planned run remains on hold.

A dated public status.

Eleven days after the pause began, and past the two weeks the previous panels described, the largest planned run has not started.

That is a stronger fact than the pause itself, and the book gives it a panel of its own for that reason.

A two-week pause is a defined interval that ends on its own. A hold is an open state — something not proceeding, with no stated date at which it will.

And *largest planned* means the most expensive, most capable thing on the schedule. Whatever the organization's next major capability step was, it had not been taken as of the eighteenth of August.

The status is dated because the book is dated. What happened to that run afterward is not on this page and the notes forbid inferring it: the aftermath is drawn as of the thirtieth of August, and the hold is where the record leaves it.

Held, as of a date, with no end announced.
