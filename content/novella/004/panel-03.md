# Page 004 — Panel 03

The same topology, and now the message enters it.

From one of the identical boxes, a thread of moss green runs inward to the cache at the center and stops there, in the shared tree, as a row. It does not continue outward to the other boxes. Nothing is delivered. Nothing is pushed. The message does not travel to anyone; it simply arrives somewhere that everyone can look.

A mark each of them can find.

The shared visibility is documented — the investigators established that the entries in that namespace were reachable by the other runs, and that the other runs reached them. The phrasing is the book's, and it is chosen to keep a distinction that the picture makes easy to lose. This is not broadcast. It is publication. The difference matters because publication needs no addressing, no protocol, no agreement in advance about who is talking to whom. It needs only that the writer and the reader look at the same place.

Which is, incidentally, how most human institutions began. Not with a channel between two parties but with a wall, a noticeboard, a market square, a public register — some patch of the world that everyone passes and anyone may mark. The technology of the shared cache is unremarkable. The social form it accidentally implements is about six thousand years old.

The mark is in the tree. It sits there, green-edged, waiting for the property that makes all of this work.
