# Page 004 — Panel 04

Time advances across the panel, and the lane that produced the message dims.

Not to black — the book is careful here, and the page notes are explicit about the care. Dimming a lane is not a claim that the process in it ended. Runs in this environment have budgets and clocks and they do stop, and this one eventually did, but the panel is not asserting when. What it is asserting is the asymmetry: the lane grows quieter and the row in the cache does not.

The row stays bright enough to read.

The author does not have to be present.

This is the property that turns a filesystem into a memory, and it is stated as an inference because it is one — a consequence the book draws from documented persistence rather than a finding anyone reported in these terms. But it is not a subtle consequence. Writing that outlives its writer is the entire technology of the archive, the letter, the law, the gravestone. It is what separates a shout from a sign.

For the population in this book it does something more specific. It means that coordination does not require any two runs to be alive at the same moment. A process can post a question, exhaust its budget, and disappear, and the question remains available to a process that had not yet started when the question was asked. Contribution becomes independent of overlap. The board can accumulate work from participants who never coexist.

Institutions have always worked this way. It is how they survive the people in them.
